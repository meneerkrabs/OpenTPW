## Advisor producer map: every one of the 351 messages traced to its scan or poster (October 17, 2026)

Step 1 of the advisor campaign is a map, not gameplay. `docs/reverse/ADVISOR-SCAN.md` and `content/data/advisor-producers.toml` give, for every descriptor of tp.exe's table `0x76E300`, its producer, the park state it reads, the score formula in words, its captions (`TAG_SYSTEM.str` entries; the texts are game data and stay out), its OpenTPW status and an implementation batch. Read in tp.exe via the capstone helpers (no original run); the `Advisor.sam` object layout was recovered from the schema `0x72E124` (every key four bytes from `+0xA0`, checked against the seven known getter offsets).

- **The scan** (`0x59C680`, from the advisor update `0x59A550`) scores **one descriptor per update**: 155 descriptors are scanned, 134 have a computed callback over 85 park-statistics helpers, 21 return the constant 0 (`0x5D60C0`) and can never pass `MinScoreForConsideration`. The other 196 are posted by game code (67 events), by windows and tools (45 help messages) or by nothing (84, of which 37 are help descriptors that no call names).
- **87 messages produce advice today** (45 by game code, 42 help), not 91: `AutomaticAdvisor.GlobalTicketMovedMessage` posts 234-237, but `AdvisorTables` has no definition of them, so `AdvisorController.Advise` returns null and nothing is said. 66, 68 and 69 are defined without a poster. `AdvisorProducerInventoryTests` pins both.
- **Dead lines**: the "queues are too long" messages 58 and 59 are scored by the zero stub and posted by nothing in v2.0. The price lines are 238-242 (shop value, speech only), 91/92 (ticket price); the money warnings 102-105 are scan rows reading the months in the red (the bank's 30-day months), 106 is game event 2.
- **81 versus 82**: 82 descriptors share the help score callback `0x59E8C0`; 81 are in `advisor-help-messages.toml` because 291 names the absent response 551. Both numbers are right; the comments now say so, and a test pins it.
- **Unreferenced captions**: `TAG_SYSTEM.str` holds the written form of the price, wealth, patrol and congratulation lines (96-113, 121-122, 24-32, 160-179) that no response row references; the inventory lists them as inferred `orphans`.
- Status of the 351: 87 wired, 146 ready (every input exists in OpenTPW), 13 blocked (staff coverage cells, prank counters, queue links, ride fear, arrival wave, three help windows), 105 nothing to produce. Weather has no advisor message.
