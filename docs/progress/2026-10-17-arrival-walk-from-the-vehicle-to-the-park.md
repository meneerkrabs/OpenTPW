## The arrival walk from the vehicle to the park is tp.exe's (October 17, 2026)

Arriving guests no longer walk a lane of five cells off the path grid. They are made where tp.exe makes them and walk the cell map through its states 0 to 5 (WALK-008 and GUEST-012 retired, ARRIVAL-001, WALK-006 and ARRIVAL-003 narrowed). Read in tp.exe via REA:

- **Creation** (`0x4CF720`, `0x4FAEC0`, `0x4FB201`): a guest is made at the centre of the bus stop B cell (two rows up for the seaplane and the Ferry), takes the entrance side (R & 1) and the sideways byte, and asks for the walk to the bus-side crossing cell (`0x4D8710`: side A's or B's X with side A's row; the y byte is 0xC8). A route makes state 0, none state 6. The person constructor stores the purpose speed 25 (`0x4FB1C1`), which the needs phase and the booth walk overwrite.
- **State 0 to 2** (`0x501A0F`, `0x4FF520`, `0x4FF730`): the walk to the crossing cell ends in state 1, which waits for the vehicle (`0x51A760`) and then draws the booth side and the two bytes of the point and asks for the route; state 2 sets the speed term and walks to the booth, ending in state 4 with the park open and in state 3 with it closed.
- **State 3 and 5** (`0x4FF7F0`, `0x4FFB20`, `0x501E6E`): a paid guest waits in state 3 with a drawn wait of 200 to 349 turns; it walks on to the entrance cell of a drawn side only as the head of its cell's thing list. A closed park or a shut gate counts the wait down and sends the guest home (mask 1, a drawn gate crossing cell, state 18 with the exit counter 0, so it is never recalled); an unpaid guest goes back to its booth. State 5 walks in, turns back to a booth when the park closes, and numbers the visitor (`0x51AAF0`) on arrival.
- **Visitor numbers**: `Guest.VisitorNumber` (`+0x1D8`) and the park's `VisitorCount` (`+0x1DA714`) are state now, saved, imported from an original save (`mArrivalIndex`, `mNumberOfVisitorsToDate`) and shown by the visitor windows ("New Visitor" until the entrance) instead of the thing id.
- **The way home** is the original's too: a booth refusal or a closed park's state 3 walks to the gate's crossing cell by the original search, and a stop cell no route reaches leaves the walk stuck in state 20 as `0x5019F0` does; the off-grid lane fallback is gone. `IsOnParkCell` is the original's cell test (`0x4FA990`: types 0, 1, 3, 9 and 10), so the thought pick and the balloon percentages no longer count a guest on a walkway cell.
- **Narrowed**: ARRIVAL-001 keeps the cell thing lists (WALK-003), the update order (DET-013) and the unrestored script state of an imported vehicle; WALK-006 keeps only the last walk into the ride's entrance cell (a trial of the original walk for it showed that guests whose queue is edited away under them would stay stuck where the route walk finishes); ARRIVAL-003's coaster and kart parts stay (the coaster test `0x441970` is mapped: self-intersection count `+0x140`, complete and closed, not the coaster being edited, `0x790FEC`). DET-014 loses the arrival clauses.

**Pinned digests move** (docs/DETERMINISM.md): the fixed synthetic run `0x5F177F986FADADB1` → `0x3DA16AED71E2F1E4`, the cross grid of `PathBuilderTests` 61 admissions `0x397F3EC43F6F70C8` → `0x7E11BFA00FE093FD`; the Jungle guest run `0x30F213E46933EB8C` (21 admissions) → `0xBC02D81A5FC6621D` (20).

**Saves**: guest snapshot schema 9 (optional `Paid`, `VisitorNumber` and the park's `VisitorCount`; state 3 is a new guest state; the gate wait `BoothWait` may now hold up to 349). A save of schema 8 or older may hold a guest on an arrival lane: it is restored on the cell map in the state its lane position stood for (the walk to the crossing, the wait, the walk to the booth, the booth, the walk to the entrance, the way home with the leaving mask, or waiting at the stop), with no draw. A guest of schema 9 that carries a lane is refused. An original save imports its guests in states 0 to 5, 18, 19 and 21 on the map in their saved state (state 3 with its paid word and wait). Verified with the jungle original-level smoke and the park-resume smokes: guests arrive, walk to the booth, pay and enter.

Still open: the cell thing lists (WALK-003), the update order and the thing sweep (DET-013), the state-13 walk into the ride's entrance cell (WALK-006).
