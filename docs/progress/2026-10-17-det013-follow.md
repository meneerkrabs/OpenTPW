## DET-013 records the skipped-slice object follow (October 17, 2026)

- **What changed.** Text only; behaviour is unchanged. The review of the substep scheduler found a second
  `(script id xor pass counter) & 7` test at `0x551A1F`: a script whose slice is skipped still follows its attached object in a
  branch of its own, on the pass its id selects. OpenTPW runs that follow on every tick. DET-013 and `docs/DETERMINISM.md` now say so, and
  that the effects update `0x475360` sits inside the original's even-substep block, where OpenTPW runs it per 60 Hz tick.
