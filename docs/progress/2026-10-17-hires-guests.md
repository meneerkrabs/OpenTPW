## Higher-resolution guest sprites for the pools extension (October 17, 2026)

Our own guests, built in Blender, are now drawn at 2x the pixel size and keep their world size. Engine and content:

- **Pixels per unit honoured.** The two u16 words of a sprite frame (A and B, 128 in every shipped frame; `SpriteFrame.UnknownA/UnknownB` are now `PixelsPerUnitX/Y`) scale a quad by 128 / A across and 128 / B down, as the original's placement `0x5890E0` spans `originX / A` to `(width + originX) / A`. `GuestAtlasFrame.Extents` does it for the world sprites (`UiSpriteFigure` scales separately); `GuestRenderer` (kids, costumes, swimmers), `RideHeadRenderer`, `BalloonRenderer`, `ThoughtRenderer`, `StaffRenderer`, the hire figure (`UiSpriteFigure`) and `GuestWorldPicker` use it. With the shipped data the factor is exactly 1.0, so every quad is bit-identical (`SpritePixelScaleTests`, 5000 random frames; `GuestAssetTests` over every shipped atlas): no capture, save or hash pin can move (sprites are presentation only).
- **Atlas.** `GuestSpriteAtlas` keeps 1024 columns up to 2048 rows and doubles the width (to 4096) beyond that; every shipped atlas stays 1024 wide, the 2x kid and head sets are 1024 x 256 to 512, the swimwear sets 1024 x 2048 (90 MiB RGBA, about 120 MiB on the GPU; the decoded pixels are released after the upload), frames at more than 128 pixels per unit get 4 pixels of padding, and a set that fits no 4096 x 4096 atlas is skipped with a warning.
- **Art.** `opentpw_guest_sprites.py --scale 2` (canvas, hotspots and ppu 2x, supersample 4, `pixelsPerUnit` 256), all 255-colour palettes, no dither needed (mean quantisation error 2.9 of 255). New `--part pool`: the swimwear table of 14 slots (`PoolSwimSlots`: stand, walk, ladder, dive, lying, shower, slide, crawl, breaststroke, tread, splash-down, spa sitting, ring floating, rope swing) from `opentpw_pool_poses.py`. Compare and sheet tool `opentpw_guest_hires_compare.py`. The kit's `sprites` command takes `pixelsPerUnit`.
- **Shipping.** `content/pools/esprites/Swim` (the swimwear sets, 6.56 MB), `Generic/Kids` (1.23 MB: only slots 0, 1 and 14, the ones the land renderers draw) and `Generic/Kidsheads` (1.05 MB) at 2x, `.TPC` only: 8.84 MB of sprites, 9.06 MB with the objects. `PoolGuestSprites` makes the kid and head sets replace the game's together, at level setup, only in a Theme Park World park and only while the new presentation-only extension `opentpw.pools-guest-art` (on by default, translated on the Extensions and mods screen, no simulation flag) is on; the swim sets are decoded on a worker at level setup. `tools/pools/build-content.sh` rebuilds it (about 30 minutes).
- **Browser.** The sprite sets are not embedded in the web build (`content/pools/esprites` is excluded; a test parses the csproj); the browser keeps the game's land guests and the kid-sprite swimmers. On-demand delivery like the Clarity pack is a follow-up.

Not verified: costumes, parents and staff keep the original art; the TPI sets render but are not shipped (a TPI park keeps its own guests, its swimmers use the TPW swim sets); the new slots are not drawn by the swim session yet; the web build was not run.
