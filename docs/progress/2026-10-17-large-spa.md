## A large spa pool for four (October 17, 2026)

A second spa object next to the one-seat spa ([POOLS.md](../POOLS.md), "Spa pool").

- **The object**: "Large Spa Pool" (Info.Ids 65216 to 65219, one per theme, 2 x 2 cells, price 1800, names in the nine languages). Placement, the access cell and the erase guard are the small spa's rules: it needs pool water on any side. Made by `tools/blender/opentpw_pool_attractions.py` (`bigspa`) and `tools/pools/build-content.sh`.
- **Four seats**: the basin fills the footprint, and the seat rule is the spa fix's (seat distance plus the seated sprite's half width below the rim stays inside the basin): four seats on a circle of 0.48 cell, one per cell, 0.68 cell apart. A fifth would not fit without crowding.
- **Unchanged**: the one-seat spa, its model and all pinned determinism runs (none of them places a large spa).
- Tests: `PoolsSpaGeometryTests` (both spas, every theme), `PoolsAttractionsTests` (four guests sit at once, one per cell), `PoolsContentTests` and `PoolsAttractionsContentTests` (the new objects, ids and counts).
