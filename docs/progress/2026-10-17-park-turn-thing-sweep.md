## The park turn runs as the original's world update (October 17, 2026)

A park turn no longer updates guests, staff, objects, the weather and the challenge system in OpenTPW's own order. The tick that starts a turn runs tp.exe's order (main loop `0x54F4BF`, world update `0x516380`; DET-013 narrowed). Read in tp.exe via REA:

- **The sweep.** The substep's ride scripts and bump module come first (`0x546C80`, `0x5516B0`), then the turn counter advances and the used list of the thing pool is walked from its head, the thing built last (`0x7CF56C`, each slot's older link read after the update, `0x516413..0x516431`). Each thing is dispatched by its type (`0x50B360`): a guest (`0x501650`, `0x5019F0`), an attraction (the wear and breakdown update `0x4E0B90`, then the ride update and its admission `0x4E0E00`), the five staff types, the weather (`0x512880`) and the challenge system (`0x4D1E90`); the analyser, the HQs, the bank and the lab have no update. A thing that has asked for its destruction (byte `+3`, set by `0x50B780`) is skipped. A lost park (world state 4) skips the walk.
- **After the walk**: the gate retry on every 30th turn (`0x51643B`), the turn's deferred destroys (`0x516641`), then `0x4D7B20`: the arrival update (`0x4CF3E0`), the staff pool, the calendar (`0x4F8260`) and every 100th turn the golden-ticket step. A guest that leaves now asks for its destruction at once, and the queue is freed only at a turn's end, not at the end of every 60 Hz tick, so a slot a demolition frees is taken again in the original's order.
- **Research** is the researcher's own update: first in its thing update (`0x502960`), on every 20th turn and unless its state is 3, 4 or 5, not an economy-wide pass after the staff.
- **Clocks.** The guests' tick count started at 0 where an economy that begins at an original save's turn (the jungle's 755) starts at that turn's tick, so the two cut the ticks into turns a tick apart in two of five turns and one turn could not be swept as a whole. The guests' turn boundaries are now put on the economy's (`GuestSimulation.AlignTurns`); their `ParkTurn` still counts from their own start. Nothing changes for a park whose clocks start together.
- **Code**: `IThingSweep` (the economy calls it where it ran its own thing update), `Level.RunThingSweep`, `GuestSimulation.SweepGuest`, `IStaffWorld.UpdateStaffMember`, `ParkEconomy.UpdateRide`, `ResearchBy`, `UpdateStaffVitals(member)` and `CountDownJob`, `RideVisitorBridge.UpdateAdmission`. The headless economy without a host keeps its own order (`EXT:headless-economy`); a lost park, a paused economy and a park without guests in the pool update as before.

**Pinned digest moves** (docs/DETERMINISM.md): the fixed synthetic run `0x3DA16AED71E2F1E4` → `0x0960BD53946D24D5`. Cause: the fixture runs Level's order and the economy advances one tick for each guest tick (it ran four, at the old Fastest speed), over 14,400 ticks so the run still crosses a month end; the lockstep clocks alone give `0xD13B0DC628488A9F`, the sweep moves that to the new pin. No other pinned digest moves (the cross grid and the Jungle guest runs drive the guests alone).

**Saves**: no field changes. A save from before loads and continues; the guests' turn alignment is recomputed from the two tick counts.

Still open (DET-013): the substep around the turn. OpenTPW runs every script, the bump module and the tour cars on each 60 Hz tick; the original's RSSE pass (`0x5516B0`) gives a script one slice per eight substeps of 31 ms (its id xor the pass counter, low three bits clear, or every pass for a script whose flag byte `+0xB8` is set) and runs the tour cars only on even substeps, so the draws they take between two turns and every ride's slice cadence differ. Porting it moves every ride's timing; it needs its own step. ECON-001's catch-up cap stays `EXT:fixed-catch-up-cap`.
