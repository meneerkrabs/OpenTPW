## Pool models keep a gap inside their footprint (October 17, 2026)

- Walkers passing the changing room were cut by its walls: the cabin and the slide towers reached the edge of their footprint, while a walker is a camera-facing sprite at the depth of its feet that the steering lets come to 2 units from a path cell's edge.
- Measured on the game's own models (cross-section 2 units above the ground against the footprint): all 32 shops and 17 sideshows stand at least 0.5 to 0.9 units inside on every side, with a median of 2.3 (sideshows) to 4.4 (shops) over all sides and a per-building tightest side of 1.5 to 3.5. The original's ground slabs fill the footprint, its walls do not.
- The changing room's entrance wall is now 1.6 units inside (1.1 at the door frame) and its flanks 1.6, with a 1.0 inset roof and a low apron slab over the whole footprint; the pool side stays 0.5 inside. The slide towers' flight and posts stand 1.5 inside on the entrance side and the flanks; the platform and the pool side are unchanged. The pool extras and water attractions already kept a gap.
- `tools/blender/opentpw_changing_room.py` and `opentpw_slide_tower.py` were changed and the 12 archives regenerated (only the `.MD2` models differ; textures, sprites and the sprite manifest are byte-identical). `PoolsContentTests` pin the gap instead of the old "fills the 2 x 2 cells" and a corpus test pins the original measurement.
- Not checked in a native capture: how the cabin and towers look with the gap and whether a tall staff sprite still touches a wall when walking at the very edge of the path cell.
