## Pools P2: changing rooms and swimming guests (October 17, 2026)

Phase P2 of the `opentpw.pools` extension ([POOLS.md](../POOLS.md)): guests choose a changing room like an attraction, change, swim through the connected pool cells with whole-body swim sprites, climb out, change back and walk on.

- **Changing room object** `[EXT:pools-changing-room]`: a 2 x 2 cabin per theme (Info.Ids 65500 to 65503, range 65500 to 65519 reserved), built with the content kit (Blender script, `GltfModelConverter`, our own SAM and RSE), shipped as a catalog root from `content/pools` (about 0.9 MB, generated, regenerate with `tools/pools/build-content.sh`; embedded in the browser build). Placement rule: a pool cell must touch its pool-side cell (`NeedsPool`). The pool tool refuses to erase a cell under a swimmer or on a room's back.
- **Swim session** `[EXT:pools-swim]`: new states `Swimming` 22, `ClimbingOut` 23, `ChangingOut` 24 (changing in is the existing hidden `Using`). Integer 16.16 sub-cell positions, 64 headings, a per-guest xorshift stream seeded by one world draw; 40 to 70 s swim, 12 turns changing back, happiness +12. Handled: catchable list, statistics, leaving (finishes the swim first), park close, demolition, pool erased, staff, balloons, snapshot (optional fields, older saves load). No `IGuestDecisions` change: the room is scored by `ScoreAttraction`.
- **Rendering**: swimmers use their own swim sprite sets (8 TPW kid sets, `content/pools/esprites/Swim`) drawn after the water, which no longer writes depth; guests changing are hidden.
- **Saves and determinism**: nothing changes with the extension off or with pools and no room; new pin `PoolsChangingRoomFixedRunHash = 0xB5B033AE20AD9BA0` (91 swims, 24 at once; it moved with the review fixes below); the asset manifest holds the extension's files only for the objects the park places, so older pool saves keep loading when the extension gains objects (found with the native smoke: a park with the extension on and no pools failed to save).
- **Review fixes**: the rooms are never research items (`"research": false` in an `objects.json`, `EconomyObjectInfo.NotResearched`; research tables, saved research and the golden ticket are the same with the extension on or off); a demolished room's guests start no swim (`IRideVisitorBridge.IsStopped`); swimmers count in the census; a pool takes 2 swimmers per water cell, at least 4; the validator bounds the swim turns per state and checks the room; swim sprites are looked for once per level (the kid sprite is the fallback); `--debug-*` numbers parse with the invariant culture.
- **Tools**: `--debug-swimmers N`, `--debug-pool-offset dx,dy`, `OPENTPW_SMOKE_POOLS=1` resume smoke.

Not verified: tp.exe's acceptance of authored files, the browser build (resource embedded, not run), the art against any original capture. The room's price is not charged by the economy. `ContentKitTests.AGuestEntersTheBuiltPoolSwimsAndLeaves` fails on main since PR #324 independent of this change.
