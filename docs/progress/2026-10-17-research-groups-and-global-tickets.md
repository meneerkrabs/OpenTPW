## Research group thresholds confirmed and global tickets move between parks (October 17, 2026)

- **Research group opener index (verdict: OpenTPW was right).** The group opener `0x503B80` reads its threshold at `0x785540 + 4h`, h the category's highest open group, and stops at h > 6.
  The settings object `0x785040` is laid out by its builder `0x401030` over the 60-byte key descriptors at `0x73FC70`: scalar keys take consecutive slots from 1 at object + 8 + 4 x slot, and an array of members repeats its block count times. Descriptor 165, `PercentageForThisTech`, sits in a one-member array of 10 at slot 317, so `ResearchTech[0]` is at `0x78553C` and the opener's `0x785540 + 4h` is `ResearchTech[h + 1]`, which is the index `ParkResearch` reads (the Mac's balance +1276 for entry 0 is the same distance from the object). Nothing else reads `0x78553C`. A `[BIN:TPW-EXE:0x00503B80 ...]` tag now states the mapping; for the jungle data (0, 0, 80, 85, 85) group 1 therefore opens at 0 % and group 2 at 80 %, and no code changed.
- **Global ticket moved between parks.** `0x5AF940` keeps a global ticket with one park: the first win sets the player-wide flag and the park's held byte and record, and a park that does not hold it takes it from a holder with a lower record (result 4). The only player-visible effects are the advisor line (message 234 + type, called from `0x4D5270` with a debug log line "Global ticket award moved here") and that the other park is no longer the holder; the player-wide count (`0x5AF4A0`) never changes. The ledger now keeps the holder and its record per global (`GlobalHolders`, optional and validated, older files load with unknown holders), the owner attaches it to the park, a second park does not earn the same global again, a better record moves it (`ParkEventKind.GlobalTicketMoved`) and the advisor posts message 234 to 237. Without a ledger the park keeps its own set. The advisor's lines for a first award are still not posted: the caller `0x4D4E50` passes the whole result of `0x5AF940` to `0x4D5270`, whose cases 1, 2 and 3 say messages 220, 221 and 222 (case 4 is the moved line). Result 1 is a first award that gains no key; 2 and 3 need the player's key total to rise (the earned count a multiple of 3 once the extra keys are added) and 3 additionally needs that total to equal the key cost (the theme settings' `Keys.CostToEnter`) of one of the four themes. OpenTPW posts no advice for any ticket award, local ones included, and needs the four theme costs at run time, so this stays open.
- **HUD ticket count.** The HUD counter handler `0x4A0FB0` shows `0x5AF5D0`, the player-wide earned count (the four global flags once, the six local bytes of every park, the two secret flags) less the player's spent count, so a moved global ticket never changes it. OpenTPW shows the current park's own available tickets; this stays the registered difference UI-051, whose text now names the player-wide count, because the spent count cannot be shared until park purchases are routed into the ledger.
