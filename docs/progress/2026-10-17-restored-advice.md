## Restored advice: the official extension that says what tp.exe never plays (October 17, 2026)

`opentpw.restored-advice` 1.0.0 gives a trigger to **56 of the 105 advisor messages** whose speech is in the data but that tp.exe v2.0 never posts (score callback the constant-zero stub, or no poster). It is **on by default** and `AffectsSimulation` is false: it only reads park state and posts advice through the advisor's own queue, so no save is locked to it (`ParkResumeMods` records nothing) and every pinned digest is unchanged. Details in [reverse/ADVISOR-SCAN.md](../reverse/ADVISOR-SCAN.md) "Restored advice", [MODS.md](../MODS.md) and [LIPS.md](../LIPS.md).

- **36 with existing triggers**: queues (58, 59), upgrades (65, 130-132), toilets (83, 84), golden tickets by the key they earn (99, 164, 165, 218, 219, 223, 224, 228, 229) and awards (230-233), the profit ticket (277, 318), challenges with a follow-up (319, 320), arrivals by bus, seaplane or Ferry (137-139), record-sized attractions (151-154), souvenirs (160), little land (173) and low balances (347, 348).
- **5 with new read-only tracking**: security cover of the path cells from guard patrol areas and cameras (24, 25), a ride exit with no path (265), and guests aboard a broken-down or condemned ride (349, 350). The original's per-cell coverage counter (`+0x3C`) is a per-role patrol-rectangle flag with no cameras in it; the restored cover joins it with the camera ticket's security level.
- **8 tutorial leftovers** (training budget and upgrade windows, blueprint and roller coaster tool hints, pop-up help off, camcorder, first message, first challenge) and **7 online lines** on OpenTPW's own online screens (publish, park details, other parks, find parks, outbox, world, news).
- **Mechanism**: `AdvisorScan` asks `RestoredAdviceScan.CallbackFor` for a row scored by the stub (the hook answers only while the extension is on), `RestoredAdviceDriver` polls the rows the scan skips and answers events, the HUD posts hints through `HudHost.RestoredHelp`. Scores are the message's own `Advisor.sam` key where its descriptor names one, else a documented `[EXT:restored-advice-<topic>]` constant.
- **Skipped on purpose**: silent stubs and the `z_error` placeholder, duplicates of lines already said (161, 321, 180-183), and lines with no input to trigger from (90, 155, 67, 168, 266, 296, 288, 338-343, 330, 336, 142, the lobby tutorial).
- **Evidence**: `AdvisorRestoredAdviceTests` fires every trigger through the real queue, checks that off is silent and that `AffectsSimulation` is false, round-trips the advisor's snapshot with the extension toggled both ways, compares the world state hash with the extension on and off, and reads the shipped `Advisor.sam` for every key and group; `advisor-producers.toml` marks the 56 rows with a `restored` topic without touching their original status.
