## MTR: no reader in any allowed binary, scale and M7 explained as far as the data goes (October 17, 2026)

The MTR sidecars of the three banner meshes still have no reader. This pass searched every allowed binary (decrypted tp.exe, the other PC executables,
QMixer.dll, usp10.dll, the online resource DLLs, the no-CD Theme Park Inc Game.exe and the Mac PEF containers) for `.mtr`, the magic and the
banner names, and tp.exe names none of `bankrupt`, `congrats`, `paused` or their `s_` mesh names, so no code that opens or places the banners
was found either. Against the paired MD2s the scale `s` turned out to be already baked into the vertices (the slab thickness is exactly 30 times
`s`), while `M7` is not derived from the shipped MD2 and looks like a stale pivot. `docs/MTR.md` and the backlog row state what is proven and what is
not; a private test pins the depth and X-centre relations. No reader code changed.
