## MD2 in the running game: scene clock, terrain texture turn and mirror, culling (October 17, 2026)

- **Terrain texture turn and mirror.** The ground quads now lay their texture as tp.exe's terrain renderer does: the cell word's bit 0x40 mirrors the texture and bits 0x8 / 0x10 / 0x20 turn it by one, two or three corner steps (`0x56F4F0`, called from the renderer `0x56F670`, which applies the same corner permutation in both of its vertex orders). `TerrainCellTexture` holds the permutation. Native captures of the jungle before and after show the same terrain with only the grass texture's direction changed.
- **Scene clock at game speed.** The original's scaled clock (`0x7B496C`, timer `0x785970`) gains the real gap times the rate (0.25..2 by the numpad keys `0x40C3B0`/`0x40C3C0`/`0x40C3D0`) and the pause (`0x4092A0`/`0x409300`) freezes it together with the unscaled clock. OpenTPW's `SceneClock` plays it for the two scene animations that still ran on real time: the sky's scroll (`0x585F10`, frame time `0x57FF60`, capped at 500 ms) and the coasters' scrolling textures (`0x435ADF`, whole milliseconds on the scaled clock through `0x4709E0`). They now run at the park's speed and stand still while it is paused. The advisor stays on the unscaled clock; the weather and particles already followed the speed.
- **Per-mesh culling.** Node bit 0x10 hiding a mesh was already drawn. The view test `0x561A30` clips the mesh record's box (widened by half its extent per side) against the view; OpenTPW culls each mesh part with its exact vertex bounds, and all 4,909 static meshes of the corpus have every vertex inside their stored box, so nothing the original draws is culled here. No determinism change.
