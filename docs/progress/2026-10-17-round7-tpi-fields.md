## Theme Park Inc park-save field meanings (October 17, 2026)

The fields of the Theme Park Inc park save now have meanings read from the no-CD `Game.exe`: the loaders and the code that uses each field (statically, nothing was run). Details: [THEME-PARK-INC.md](../THEME-PARK-INC.md#field-meanings-the-attraction-history-the-control-record-the-calendar-the-special-rides).

- **Attraction history (SAVE-940, narrowed)**: the 19 rings are 13 daily (30 entries, new-day message 11) and 6 monthly (144 entries, new-month message 12), sent by the calendar step `0x6083d0` and handled by the attraction at `0x5df19f` and `0x5defff`. `ThemeParkIncAttractionRing` names each by what feeds it (takings, costs, customers, walk-aways, four mood grades, declined, need checks, cheap and expensive price, and the monthly takings, costs, total costs, running cost, profit and customers), `HistoryRings` gives the capacity, period and in-memory offset (the object is 0x14c8 bytes), and `NewestFirst` reads a ring back as the game does (`0x5eb090`). The three gauge words are an unread gauge, the life gauge and the state of repair. The ring names are our descriptions; three rings stand for several conditions.
- **Control record (SAVE-941, retired)**: the added word at +0x20 counts placements (`0x667cb0`) and numbers the copies; the others are the price, the built count (+0x18), the tick of the first build (+0x1c, -1 until built) and the research level. The real prebuilt shows built counts of 1 and 2, ticks 33 and 47 and placement counts of 1 and 2 for the objects the player placed. The two bytes after the 16-bit id are raw memory and no longer checked.
- **Calendar and arrivals (SAVE-942, retired)**: the serializer's field log gives the calendar order; the arrivals are typed (`ThemeParkIncArrivals`: rate, time signature, target vehicle capacity, people on the bus, offloading, gates open).
- **Special rides (SAVE-943, retired)**: the loader reads the count into the global and loops by it, so the saved count is the loop count. The table holds four records before its own count word, so a larger count is refused (it was bounded at 1024); a record is typed as 20 slots of thing id, ability and type (unused slots are type 7).
- **Bodies (SAVE-944, narrowed)**: the guest's seven `pv` words are 0..100 float meters (happiness, thirst, hunger, toilet, illness, litter and one unnamed), a staff member's three are float meters whose names are not assigned, and the weather's wind and last lightning location are floats. The remaining words keep their stored width.
- **Tests**: `ThemeParkIncAttractionHistoryTests`, `ThemeParkIncWorldFieldTests` and additions to `ThemeParkIncThingBodyTests`.
