## Smarter guests, phase 4: queue patience (October 17, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.2.0 to 1.3.0 with the rules of phase 4 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-4-queue-patience)): (1) a guest does not join a ride whose expected wait (the queue ahead only: guests still walking there will stand behind it) is above `90 x patience` turns when another ride it could actually choose next (open, with room, admitted, not in its used or temporary history, not of the type it rode last) has a wait of at most 80 percent of it; the refusal is the original's `QueueTooLong` (thought 16, queue-limit statistics, the ride in the temporary history); (2) a guest not at the front gives up after `120 x patience` turns from `QueueJoinTurn` under the same alternative condition (thought 16, the original dismissal's happiness step through the host's existing path, `QueueEndReason.Impatient`). Patience is a stateless 70 to 130 percent trait; the base is the measured baseline (the original's boarded waits have p90 73 and p99 108 turns), not `BoredomThreshold`. Only rides are concerned; the existing leave tests, a guest being called forward and an interlude are untouched. Off, nothing runs and every pin is unchanged. The extension is still not player-selectable.
- **Honest result.** The phase 4 wait targets are **not met**. After an independent review's fixes the rules act rarely (about two refusals a seed, no give-up in 8 extended seeds), and `park-scenario` is the same as 1.2.0 within the noise: censored p90 93 and p99 116 turns (1.2.0: 96 and 117; default: 73 and 108), happiness, revenue and use Gini unchanged. No setting of 12 tried (join 10 to 90 turns, give-up 50 to 120, alternative share 60 to 150 percent) met the targets without refusals that cost guest time. The four level parks are bit-identical to 1.2.0 (one ride: no alternative). The first build looked better (p90 43, p99 81) only because it counted walkers behind the guest as ahead of it and used every ride as an alternative; it refused guests whose wait was fine, 34 percent of its refusals were refused again, and guests rode less (104.2 guest seconds per ride against 91.2). Rule 2 never fired in the measured seeds; it is kept as insurance for parks with a badly slow ride and a test constructs that case.
- **New guarded metric.** `use.guestSecondsPerRide` (the window's guest time over the boardings of rides, the pooled "time between a guest's boardings"; down is good) is in the report and `--guest-compare` as a regression guard, so a wait gain bought with refusals shows up. Phase 4's target row now names it as guarded.
- **State and saves.** None: the patience is a trait and the rules read host facts. The phase 3 resume test covers the extension at 1.3.0, and a 1.2.0 park is refused with the usual message.
- **Pins.** Off-path and script-mod pins unchanged. `SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash` unchanged (0xC8534726E08D0EC2) and `ScenarioSmarterFixedRunHash` unchanged (0xC0344925E23A32C1: the scenario's 12,000-tick run never refuses or dismisses a guest). Explained in DETERMINISM.md.
- **Interface (additive, for sessions building on `IGuestDecisions`).** Default-implemented `AlternativeLookAfterTurns( context, guest )` (-1: not asked; `ScriptGuestDecisions` forwards it); optional trailing `BestAlternativeWait256` (default -1) on `AdmissionView` and `QueuePatienceView`; `QueuePatienceView.Position` is the current place after a move-up; `GuestSimulation.BestAlternativeWait256` (pure, scanned only for a guest not at the front from its own give-up turn); additive `ParkScenarioOptions.TwinAHoldMilliseconds` and `SingleRide` (defaults unchanged). No change to `GuestState`, the state switch, `Guest` or the snapshot. A policy that ignores the members behaves exactly as before.
