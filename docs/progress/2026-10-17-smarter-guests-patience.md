## Smarter guests, phase 4: queue patience (October 17, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.2.0 to 1.3.0 with the rules of phase 4 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-4-queue-patience)): (1) a guest does not join a ride whose expected wait from the back (phase 3's estimate) is above `90 x patience` turns when another ride it could join has a wait of at most 80 percent of it (a refusal is the original's `QueueTooLong`: thought 16, queue-limit statistics, the ride in the temporary history); (2) a guest not at the front gives up after `120 x patience` turns from `QueueJoinTurn` under the same alternative condition (thought 16, the original dismissal's happiness step through the host's existing path, `QueueEndReason.Impatient`). Patience is a stateless 70 to 130 percent trait; the base is the measured baseline (the original's boarded waits have p90 73 and p99 108 turns), not `BoredomThreshold`. Only rides are concerned; the existing leave tests, a guest being called forward and an interlude are untouched. Off, nothing runs and every pin is unchanged. The extension is still not player-selectable.
- **State and saves.** None: the patience is a trait and the rules read host facts. No new section member, so no new save validation; the phase 3 resume test covers the extension at 1.3.0, and a 1.2.0 park is refused with the usual message.
- **Measured** (extended scale, 8 seeds, 3 SE): on `park-scenario` the censored p90 falls from 96 (1.2.0) to 43 turns and the p99 from 117 to 81, below the default's 73 and 108 (-7.8 and -5.5 SE against the default); happiness (+11.7 SE), revenue per admitted guest (+4.0), price rejections (-26.6), the use Gini (-5.1) and stuck guests (0.4) keep phase 2 and 3's gains. The price: the boarded share falls from 0.755 to 0.583 and the rejected share rises to 0.399 against 1.2.0 (refused guests try again, so attempts rise 387 to 457; boardings fall 9%, 292 to 266, and happiness and revenue do not move). **The four level parks are bit-identical to 1.2.0**: one ride means no alternative, so no rule acts. Without the alternative condition the rules won the scenario and cost the level parks up to 49 SE of boarding and 8 to 24 SE of happiness (a give-up alone, 12 to 25 SE), so they were rejected.
- **Pins.** Off-path and script-mod pins unchanged, `SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash` unchanged (0xC8534726E08D0EC2: one ride in the synthetic park). `ScenarioSmarterFixedRunHash` 0xC0344925E23A32C1 → 0x83140C77CD575BE1: the rules act on the scenario's four rides. Explained in DETERMINISM.md.
- **Interface (additive, for sessions building on `IGuestDecisions`).** Default-implemented `AlternativeLookAfterTurns` (-1: not asked; `ScriptGuestDecisions` forwards it); optional trailing `BestAlternativeWait256` (default -1) on `AdmissionView` and `QueuePatienceView`; `GuestSimulation.BestAlternativeWait256` (pure) and two small call-site edits in `GuestSimulation.cs` and `GuestSimulation.Decisions.cs`, gated on the policy asking. No change to `GuestState`, the state switch, `Guest` or the snapshot. A policy that ignores the member behaves exactly as before.
