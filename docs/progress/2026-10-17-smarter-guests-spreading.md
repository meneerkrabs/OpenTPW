## Smarter guests, phase 3: spreading (October 17, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.1.0 to 1.2.0 with the rules of phase 3 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-3-spreading)): (1) the queue term of the score is the expected wait for every attraction, at any distance, replacing the original's near-only queue match: `(guests in the queue + guests walking there) x observed time per guest`, from a persisted moving-average throughput estimate per attraction instance (`RideThroughputs`: gaps between boardings while the attraction had demand; cold start from the live capacity and a default cycle of 60 turns for rides and 12 for services; closure, breakdown and an empty queue start a new gap; an upgrade restarts; the interval is clamped to 1 to 1,200 turns; a stalled ride is read as its silence), never taking a candidate the original would have chosen below the choice floor; (2) a stable 94 to 106 percent preference per guest and attraction instance; (3) hysteresis between rides in the minor decision (20 percent of the target's score, at least 2 points; shops, toilets and sideshows stay impulse stops, which the measurement required). Off, nothing runs and every pin is unchanged. The extension is still not player-selectable.
- **State and saves.** `Throughputs` joins the `SmarterGuests` section (attraction-id order, omitted while empty), is validated strictly (attractions the save holds, ascending ids, capacity, interval and sample limits, boarding turn not after the park's turn, demand only after a boarding) and hashes only while non-empty. The resume continuation test now includes it. A 1.1.0 park is refused with the usual message.
- **Review fixes.** (M1) The stuck-guest explanation was wrong: an independent log of 64 stuck guests showed none had all four rides in its history (44 had two, 18 one, 2 none) and in 62 of 64 every attraction, unused rides included, scored below the choice floor from distance and excitement alone. A ride the guest has not used lately and the admission gate would let it through to is now kept at the floor, which brings the stuck guests back to the default's level. (M2) Demand follows the queue state at every park turn start: an open, working attraction with a queue gets an entry and a silence clock, so a repaired ride whose head never boards and a ride that never boards anybody are read as stalled. (M3) The estimate keys on the live capacity (`LiveCapacity`, the script's `VAR_CAPACITY`), so ride-settings changes restart it. (L4) Candidates kept at the floor rank as `10 + score / 5`. (L5) Same-turn boardings are gaps of 0 (the minimum interval is now a quarter turn). (L6) The walkers per attraction are counted once per decision. (L7) Comments fixed.
- **Measured** (extended scale, 8 seeds, 3 SE, against 1.1.0 and the default): on `park-scenario` the median wait falls from 17.0 to 13.5 turns (-4.1 SE against 1.1.0; the default's is 11.6, +2.0 SE, "same"), the censored p90 from 101 to 96 (-1.9 SE, "same"), departures per admission and the use Gini improve, **the stuck guests fall from 5.25 to 1.25 (-4.1 SE, equal to the default)**, and phase 2's gains hold (price rejections 0.024, happiness +12.1 SE and revenue per admitted guest +5.2 SE against the default). **Still failing there:** the censored p90 and p99 against the default (96 and 117 against 73 and 108): the park keeps its guests (41 against 33) and the four one-guest rides carry them at the queue limit; that is for the patience rule (phase 4). The preference and the hysteresis are invisible in the scenario (its twin rides were already used evenly); the hysteresis acts a handful of times per 40 days. **The level parks changed** (they were bit-identical under phase 2; now no seed ends in the default's state hash) but only space moves beyond 3 SE: median wait 33.0 to 35.3 (regressed), p90 432 to 387 and revenue per admitted guest +8.9 SE (improved), use Gini over all attractions 0.285 to 0.302; Halloween's happiness of the guests still inside +3.2 SE. Hysteresis on every kind of attraction was measured and rejected: it cost Halloween 14 SE of revenue per admitted guest.
- **Pins.** Off-path and script-mod pins unchanged. `SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash` 0xCB2F2ECD287ED83F → 0xC8534726E08D0EC2: the synthetic park's guests are still the off run's (equal `ComputeStateHash`); only the drinks shop's throughput estimate joins the world hash (the review fixes added the silence clock to it, hence the second value). `ScenarioSmarterFixedRunHash` 0x5680EC44DBB01974 → 0xC0344925E23A32C1 (the guest state hash of a 12,000-tick run; the review fixes did not change it): the rules act on the scenario's rides. Both explained in DETERMINISM.md.
- **Interface (additive, for sessions building on `IGuestDecisions` and on the guest simulation).** Default-implemented members `UsesSpreading` and `SwitchesTarget( context, guest, target, targetScore, candidate, candidateScore )`; optional trailing `AttractionView.Reservations` and `TurnsPerGuest256`, and `AttractionScoreInput.QueueMatchOverride`; `ScriptGuestDecisions` forwards both members. In `GuestSimulation` the host edits are small and gated on `decisions.UsesSpreading`: one call in `OnVisitorBoarded`, one in `BeginTick`, one in `Unregister`, the hysteresis in `MinorDecision`, and the rest in `GuestSimulation.Decisions.cs`; no change to `GuestState`, the state switch or the guest snapshot. A policy that ignores the members behaves exactly as before.
