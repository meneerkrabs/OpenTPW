## Smarter guests, phase 3: spreading (October 17, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.1.0 to 1.2.0 with the rules of phase 3 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-3-spreading)): (1) the queue term of the score is the expected wait for every attraction, at any distance, replacing the original's near-only queue match: `(guests in the queue + guests walking there) x observed time per guest`, from a persisted moving-average throughput estimate per attraction instance (`RideThroughputs`: gaps between boardings while the attraction had demand; cold start from the catalogue capacity and a default cycle of 60 turns for rides and 12 for services; closure, breakdown and an empty queue start a new gap; an upgrade restarts; the interval is clamped to 1 to 1,200 turns; a stalled ride is read as its silence), never taking a candidate the original would have chosen below the choice floor; (2) a stable 94 to 106 percent preference per guest and attraction instance; (3) hysteresis between rides in the minor decision (20 percent of the target's score, at least 2 points; shops, toilets and sideshows stay impulse stops, which the measurement required). Off, nothing runs and every pin is unchanged. The extension is still not player-selectable.
- **State and saves.** `Throughputs` joins the `SmarterGuests` section (attraction-id order, omitted while empty), is validated strictly (attractions the save holds, ascending ids, capacity, interval and sample limits, boarding turn not after the park's turn, demand only after a boarding) and hashes only while non-empty. The resume continuation test now includes it. A 1.1.0 park is refused with the usual message.
- **Measured** (extended scale, 8 seeds, 3 SE, against 1.1.0 and the default): on `park-scenario` the median wait falls from 17.0 to 12.9 turns (-4.7 SE against 1.1.0; the default's is 11.6, now "same"), the censored p90 from 101 to 92.5 (-3.0 SE), departures per admission and the use Gini improve, and phase 2's gains hold (price rejections 0.024, happiness +11.9 SE and revenue per admitted guest +5.7 SE against the default). **Still failing there:** the censored p90 and p99 against the default (92.5 and 115 against 73 and 108) and the stuck guests (5.6 against 1.25; unchanged from 1.1.0): the stuck are guests that have used all four rides lately, so every attraction scores below the choice floor; that needs a rule for the guest done with the park (phase 5), not more spreading. The preference and the hysteresis are invisible in the scenario (its twin rides were already used evenly); the hysteresis acts a handful of times per 40 days. **The level parks changed** (they were bit-identical under phase 2; now no seed ends in the default's state hash) but only space moves beyond 3 SE: median wait 33.0 to 35.3 (regressed), p90 432 to 396 and revenue per admitted guest +8.2 SE (improved), use Gini over all attractions 0.285 to 0.300. Hysteresis on every kind of attraction was measured and rejected: it cost Halloween 14 SE of revenue per admitted guest.
- **Pins.** Off-path and script-mod pins unchanged. `SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash` 0xCB2F2ECD287ED83F → 0x6D65CF08DB99F3C5: the synthetic park's guests are still the off run's (equal `ComputeStateHash`); only the drinks shop's throughput estimate joins the world hash. `ScenarioSmarterFixedRunHash` 0x5680EC44DBB01974 → 0xC0344925E23A32C1: the rules now act on the scenario's rides. Both explained in DETERMINISM.md.
- **Interface (additive, for sessions building on `IGuestDecisions` and on the guest simulation).** Default-implemented members `UsesSpreading` and `SwitchesTarget( context, guest, target, targetScore, candidate, candidateScore )`; optional trailing `AttractionView.Reservations` and `TurnsPerGuest256`, and `AttractionScoreInput.QueueMatchOverride`; `ScriptGuestDecisions` forwards both members. In `GuestSimulation` the host edits are small and gated on `decisions.UsesSpreading`: one call in `OnVisitorBoarded`, one in `BeginTick`, one in `Unregister`, the hysteresis in `MinorDecision`, and the rest in `GuestSimulation.Decisions.cs`; no change to `GuestState`, the state switch or the guest snapshot. A policy that ignores the members behaves exactly as before.
