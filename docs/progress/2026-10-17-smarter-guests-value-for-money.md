## Smarter guests: value for money, the first real rules (October 17, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.0.0 (the seam) to 1.1.0 with the rules of phase 2 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-2-value-for-money)): the host adds price and willingness to the shop and sideshow candidate views and a guest no longer walks to a stand it expects to refuse (the stand-point check stays the host's and records its statistic once); a guest whose choice this rule empties goes home after K = 1 such decisions with thought 6, the original's "too expensive" thought (counted per birth id in `SmarterGuestsState.Stalls`, saved as `Stalls` in the `SmarterGuests` section, hashed while non-empty, validated strictly); the booth scales the rating part of the park value by 100 to 115 percent by the open rides and sideshows (never harsher than the original); a per-guest price sensitivity trait (90 to 110 percent) scales the willingness. Off, nothing runs and every pin is unchanged. The extension is still not player-selectable (it opens once phases 2 to 6 are done).
- **Why the guest goes home.** An independent review found that rule 1 alone makes "can't decide" outcomes 19.5 times as frequent (59 to 1,150 over 8 seeds), because the guest the original sends to the dear shop (and loses two happiness steps there until it leaves) now wanders and tries again every ~30 turns; that loop, not a longer healthy stay, was the stuck-guest regression. K was tuned by measurement over 1, 2, 3 and 5 (K = 1 is best on stuck guests, waits and park size). A first "too expensive" memory of refused stands was removed: it never acted once rule 1 predicts refusals with the stand's own function.
- **Measured** (extended scale, 8 seeds, 3 SE): on `park-scenario` price rejections fall 761 to 65 (-26.7 SE), guest-time happiness 49.0 to 62.8 (+11.8 SE), revenue per admitted guest 82.1 to 94.2 (+2.3 SE, not significant), admissions unchanged, happiness-0 departures -25.5 SE. Still failing there: queue wait p50/p90 (11.6 to 17.0 and 73 to 101 turns; the dear shop no longer absorbs 28% of the attempts and the four one-guest rides carry them, every variant tried lands on 101 to 108) and the stuck-guest count (1.25 to 5.25, +4.5 SE). The four level parks are bit-identical to the default (they have no price rejections). The booth is validated by unit tests and a scenario variant with a park rating, not by the report.
- **Pins.** Off-path pins unchanged. `SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash` did not move (the synthetic park never refuses a guest on price, so the rules never act there); the new `ScenarioSmarterFixedRunHash` (0x5680EC44DBB01974) pins the scenario with the extension on. The script-mod pins run over the original policy and are unchanged. The report gained `queue.rejected.price.rate` for this phase's target.
- **Interface (additive, for sessions building on `IGuestDecisions`).** Default-implemented members: `UsesValueForMoney`, `PriceTurnedAway( context, guest, attraction )` and `DecidePriceStall( context, guest, consecutive )` returning the new `PriceStallDecision( GoHome, Thought )`; `AttractionView` gained `Price` and `Willingness`, `BoothView` gained `OpenAttractions` (optional trailing parameters, 0 unless the policy asks); `ScriptGuestDecisions` forwards the three members. A policy that ignores them behaves exactly as before.
