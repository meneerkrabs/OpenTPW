## Smarter guests: value for money, the first real rules (October 17, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.0.0 (the seam) to 1.1.0 with the rules of phase 2 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-2-value-for-money)): a guest no longer walks to a shop or sideshow it expects the stand to turn away on price (the host adds price, willingness and a remembered-refusal flag to the candidate views; the stand-point check stays the host's and records its statistic once), a refused guest remembers that stand for 46 park turns (`PriceMemory`, bounded at 4 per guest, saved in the `SmarterGuests` section as `Refusals`, hashed while non-empty, validated strictly), the booth weighs the fee against a park value scaled by 85 to 115 percent by the open attractions, and a per-guest price sensitivity trait (90 to 110 percent) scales the willingness. Off, nothing runs and every pin is unchanged. The extension is still not player-selectable (it opens once phases 2 to 6 are done).
- **Measured** (extended scale, 8 seeds, 3 SE): on `park-scenario` price rejections fall 761 to 50 (-27.7 SE), guest-time happiness 49.0 to 59.9 (+9.0 SE), revenue per admitted guest 82.1 to 99.9 (+4.0 SE), admissions unchanged. The four level parks are bit-identical to the default (they have no price rejections). Queue waits (p90 73 to 103 turns) and the stuck-guest count (1.25 to 19.75) regress in the scenario because guests now stay in the park longer and queue at the rides; that is for the spreading phase, and nothing was tuned to hide it.
- **Pins.** Off-path pins unchanged. `SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash` did not move (the synthetic park never refuses a guest on price, so the rules never act there); the new `ScenarioSmarterFixedRunHash` pins the scenario with the extension on. The script-mod pins run over the original policy and are unchanged. The report gained `queue.rejected.price.rate` for this phase's target.
