## Spa pool guests sit in the water (October 17, 2026)

A guest in a spa no longer sits half through the tub's wall ([POOLS.md](../POOLS.md), "Spa pool").

- **Cause**: measured from the shipped models, the seated swimwear sprite is as wide as the whole old basin (0.73 engine units each side of its hotspot below the rim, basin 0.68 to 0.74 across the flat sides), so any of the eight seats on the 0.27 cell circle put the body through the wall. The old seated height of 0.35 engine units also put the guest's water line far below the tub's water (4.3 model units).
- **Fix** (`[EXT:pools-attractions]` only): the tub's basin is wider (inner circumradius 4.0 model units, 3.70 at the flat sides) with the water just below the rim (5.2 units, 1.04 engine units), the bench and dividers are gone, and the spa has one seat, in the middle, with the torso at the water surface (`PoolAttractionSettings.SpaSeats` 1, `SpaSeatRadius` 0, `SpaWaterHeight`). All four themes' `spa.MD2` were rebuilt with `tools/blender/opentpw_pool_attractions.py`; textures and scripts are unchanged.
- **Capacity**: a spa now holds one guest at a time (it was eight). Nothing of the original game changes.
- **Determinism**: no new random draw; `PoolsAttractionsFixedRunHash` moved on purpose because a taken spa declines the next swimmer (DETERMINISM.md, "Pools").
- Tests: `PoolsSpaGeometryTests` (new: for every theme, the seat plus the sprite's half width stays inside the measured basin, the seated height is the measured water surface, the tub is centred), `PoolsAttractionsTests` (one seat).
