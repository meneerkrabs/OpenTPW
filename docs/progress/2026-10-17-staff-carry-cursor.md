## A hired or carried staff member hangs at the cursor again (October 17, 2026)

Hiring a worker or picking one up showed only the glove cursor: the person was invisible. The tools' dangling sprite read slot 6 of the staff bank, and that slot is a 2 x 2 pixel placeholder in seven of the eight shipped staff banks (only the Jungle explorer bank, SPR_EX, has real art there, and it is not a dangle), so the quad was "drawn" every frame (the old smoke test only asked whether a draw call happened) but covered four pixels. Reading tp.exe, the tool's sprite is script 6, 5, 4, 7 or 8 (by role) of the sprite set at 0x74ed98 (the tool's enter, 0x46c730), not the bank slot, and its per-frame update (0x46c480) draws it at the exact world point under the mouse (0x7a1c90) 5 units above the ground, whatever the pointer is over. The set's art is not decoded yet (`GUEST-021`), so the person's standing frame facing the player hangs there instead.
The sprite now follows the exact ground point under the pointer every frame (`StaffPlacement.Cursor`, `Level.TryGetGroundCell`), also while the pointer is over the HUD and between cell centres; before it snapped to the hovered cell's centre and vanished over the interface. The hire window already closes with the hire (0x49b9dd) and the pick-up closes the employee window; a unit test pins that no screen stays on the stack. The money and cancel rules are unchanged (Escape or right click still leaves the candidate in the pool, and a carried member goes back to its cell).
Tests: the sprite position follows the pointer frame by frame, and the hire leaves no window on the stack. The native smoke now moves the pointer over three nearby points for both tools and requires the sprite at the ground point under each one (it differs from every cell centre) with no hire or employee window open; with `OPENTPW_STAFF_CAPTURE=1` it saves each probe frame.
