## Staff and guests turn with the camera the way the original does (October 17, 2026)

Staff looked more side-on than in Theme Park World when the camera was turned. tp.exe picks a sprite's direction from the heading in 45 degree sectors plus the camera's yaw sector, and never from the camera's pitch: the banks store one elevation (5 directions per slot, the rest mirrored), the same for staff and guests (`0x518F90`, `0x4FA030`, `0x542010`). The yaw sector is `trunc((yaw - pi / 8) / 2 pi x 8)`, which truncates toward zero, so every yaw from 0 to 67.5 degrees draws the sprites as the Home pose does (backs, fronts and profiles along the paths) and above that they turn one sector behind the exact angle (`0x540AB0`, `0x46F1C0`). OpenTPW used the exact angle, so a camera turned by 45 degrees showed three-quarter views where the original shows the back. The kid, costume and staff renderers now use the original's rule; the Home camera is unchanged, the swimmers' renderer keeps the exact angle for its lying poses.
Presentation only: nothing here touches the world generator, so no pin moves. `--debug-staff` (Debug builds) hires and places one of each role for captures; `--debug-staff-at x,y` picks the cell.
