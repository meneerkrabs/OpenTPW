## Staff and guests pick their view direction the way the original does (October 17, 2026)

tp.exe picks a sprite's direction from the heading in 45 degree sectors plus the camera's yaw in 45 degree sectors, and never from the camera's pitch: the banks store one elevation (5 directions per slot, the rest mirrored), the same for staff and guests (`0x518F90`, `0x4FA030`, `0x542010`). The yaw sector is `8 - (trunc((yaw + pi / 8) / 2 pi x 8) & 7)` with the park camera's yaw wrapped to 0 to 2 pi (`0x540AB0`, `0x46F1C0`), so heading and yaw are quantised separately. OpenTPW snapped the exact camera-relative angle once; the kid, costume and staff renderers now use the original's rule, the Home camera is unchanged, and the swimmers' renderer keeps the exact angle for its lying poses.
Presentation only: nothing here touches the world generator, so no pin moves. `--debug-staff` (Debug builds) hires and places one of each role for captures; `--debug-staff-at x,y` picks the cell.
