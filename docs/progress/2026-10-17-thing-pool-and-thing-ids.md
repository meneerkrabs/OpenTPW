## One pool of thing ids, as in tp.exe (October 17, 2026)

Every thing of the park now takes its id from tp.exe's one pool of 10239 slots, so a guest's id is the original's thing id and the rules that read it see the original's numbers. Read in tp.exe via REA:

- **The pool** (`ThingPool`, WALK-010 narrowed): the allocator `0x516270` takes the head of a free list that starts ascending (`0x515F30`, slot 1 first, last id `0x27FF`) and makes the new thing the newest of the used list; the free `0x516200` puts the slot at the head of the free list, so the next thing takes the slot freed last; a destroy request (`0x50B780` → `0x516310`) goes to the head of a queue that the park turn's end empties from the head (`0x516641`, so a turn's requests are freed last first); a load keeps each saved thing in its own slot and rebuilds the free list ascending (`0x518D43`). Twelve constructors reach it through the base constructor `0x50AFE0`.
- **The order of a new park** (`0x5156A0`): the staff HQ, mechanic HQ, advisor controller, tag system, park analyser, research lab, weather, bank, UI message receiver and challenge system are things 1 to 10, then Gates (11) and Lights (12). The jungle level's shipped save lists exactly that (Gates Info.Id 1601 is thing 11, Lights 1603 thing 12, the bus 1600 thing 15), and a test reads it from the game data. The first guest is thing 13. The guest spawner `0x516700` the world's online code uses (25 guests and staff) is not the offline arrival; staff things are made at the placing click (`0x46C8E0`), vehicles when first sent (`0x51A2F0`).
- **Who takes slots** (all through the pool): guests (`GuestSimulation.Create`, id before the first draw), placed objects, fixed items and arrival vehicles (`ParkObjects`), hired staff at the placing click (`ParkStaff.Hire`), freed at the turn's end (`Level.AdvanceSimulation`; a standalone guest simulation owns a pool and ends the turn itself). An original save puts its whole chain back slot by slot and frees the slots of the things the import does not rebuild.
- **The rules that read the id**: the needs phase (`0x501676`), the reseed when a guest is made (`0x4FB19E`, DET-014 narrowed), the prank flag (`0x4FB114`), the advisor's prank line, the queue edit's anger (`0x501496`, id modulo 3) and two new ones. The dismissal (`0x5012F0`) plays the guest sound `cat_kids` 0x80 for a thing id with its low three bits 0 (`0x50133D`; `GuestSimulation.Voiced`, presentation only, played at the guest by `GameAudio`; no draw from a generator). The booth walk (state 2, `0x4FF730`) sets the speed term on every park turn: 25 for a thing id with its low two bits 0, 50 while the bus waits at status 3, otherwise 0. The staff's pre-update phase (`0x505490`) reads the member's thing id.
- **Retired**: GUEST-003 (the price test, statistics, walk-aways and the refusal's dismissal sound are now the original's; the event records 6 and 10 are the debug-only `[EXT:guest-event-ring]`) and QUEUE-005 (the leave-for-room rule, the re-route `0x501160` with its failure, anger by thing id, the dismissal's sound). **Narrowed**: WALK-010 (the pool holds only what OpenTPW builds, the destroy requests of a turn come in OpenTPW's update order, the guests' histories hold the script world's attraction ids, OpenTPW's own save keeps the free list's exact order) and DET-014 (the thing-id clause is gone; the entrance cell a creation takes is read now: `0x4D8710` takes `FixedItemInfo.CrossingBSSideAPosX` or `BPosX` with `APosY`, the words `0x7855DC`, `0x7855E4`, `0x7855E0`, the lane's second cell, which the arrival walk will use).

**Pinned digests move** (docs/DETERMINISM.md): the fixed synthetic run `0x01946A39A53F851E` → `0x5F177F986FADADB1` (the pool alone gave `0xA7A559BCC863F8E2`; the booth term moved it again), the Jungle guest run `0xF55B1DF82DFF7F3F` → `0x30F213E46933EB8C` and the cross grid of `PathBuilderTests` 60 admissions `0xD2F05F91D6567215` → 61 admissions `0x397F3EC43F6F70C8`.
