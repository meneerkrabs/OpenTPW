## Theme Park Inc thing bodies traced from their load routines (October 17, 2026)

The Theme Park Inc park save no longer takes any thing width from the one witnessed save or from a search for the next header. The staff HQ, advisor controller, park analyser, research lab, weather and challenge system (formerly "closure derived": the prebuilt's constants 411, 233, 76,617, 8,414, 104 and 1,287, checked by the following header) and the guests, staff and gardeners (formerly bounded by the first following header that reads) are now sized by layouts traced from their load routines in the no-CD `Game.exe`: the thing dispatch of the World loader (`0x64c771`, jump table `0x64d7e0`), then each class's load (guest `0x60d260`, staff base `0x6267c0` with the role loads, `0x6326b0`, `0x7177f0`, `0x5ace30`, `0x61bce0`, `0x641510`, `0x5c7c70`). Details: [THEME-PARK-INC.md](../THEME-PARK-INC.md#thing-bodies).

- **Layouts** (`ThemeParkIncBodyLayout`): fields, arrays, embedded records and history rings with the serializer's own member names. A body's width is its fixed part plus `mNumEntries` entries for each ring (the staff have six rings of at most 30 entries, so 684 to 1,416 bytes; the analyser 63,177 to 76,639; the guest is 619). The version check of every routine accepts 0 and 1 and nothing but the ring counts depends on stream data. A saved count above a ring's capacity is refused (the original would overrun the ring's own counters).
- **Verified three ways**: reading the routines, running each of them in an x86 emulator on streams of chosen content (the width and the offset and size of every read match the layouts for random streams and for ring counts at 0 and at capacity), and reproducing the prebuilt (the six formerly closure-derived widths come out of the layouts, and the analyser's 1,024 cells fall on a 32 x 32 grid of 4-square cells). The mechanic, handyman, entertainer, guard, researcher and gardener bodies have no witnessed save; that remains SAVE-944.
- **Typed**: `ThemeParkIncStaffHq`, `ThemeParkIncAdvisorController`, `ThemeParkIncParkAnalyser`, `ThemeParkIncResearchLab`, `ThemeParkIncWeather`, `ThemeParkIncChallengeSystem` (words keep their stored width, 12-byte vectors stay raw, 8-byte times follow the calendar) and `ThemeParkIncPerson` for guests, staff and gardeners, whose every member is reachable by path and offset through `ThemeParkIncBody`. `ThemeParkIncWidthSource` has one member, `Reader`; `ClosureDerived`, `MarkerSearch` and `MarkerBounded` are gone.
- **Tests**: `ThemeParkIncThingBodyTests` (synthetic bodies from the layouts, emulator-derived widths for chosen ring counts, offsets of the named members, damage and capacity refusals, a header lookalike planted in every traced body, guests/staff/gardeners spliced into the prebuilt's thing chain, and the prebuilt's typed values).
- **Register**: SAVE-944 is narrowed to the unwitnessed guest, staff and gardener layouts and the untyped words.
