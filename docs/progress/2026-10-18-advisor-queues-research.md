## The advisor's price, shop value and research callbacks (October 18, 2026)

- **31 more scan callbacks are ported from `tp.exe`.** `AdvisorQueuesCallbacks` (`World/AdvisorScanQueues.cs`) scores the researcher lines (15-17), the research-more lines (61, 72, 76, 80), the two upgrade lines (63, 64), the ticket price lines (91, 92) and the 20
  shop value and satisfaction lines (238-257). The callbacks read the park through `IAdvisorParkState` and the new `IAdvisorQueuesState`, which `EconomyParkState` (now a partial class) implements from the economy without writing to it
  (`0x4C9830`, `0x4C9920`, `0x4C9E40`-`0x4C9ED0`, `0x4CA4C0`, `0x4CA630`-`0x4CA690`, `0x503980` and the shop scans `0x4CAAE0`-`0x4CC090`). The advisor itself stays outside the world hash, but the booth's perception ring 0 (`PriceStatistics`), which `GuestSimulation.PayAdmission` now fills, is economy state and enters the hash: with game data, a park with admissions hashes differently than before (no pin moves, no-data parks record nothing).
- **Reading the bytes corrected the first mapping.** The shop figures count attractions older than 30 days (`0x4DD670` is the age in days), not shops with more than 30 customers; the "value" is the walk-away share of the customers over the 30-day rings. Every
  shop callback scores (figure - threshold) x `ScorePerPoint` with the figure first, so the ten "poor" lines are negative for the shipped positive multiplier and never pass (ported as they are). 247 calls the sideshow value helper where its four siblings call a satisfaction helper.
  15 needs no researcher and the other research lines need one, 61, 72, 76 and 80 test no park-open state, 80 is the only research-more line without a research rate test, and 17 also needs a researcher waiting in the hiring pool.
- **The ticket price lines needed a producer of their ring.** 91 and 92 read perception ring 0, which only the booth's verdict `0x4FF5B0` writes (the `PeepInfo.OpinionFor*` bytes of its four bands, nothing for the early fair exit). `BoothVerdict.Opinion` and `GuestSettings` carry them
  (read only when the settings file has the keys) and `GuestSimulation.PayAdmission` records them through the park's statistics; the price statistics were already saved with a park.
- **Inventory.** The 31 rows (22 queues and prices, 9 research) are `wired` in `content/data/advisor-producers.toml` with the formulas corrected, and ADVISOR-SCAN.md and LIPS.md describe them. Not set: the handle and figure each shop callback keeps in the object's `+4` and `+8`, which `0x59B590` copies into the queued advice; OpenTPW has no reader for it and the lines are speech only.
- **Tests.** `AdvisorScanQueuesTests`: each callback below, at and above its threshold over hand-built states, a whole scan pass through the real queue, the statistics over a real `ParkEconomy` (the shop figures, the research counts and the upgraded ride types), the booth's opinion bytes and a with-data check of the shipped thresholds.
