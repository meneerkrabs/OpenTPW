## The README and the website say how much the advisor says (October 18, 2026)

The README and the website said the advisor speaks "for the game events traced so far, such as a new park, Instant Action
and bankruptcy", which undersold it: OpenTPW posts 133 of the 246 messages tp.exe v2.0 gives (the welcome, money trouble
and bankruptcy, rides and breakdowns, litter, guest mood, staff, research, golden tickets and the help of the windows and
tools; `content/data/advisor-producers.toml`), all with their original speech and lip sync, and the restored-advice
extension adds 55 of the 105 messages the original never posts. Both texts now give those numbers. The other 113 are
ported in batches (100 have every input they need, 13 wait for one).
