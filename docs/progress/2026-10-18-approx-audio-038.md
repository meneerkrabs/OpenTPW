## AUDIO-038: out-of-range loops get a fresh output (October 18, 2026)

Added the [AUDIO-038 proposal](../proposals/audio-038-trace-out-of-range-voice-resumption.md) on October 18; its bounded part is implemented in the same PR (#553), see [the October 20 entry](2026-10-20-audio038-loop-range.md): a catalogue loop that leaves the hearing range keeps its owner and is rebuilt with a fresh output when it is admitted again, as tp.exe's range stop `0x6BCA70` and owner callback `0x6BBE40` do. AUDIO-038 is narrowed, not retired: finite one-shot restart and expiry and the sentence refill timing remain open.
