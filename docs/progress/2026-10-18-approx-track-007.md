## TRACK-007: Directional track demolition (October 18, 2026)

Added the [TRACK-007 proposal](../proposals/track-007-replay-directional-track-demolition.md) on October 18 and implemented its bounded part in #593 (merged October 20 with exact-head CI green): a two-line full crossing whose laid flow matches the replayed chain keeps its other flow during a ride's demolition. Thirteen synthetic cases cover both collectors, kart and water crossings, saved rotation, callbacks, add-ons and the unchanged contrary-flow policy. The cleared bit matches tp.exe: its line step `0x53B280` clears the link bit opposite to the step, and the replay `0x52FE50` runs each line from the later corner point back to the earlier one, so the bit is the chain's own heading. TRACK-007 stays open for the other junction shapes and the per-line arm writes.
