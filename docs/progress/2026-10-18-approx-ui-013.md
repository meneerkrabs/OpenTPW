## UI-013: Online pause layout state (October 18, 2026)

Added the [UI-013 proposal](../proposals/ui-013-scope-online-pause-layout-state.md) on October 18 and implemented its bounded part in #470 (merged October 20 with exact-head CI green): the park pause menu lays its lines out from top 0 with no gap while the player is logged in, as tp.exe does while its net-session field `+0x60` is set (`0x48c36c`, `0x5b6450`), and from top 10 with a gap of 5 otherwise. The field is set only by the login reply "Authorization OK" (`0x5b6363`), so OpenTPW maps it to the authenticated session the park HUD samples when the menu opens (`HudHost.IsLoggedIn`). See [AUTHENTICATED-PAUSE-LAYOUT](../AUTHENTICATED-PAUSE-LAYOUT.md). UI-013 stays open for the menu font tier.
