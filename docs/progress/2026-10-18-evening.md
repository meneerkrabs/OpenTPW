## Evening and night: a day cycle and lamps that light at dusk, as an official extension (October 18, 2026)

`opentpw.evening` 1.0.0 gives the park a day and night cycle. It is **presentation only** (`AffectsSimulation` is false, so no save records it and no hash, pin or simulation value changes) and **on by default** (the user decided after the tuning captures; it can be switched off); the
extension's name and description are in all eleven interface languages on Options > OpenTPW > Extensions and mods.

- **Cycle**: a clock on **play time** (real frame time while the park runs, standing still while it is paused, whatever the park speed), starting at noon; the phase is not saved, a resumed park starts a new day. The pace is a **Day cycle** row on the OpenTPW options
  page (10, 20 or 40 minutes, or always day, dusk or night; default 20, saved in `opentpw-options.json`, an unknown value reads as the default). Full day is the original's look exactly, down to the bytes of the uniform block.
- **Dark**: the sun colour, the ambient (a new `g_flAmbientLevel` in the padding after the sun position of the lit shaders, 0 = unchanged), the sky, the fog and backdrop, the guest, staff and litter sprites, and the pool water. The GLES shaders are regenerated.
- **Lamps** (`content/data/evening-lights.json`, 21 rows, 34 lights, no model changed): the pools extension's lamp and underwater light, the original lamp posts and gates of the four themes, a few lit features (pumpkin, demon statue, plasmatrope, crystals, fountain,
  lava) and a few ride lights (candles of Hocus Pocus, fire pots of Aztec Mayhem, Eruption, Pumpkin Castle, beams of Escape Velocity and Star Tours), as point lights that fade in with the dusk and follow their model's animation.
- **Evidence**: `EveningTests` (curves, the exact-day look, the clock, the options value and row, every interface language, registration, the lamp table and its resolution on the shipped models and, with data, on every original object it names) and a park run whose world hash is equal with the extension
  on and off (`DeterminismTests`). The look was tuned on offscreen captures of the jungle park and a pool at day, dusk and night (tinted ambient for a warm dusk and a blue night); [EVENING.md](../EVENING.md) ("Tuning", "Not done") lists what is still open. `--evening-pace` is a run-scoped override and is never saved.
- **Also**: two comment fixes from the review of the point lights (the glTF light extra's summary, and the light record type word in LIGHTS.md).
- Review follow-up: the docs, the site's document list and the extension's own comment now say the extension is on by default (MODS.md said off until tuned); particles not tinted at night are listed under Not done.
