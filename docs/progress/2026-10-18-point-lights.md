## Point lights, light dummies and the light opcodes (October 18, 2026)

The original engine has point lights that nothing creates and four RSE light opcodes with nothing behind them; OpenTPW now draws point lights and gives objects the lights those opcodes drive (`docs/LIGHTS.md`, `[EXT:point-lights]` and `[EXT:object-lights]`). It is presentation only: nothing is hashed, every determinism pin and every saved park of the original objects is unchanged, and a park without lights draws as before.

- **Renderer.** `Level.PointLights` gathers the lights once a frame (lights wholly outside the view are dropped) and each draw of a lit material (the ground, objects, coasters, tracks, tours, the pool deck and basin) gets the nearest 16 whose sphere reaches its world box, ordered by distance to the box, then to the camera. The shaders add the original's falloff, `(r2 - d2) x (1 / r2)` times the light colour with no normal term (`0x5741B0`), to the sun and ambient shading. The original's radius of 100 model units is 20 engine units, ten cells. The lights ride in the draw's own uniform upload after the engine block (`g_vPointLight[32]`, the count in the padding after the light colour): a draw with no lights uploads the same 272 bytes as before, one with all 16 uploads 784. `test.shader`, `pool-deck.shader` and `pool-basin.shader` changed; the browser's precompiled GLES versions are regenerated. The pool water, sprites and particles take no light.
- **Light dummies.** A model node attribute of class `0x20000` (OpenTPW writes type `0x20040`) with the light number as its value and a data block holding the base colour and the radius in model units is a light; `ModelLights` reads it, `ModelBuilder.AddLight` writes it, and the glTF converter turns `KHR_lights_punctual` point lights into dummies (colour, `range` as the radius, `opentpw_light` and `opentpw_light_radius` extras; the Blender exporter writes lights now). The object's `.sam` tunes them with `Lights[n].Red`, `.Green`, `.Blue` (percent), `.Radius` and `.On`.
- **Opcodes.** `ENABLELIGHT`, `DISABLELIGHT`, `SETLIGHT` (base colour x level x 0.01) and `COLOURLIGHT` (operands x 0.01) act on the light of the object's dummy number and no longer count as unimplemented effects; `APPROX:RIDES-046` is retired. Naming a light the model does not have is a no-op logged once, deliberately safer than the original, which would fault there. Each placed object's lights follow their dummy node's world transform, so a light on a moving part moves.
- **Saves.** The on/off state and colour of an object's lights are saved with its runtime snapshot (an optional member, written only for objects with lights, validated on load) and restored on resume; they are not part of any state hash.
- **Example mod.** `opentpw.example.lamp-lights` (official, off by default, not in the browser build) adds a lamp post whose script pulses, flickers and colour-cycles its light; `tools/lights/build-example-mod.sh` rebuilds it.
- **Not looked at.** No capture was taken: how the light looks in the park (strength against the sun and ambient, the 16-light limit on the one-mesh ground) is still to be judged on screen.
