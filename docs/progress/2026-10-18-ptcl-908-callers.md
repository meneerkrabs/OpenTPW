caller (`0x48dfbe`, effect `0x5a`, in the region `0x48DE00`-`0x48E0C0` that Ghidra left unanalysed, next to `0x48de1a` with effect
`0x5b`), and `0x4b8ee0` is a third caller of the lobby's key-panel effect `0x61`. The first-draw beam 15 is not served for every
vehicle: `0x549DB0` flags every vehicle it creates (`0x54a009`) and the per-vehicle draw reads the flag at `0x546a89` with no family
check found, while OpenTPW raises it for karts only, so it stays in PTCL-908. The rider beam at the BUMP 12 load is every vehicle
family's (`0x549B80` is shared); OpenTPW places it at the vehicle's simulation position where the original uses the instance's root
translation. The guest teleport beam test now pins one beam at boarding and one at leaving.
