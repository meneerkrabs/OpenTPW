## Guests, fidelity round 8: sideshow prizes, cell lists, booth timing (October 18, 2026)

Package 4 of fidelity round 8 reads the open guest, queue, walk, arrival, tour, head and path rows against tp.exe and builds what the instructions show.

Built: a won sideshow adds its prize (the cost of goods, `0x4E1A10`) to the guest's purse before the outcome and ends with `happiness += trunc(medium step x log2(prize / price))` plus one world draw (`0x4FE231`, `0x4FE802..0x4FE8D9`; a free play or a prize of 0 gives the x87's indefinite integer, so the happiness goes to 0, as `__ftol` does). The per-cell thing lists are persistent and head-inserted (`0x4D91F0`) instead of being rebuilt in guest order every park turn; separation (`0x5102F0`) and the balloon prank walk each list newest first, and each guest saves the entry number that orders its list (the optional `CellSerial`; an older save starts the lists in guest order). The bin search (`0x500DC0`) makes a route request for every candidate closer than the best, so a closer bin that fails its route leaves the walk stuck and the guest drops its litter on the cell, and a route request of a stranded guest refuses at once (`0x4FA530`, `0x4FA770`). The booth's first verdict is the guest's next update after the walk's arrival (`0x4FF730`, `0x4FF9D0`), not a second later. A path laid over a queue cell edits the queue of the ride that owns it (`0x5346D0` at `0x534858`), which retires `WALK-007`. A show or performer turn writes the original's 11-bit heading angle (the table-driven arctangent at `0x6E7074`, table entry `floor(atan(i / 256) x 1024 / pi)`). The balloons are placed in the order of the live thing list (`0x518F90`). A staff member can be put down on a ride's queue-end entrance cell (type 9); the footprint cells are refused by the types the placement writes, not by an object list (`0x46CC40`, `0x528A70`).

Retired: `WALK-007` and `TOUR-008` (no shadow under the tour cars: the batcher `0x581DB0` only sorts parts, the shadow model is skipped by its loader `0x489060`). Narrowed with new evidence: `GUEST-002` (the queue count equals `0x4DDF50` because every listed guest is in a queue state), `GUEST-005`, `GUEST-007`, `GUEST-010`, `GUEST-011` (shops carry the openable flag: `Info.IsChoosable` is 1 in the four `Shops.sam`), `GUEST-013`, `GUEST-014` (the per-role member constructors are called only by the place click), `GUEST-017`, `WALK-003`, `WALK-005` (the test at `0x4D89C3` reads a track record, not an object), `ARRIVAL-006`, `QUEUE-023` and `GATE-006`.

Pins moved (docs/DETERMINISM.md): the fixed run `0x2A8418A90725699A` to `0x5D8385D13CC3BB9B`, and the pool, smarter-guests, script-mod and scenario pins with it. The guest report (`--guest-report --guest-scale extended`) shows no metric beyond three standard errors apart from a better guest-time happiness in the Jungle and a 1.6-turn longer median queue wait in the Space park; the stuck-guest counts are unchanged.

Tests: the sideshow tail and prize, the log of zero and infinity, the bin walk with a failing closer candidate, the stranded route refusal, the list order across turns and saves, the prank victim, the heading angle, the booth's first verdict, the paved queue end and the staff drop on a ride's entrance.
