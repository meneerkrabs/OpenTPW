## Smarter guests, phase 6b: litter bins (October 18, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.5.0 to 1.6.0 with one rule ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-6b-litter-routes-and-bins)): a guest whose litter meter is full looks eight cells around it for a litter bin instead of the original's three (`SmarterBinCarry.SearchRadius`; the host keeps the bin search, the route, the walk, the throw and the drop). The extension stays hidden (`PlayerSelectable` false): the release is the user's decision after these numbers.
- **Measured honestly.** Where a guest fills its litter meter and the bins stand more than three cells from where it litters (the very dirty scenario with four bins on the street, 24 seeds): litter on the paths -27 percent (-6.9 SE; -11.7 SE over 64 seeds), the littered share of guest time -31 percent (-7.5 SE), steps onto litter per visit -34 percent (-6.2 SE), with happiness, revenue, admissions and guests in the park within 1 SE; stuck guests 3.4 to 4.3 (+1.7 SE, +2.3 SE over 64 seeds), the one number to watch. **No effect** in the plain, busy and dirty scenarios without bins, with bins at the shops, or in the four level parks (every metric identical; their litter is vomit and their guests never fill the meter).
- **Litter-aware routes were built, measured and not kept.** The original's route search is a line tracer, not a weighted search, so the prototype refined its result with a bounded detour search over the path cells (commit `61cfa902`). On the one-cell streets of every scenario and level park it found no detour; on a three-cell street it lowered steps onto litter by 8 to 12 percent (-0.9 and -2.5 SE) and nothing else.
- **Jungle happiness (phase 6).** The 0.8-point drop reproduces; with the complaints off it is -0.92 (t = -4.0), so the complaints are not the cause; six other arrival timers give -0.3 (t = -1.4), eleven together -0.53 (t = -3.4): real but smaller, from the two goal rules together, mechanism unexplained.
- **State and saves.** None: nothing added to the `SmarterGuests` section; a 1.5.0 park is refused with the usual version message. No random draw of its own: a guest that now carries its litter to a bin does not take the drop's draw.
- **Measurement.** New passive metrics `path.litterDropped.perAdmission` and `path.litterBinned.perAdmission` (`IGuestObserver.LitterDropped`, `LitterBinned`), scenario variants `--guest-bins`, `--guest-bins-shops`, `--guest-level-bins N` and the baseline `--guest-rules phase6`; `--guest-cost` takes the scenario flags.
- **Pins.** No existing pin moved; new `ScenarioBinsSmarterFixedRunHash` 0xF43AEA85CC6DB665 and `ScenarioBinsPhase6FixedRunHash` 0x8E13F761512B0043. Explained in DETERMINISM.md.
- **Interface (additive).** `IGuestDecisions.UsesBinCarry` (default false, forwarded by `ScriptGuestDecisions`) and `BinSearchRadius` (default the original's 3; the host accepts 0 to `GuestSimulation.MaxBinRadius` = 16 and throws naming the policy otherwise); `SmarterRuleSet.Bins`, `Phase6`.
