## Smarter guests, phase 6: clean and quiet paths (October 18, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.4.0 to 1.5.0 with the two rules of phase 6 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-6-clean-and-quiet-paths)): (1) the wander goal moves to a cleaner or quieter cell of the original's own random walk when that cell is at least a complaint's worth better (litter around a cell counts 5 per unit, 2 for vomit and stinkbombs; a guest in the cell counts 2); (2) a guest standing in litter (2 units around it, the original's "Too messy" share over nine cells) or in a crowd (5 guests, one more than a queue cell holds) complains with the original's "Too messy" or "Dissatisfied" thought, at a cost of half a happiness point (a tenth of the small step), at most once per 240 turns (a persisted complaint time per guest).
- **No new draws.** The host runs the original wander search unchanged, with the same draws in the same order, and only copies the cells the walk stood on; the policy picks among them and draws nothing.
- **Honest result: no measurable benefit.** In the litter scenarios (24 seeds) guests meet as much litter and as large crowds with the rules as without (steps onto litter per visit 2.30 to 2.52, +0.8 SE; crowded share of guest time 0.058 to 0.060); the crowded share falls only in a park left dirty on purpose (0.061 to 0.055, -3.0 SE). What the rules measurably do is complain (0.5 to 1.5 times per admitted guest), costing up to 0.7 points of guest-time happiness (-1.8 SE in the busy scenario). In the four level parks the crowd is real (a third to a half of guest time in cells of 5 or more) and complaints are 1.8 to 2.4 per admitted guest; the crowded share falls in three parks and rises in Fantasy.
- **Measurement.** `ParkScenario` gets a litter variant (`--guest-litter`, a handyman proxy, `--guest-handyman-turns`, `--guest-litter-effect`, `--guest-scenario-arrivals`), nine passive path metrics (littered and crowded share of guest time, steps onto litter, guests per occupied cell, complaints, moved goals), and `--guest-rules` learns `phase5`, `litter`, `crowding` and `paths`.
- **State and saves.** `Complaints` in the `SmarterGuests` section (birth id and park turn, left out while empty, validated); a 1.4.0 park is refused with the usual version message.
- **Pins.** Every off-path pin and the script-mod pins are unchanged; `ScenarioSmarterFixedRunHash` and `ScenarioFoodSmarterFixedRunHash` unchanged; `SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash` 0x64340E899EC258EB to 0x00ADEF2E451729B9 (the cross park crowds, so its guests complain). Explained in DETERMINISM.md.
- **Interface (additive).** `IGuestDecisions.UsesPathComfort` and `ChoosePath` (forwarded by `ScriptGuestDecisions`), `GuestView.ComplaintTurn`, `PathCellView`, `WanderPathView`, `PathDecision`, `PathComplaint`, `GuestWander.Pick` with a trail, `IGuestObserver.PathComplained` and `WanderGoalMoved`, `SmarterRuleSet` (the renamed rule switches, `Litter` and `Crowding` added).
- **Release: selectable, and the cost.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` 1.5.0 is released as an opt-in extension (`PlayerSelectable: true`, still off by default): the Extensions and mods screen shows it with its name and description in the nine languages (the description no longer says the guests behave like the original's), and the README and MODS.md list it as "off, selectable". The CPU gate (at most 1.10 times the original guests) is met **per guest-turn** (a guest in the park for one park turn): 1.03 in the default scenario and 1.05 to 1.07 with a food shop, down from 1.09 and 1.12 before the optimisation. **In total it is not met** (1.21, and 1.40 with a food shop) because the extension keeps 22 percent (42 percent) more guests in the park; the gate is stated per guest-turn. The optimisation changes no behaviour (every pin unchanged): queued guests are no longer asked about patience on the turns before the give-up test can fire (`IGuestDecisions.QueuePatienceQuietTurns`), the queue hook's view no longer scans every guest for walkers, the candidate view is built once, and two small per-call allocations are gone. New `--guest-report --guest-cost` measures it.
