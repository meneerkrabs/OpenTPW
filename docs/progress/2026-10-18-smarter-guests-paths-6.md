## Smarter guests, phase 6: clean and quiet paths (October 18, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.4.0 to 1.5.0 with the two rules of phase 6 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-6-clean-and-quiet-paths)): (1) the wander goal moves to the cheapest of the cells of the original's own random walk, the linked cells beside its goal and the goal itself, when that is at least 2 cheaper (litter around a candidate 5 per unit, 2 for vomit and stinkbombs; a guest in the cell 2, a guest in the eight cells around it 1); (2) a guest standing in litter (2 units around it, the original's "Too messy" share over nine cells) or in a crowd (5 guests, one more than a queue cell holds) complains with the original's "Too messy" or "Dissatisfied" thought at a cost of 0.1 happiness, at most once per visit (a persisted complaint time per guest).
- **No new draws.** The host runs the original wander search unchanged, with the same draws in the same order, and only copies the cells the walk stood on; the policy picks among candidates and draws nothing.
- **Result.** The share of guest time spent in crowds falls by 15 to 20 percent in the busy and dirty litter scenarios (0.062 to 0.050, -6.1 SE; 0.060 to 0.051, -4.6 SE; 24 seeds) and by 10 to 30 percent in all four level parks, with happiness, revenue, admissions and stuck guests unchanged within the noise. It does not reduce litter exposure: where a guest meets litter is set by its routes, not by where its idle wander ends. The two red flags of the first version (Fantasy stuck guests and admissions, busy-scenario revenue) were investigated: no moved goal left a guest stuck, the Fantasy admissions are chaotic (moving only the arrival timer of the rule-off park gives 84 to 126), and the revenue movement did not reproduce after main's per-cell list change.
- **State and saves.** `Complaints` in the `SmarterGuests` section (birth id and park turn, left out while empty, validated); a 1.4.0 park is refused with the usual version message.
- **Measurement.** `ParkScenario` gets a litter variant (`--guest-litter`, a handyman proxy, `--guest-handyman-turns`, `--guest-litter-effect`, `--guest-scenario-arrivals`), ten passive path metrics, and `--guest-rules` learns `phase5`, `litter`, `crowding` and `paths`.
- **Cost, and the gate.** `--guest-report --guest-cost` measures the guests' update per guest-turn. The gate is stated per guest-turn (a guest in the park for one park turn) and is met: 1.057 of the original's in the default scenario and 1.068 with a food shop (1.09 and 1.12 before an optimisation that changed no behaviour: queued guests are not asked about patience before the give-up test can fire, the queue hook's view no longer scans every guest, the candidate view is built once). In total the cost is 1.2 (1.4 with a food shop) because the extension keeps 18 (44) percent more guests in the park.
- **Still hidden.** The extension stays `PlayerSelectable` false until phase 6 shows a measurable positive effect on the whole rule set's outcomes; its rewritten name and description exist in the nine languages for that day.
- **Pins.** Main's per-cell list change (PR #401) is merged in; every off-path pin and the script-mod pins are main's. Three on-path pins moved because the path rules act in them (`SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash`, the scenario and the food-shop scenario with the extension); the 1.4.0 scenario pin still holds for `SmarterRuleSet.Phase5`. Explained in DETERMINISM.md.
- **Interface (additive).** `IGuestDecisions.UsesPathComfort`, `ChoosePath` and `QueuePatienceQuietTurns` (forwarded by `ScriptGuestDecisions`), `GuestView.ComplaintTurn`, `PathCellView`, `WanderPathView`, `PathDecision`, `PathComplaint`, `GuestWander.Pick` with a trail, `IGuestObserver.PathComplained` and `WanderGoalMoved`, `SmarterRuleSet` (the renamed rule switches, `Litter` and `Crowding` added).
