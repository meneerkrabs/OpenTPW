## Smarter guests, phase 5: planning needs (October 18, 2026)

- **What changed.** `opentpw.smarter-guests` goes from 1.3.0 to 1.4.0 with the three rules of phase 5 ([SMARTER-GUESTS.md](../SMARTER-GUESTS.md#phase-5-planning-needs)): (1) a shop or toilet whose need will cross its level (hunger or thirst byte 100, toilet byte above 80) before the guest is done with the quickest ride it could take (expected wait plus cycle, capped at 180 turns) is scored as if the need had already grown, by the needs block's own growth; (2) a service in the minor decision's window gets each need it serves read one step (10 points) higher; (3) within 160 turns of a meal, a ride that would add at least 25 illness to this guest has its score halved (never below the choice floor). The extension stays hidden (`PlayerSelectable` false) until phase 6.
- **Honest result: no measurable effect.** The needs are slow (only a guest whose id is a multiple of 4 ever grows), so the rules act rarely: in the default scenario one seed of eight differs from 1.3.0; with a food shop (24 seeds) planning and the meal rule change the run but no needs metric moves beyond 1.1 standard errors (vomits per admission 0.357 to 0.347, need thoughts 1.234 to 1.189, unmet needs at departure 0.078 to 0.077, happiness and revenue the same); the detour step changes no decision in 24 seeds because the original's minor decision already retargets to any open attraction in its window. Kept small and cheap; the page says what they do not do.
- **State and saves.** `Meals` in the `SmarterGuests` section (birth id and park turn, birth-id order, left out while empty, validated, refused after the park's turn); a 1.3.0 park is refused with the usual version message.
- **Measurement.** New passive metrics (vomits, vomits within 160 turns of a meal, need thoughts, departures with an unmet need, mean illness at departure, queue exits for the toilet), `--guest-rules none|all|planning,detour,meal` (`none` is 1.3.0's behaviour from the same build) and `--guest-food` (a scenario with a food shop). `IGuestObserver` gains default-implemented `Vomited` and `Thought`.
- **Cost.** Phase 5 adds no measurable CPU (a Stopwatch over 250 in-game days, median of 21 runs: 0.997 of 1.3.0 without a food shop, 1.02 with one, about the two percent of the run-to-run noise); the look at the rides is paid only by a grower near a level.
- **Pins.** Off-path and script-mod pins unchanged; `SmarterGuestsFixedRunHash` and `ScenarioSmarterFixedRunHash` unchanged (no rule acts in them, which the page explains); new `ScenarioFoodSmarterFixedRunHash` 0x336E044598FE1EBB for the food-shop scenario. Explained in DETERMINISM.md.
- **Interface (additive).** `IGuestDecisions.UsesPlanning` (default false, forwarded by `ScriptGuestDecisions`); `GuestView.MealTurn` and `PlanTurns256`; `AttractionView.CycleTurns256` and `OnTheWay`; `SmarterGuestDecisions( SmarterPlanningRules )` (a measurement switch, all three on in the extension).
