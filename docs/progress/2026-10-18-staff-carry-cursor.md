## A hired or carried staff member dangles from the cursor again (October 18, 2026)

Hiring a worker or picking one up showed only the glove: the person in hand was invisible. The tools draw script 0x15 of the person's own sprite bank, slot 6 frames 0-3 looping (the tool's enter 0x46c730 creates it with 0x475a10 on the role's sprite type and the candidate's variant), and OpenTPW drew that slot from the `.FPC` bank, where every staff bank holds it as 2 x 2 pixel placeholders (all but Jungle's explorer, which draws its `.TPC`). The real animation, the person kicking their legs, is in the `.TPC` bank, and tp.exe always draws the `.TPC`: its ESP loader opens the `.FPC` only when a detail flag is set, and the flag's only writer is passed 0 by both of its calls (TPI's Game.exe is the same). An earlier reading had taken the call's second argument for the flag. The Dutch gameplay video shows it at 81:30: the hired handyman dangles at the carry glove (just below it, where the code puts the person above the pointer; this remains open in `GUEST-021`).

OpenTPW now fills slot 6 from the `.TPC` (`GuestSpriteAtlas.LoadSets`; a set with art of its own keeps it). Every frame it hangs the dangling person at the exact ground point under the pointer, 5 units up, with heading 4 plus the camera's yaw sector, as tp.exe's tool frame and sprite pass do (0x46c480, 0x542010). It follows the pointer between cell centres and over the interface; before, the sprite snapped to the hovered cell's centre and vanished over the HUD. Two known differences are registered. `GUEST-021`: tp.exe's picker can also land on an object's model, while OpenTPW uses the terrain point. `GUEST-022`: OpenTPW still draws the `.FPC` of every other set, so the dangling person is about 17 % smaller than the placed one. Moving everything to the `.TPC` also needs the hi-res guest sets and the texture packs' guest atlases rebuilt. The hire window already closes with the hire (0x49b9dd), and the pick-up closes the employee window.

Tests: the sprite position follows the pointer frame by frame; the hire leaves no window on the stack; every staff bank's dangling frames have real art. The native smoke moves the pointer over three nearby points for both tools. At each point it requires the sprite at the ground point under the pointer (which differs from every cell centre), a dangling frame larger than a placeholder, and no hire or employee window open. With `OPENTPW_STAFF_CAPTURE=1` it saves each probe frame.
