## Tour ground and walking bodies (October 18, 2026)

Two tour approximations narrowed against tp.exe (docs/TOURS.md); neither is retired.

- **TOUR-005, the ground the cars clear.** The cell lookup `0x00452AE0` asks the second layer first (`0x0053BF30`), so a kart or water track tile's own piece height map now answers before the object on the cell
  (`TrackRenderer.PieceGround`, `Level.TourObjectGround`): the tile's instance sits two cells over by the engine rotation (`0x00522B90`, `0x00522D50`, `0x00466F90`), its 2 x 2 map is indexed from that corner and the byte is
  scaled by 100/255 on the tile height (`0x00466D70`). Every shipped piece map holds the tile's four cells at all four rotations. The map rebuild `0x00451880` is taken only for a missing or mis-sized map and every shipped
  object map has its shape's size, so it never runs for the catalogue. Left: the park fence's hoarding posts (`0x005229E0`, table `0x00763388`) and the station tiles. Every tour caller passes no owner out-pointer, so a cell
  without an instance giving the terrain was already the original's rule.
- **TOUR-011, the walking bodies.** The walk update (`0x00557D80`) draws every table entry through `0x005580A0`, so the riders of all 38 shipped rides that run WALKON are now seen: walking on and off along the nodes by the
  elapsed share (template 1), standing at the fourth node until WALKGET, and on board the actions 3, 5 and 6 stand, make the short gesture or cheer (templates 3, 8 and 5 at node u2, with the heading of the node's
  orientation); the head actions 1, 2 and 4 draw no body, and the walk float of Zero G swings the body in the running template. WALKON also stores its heading word (the sector count of `atan2(z2 - z1, x2 - x1)`). Presentation only:
  nothing is saved, hashed or drawn from a random stream. Left: the template frame steppers (every body steps each 100 ms), the terrain-height form (no shipped script uses it) and the per-frame cadence of the state changes.

The ground over track tiles is a simulation input for the tour cars, so it is read from the level's own piece lookup (not the drawing's renderer) and a tour over kart or water track flies differently (docs/DETERMINISM.md); no pinned fixture holds one, so the pinned determinism hash does not change.
