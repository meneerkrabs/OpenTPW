## Park information window and lobby sky built from tp.exe (October 18, 2026)

- **UI-914, the park information window.** Besides the Top 3 Thoughts it now has the original's line graph (`0x4A4B90`, `0x4A4750`, `0x480810`, `0x47FE90`, `0x47FCD0`): five independent toggles (people in the park, arrival rate, average happiness, average time in the park, park rating; mask `0x753C48` starts at 8) and the 1 / 3 / 12 year radio buttons (12, 36 and 144 samples, kept while the window is closed), drawn as a black plot with a dark green grid, green axes and numbered steps, each series in the finance graph's first five colours with a 3 x 3 mark per sample under 144 and the last value written at the line end. The scale runs 0 to the highest sample of the selected rings, read one sample past the drawn period as `0x4801D0` does. The counts frame shows the guests in the park (`0x4C9130`) and the visitors so far (`0x4C94C0`), the happiness levels show the percent below 33 (`0x4C9750`) and above 66 (`0x4C9670`) as a blue, orange and yellow bar from `sad_undec.wct` and `hap.wct`, and the three buttons open All Visitors, All Staff and the attractions window, each replacing the window.
- **The data.** `ParkStatisticsHistory` holds the analyser's monthly rings that the month handler `0x4C7720` fills: the guests the bus dropped off (counted per group step, `0x4CF3E0`), the mean happiness, the mean stay of the last 50 guests that reached the stop (`0x4C7600`, whole four-turn steps as days, paid guests only) and the guests in the park; the park rating is read from the ledger, which stores the same value at the same close. It is saved as an optional `StatisticsHistory` (older saves load with it empty, a malformed one is rejected) and kept out of the run hash like the analyser caches because nothing in the simulation reads it, so **the pinned determinism hash is unchanged**.
- **Hover text.** The icon class's frame getter (`0x661596`) is `return 0` and the setter (`0x661525`) stores no frame, so every shown icon names THOUGHTS.str entry 1; OpenTPW now does the same instead of naming each icon's own thought.
- **UI-018, the lobby.** The lobby draws the engine sky's cloud layer (the fantasy level's sky files) with the whole grid in the eased island colour over the fog-coloured clear (`0x5D8B50`, `0x5D96C0`); SKYQUALITY 0 or 1 keeps the fog colour; the flying-mesh share is the LOBBYOBJECTS setting (`0x5D9DB0`, `[0x7858DC]`, presets 10, 70 and 100), no longer fixed at 100; each island placement draws the constructor's three `rand()` values and an island without SKYCOLOUR keeps them (`0x5DFB4A`); the flyers and island clips keep stepping while the globe is up (`0x5D5C50` has no globe branch); the lightning flash is drawn as the quad of state `0x9A1`, whose blend is INVDESTCOLOR and ZERO, so it inverts the picture for a frame (`0x57ED75`, `0x5679B0`). The device word `0x78D8D8` is the 3D-acceleration switch and is 1 here.

UI-018: the lobby's own fog, the engine sky's gate flags, the lobby subsystems' order and whether the scene is drawn behind the globe. No new APPROX ids.
