## Umbrellas: the Theme Park Inc trace and our own art and data pipeline (October 18, 2026)

The first two phases of the umbrellas extension ([UMBRELLAS.md](../UMBRELLAS.md)), content only; nothing is wired into the game yet, so no pin, save or park changes.

- **The trace (U0).** The static trace of the Theme Park Inc no-CD `Game.exe` is in docs/UMBRELLAS.md with `[BIN:TPI-EXE:0x...]` addresses: the umbrella settings, the five guest states, the demand rule (shop gate, weather and generation multipliers, the balloon factor with its zero quirk), the purchase, the weather rule and its day gate, the scheduling, the balloon trade, closing on entering an attraction, the drawing rule and the shipped data. THEME-PARK-INC.md links to it.
- **Our own art and data (U1).** `content/umbrellas` holds four 2 x 2 brolly shops (one per Theme Park World theme, ids 64500 to 64503, available from the start), their `.sam` (price 30, weather 200/200/150/5 percent) and shop script, the umbrella sprite set (three colourways, five directions mirrored to eight, 2x at 256 pixels per unit, the pools kid camera, hotspot at the handle tip, 29 KB) and the hand anchors of the eight 2x kid sets, emitted from the rig by `opentpw_guest_sprites.py --emit-anchors` (exact by construction). `tools/umbrellas/build-content.sh` rebuilds all of it in about 20 seconds from `tools/blender/opentpw_brolly_shop.py`, `opentpw_umbrella.py`, the kit and the pools' converter. No Theme Park Inc or Theme Park World bytes are used.
- **Tests.** `UmbrellasContentTests` (8): ids and names, `.sam` keys, models and wall gaps in all four themes, the shop script, the sprite banks, the anchor table and that nothing is wired in yet.
- **Left for U3.** The anchors of the original (1x) kid sets and the four costume sets need a local measuring script that reads the player's own game frames and writes numbers only.
- Review follow-up: the content's source and licence (our own, MIT) are stated, `ISIndoors` 0 is an open question, and `[EXT:umbrellas-weather-map]` / `[EXT:umbrellas-save-due]` are marked as needing the user's approval before U2.
