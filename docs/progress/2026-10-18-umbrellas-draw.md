## Guests carry drawn umbrellas (October 18, 2026)

Phase U3 of the umbrellas extension: guests whose umbrella is open (or open until a due close, TPI's states 4 and 1, `[BIN:TPI-EXE:0x60ae35]`) are drawn holding it. `UmbrellaRenderer` places the handle tip of the extension's own umbrella sprite at the guest's hand, read from a table by sprite set, slot, direction and step, pulls it 10 % towards the camera so it covers the guest, mirrors five directions to eight and picks the colour from the guest; the thought bubble rises from 2.5 to 6.0 units while the umbrella shows (`[BIN:TPI-EXE:0x60b0a4]`).

The hand anchors of the original 1x kid sets and the four costume sets come from `opentpw-kit measure-anchors`, which reads the player's own frames and writes numbers only (`content/umbrellas/anchors-original.json`; kids from the 2x rig anchors registered to the original silhouettes, costumes from a rule fitted on the kids, leave-one-set-out error 3.55 px). The browser build embeds `content/umbrellas` (shops, the 29 KB sprite set, the tables; 544,475 bytes, about 190 KB deflated, downloaded also with the extension off). Presentation only: no random draws, no pin change, nothing saved. Debug builds take `--debug-umbrellas` and `--debug-umbrellas-follow` for captures. Details in docs/UMBRELLAS.md ("Drawing").

User decisions (2026-10-18, asked directly): the sight-line pull is registered as `APPROX:UMB-004` ("Als benadering (APPROX:UMB-004)"; unknown: the `YAdjustCamera` value and the resulting screen-space placement); `[EXT:umbrellas-draw]` with the steady hand is approved ("Akkoord"); committing `anchors-original.json` is approved, numbers only ("Ja, getallen mogen"). `[EXT:debug-umbrellas]` stays a Debug-only test hook.

Also approved by the plan of 2026-10-18 ("doe het allemaal"): the five-to-eight facing and the hand anchors are ours (TPI's umbrella has no directions and sits straight above the guest). TPI also closes the umbrella of a guest standing on a kart or water track cell (`0x60AF32`); documented under UMB-002, not modelled (U2).
