## Umbrellas follow-up: measured numbers, UMB-002 narrowed, reproducible frame cost (October 18, 2026)

(The kid score of 75 % in the shipped data and the stale names in comments came with #626.) A post-merge review of the umbrellas tuning (phase U5) found claims that the measurements did not carry, and this change corrects them. The "+$40 a week" for a kid score of 100 % was not a measurement: rerunning the matrix gives the same mean balance (49,742.5) at 75 % and at 100 %, so the docs now say there is no measurable difference in money; the 12.5 against 13.75 buyers and the balloon releases (4 against 7) stand. `TheKidScoreOf75PercentSellsNoMoreThanOf100` allowed two extra buyers and barely tested anything; it is now `TheKidScoreOnlyMattersThroughBalloonHoldersAndSellsFewerUmbrellasAt75Percent`: the two values play out identically when nobody holds a balloon, and over the four seeds 75 % sells fewer umbrellas (50 against 55) and lets fewer balloons go (16 against 28).

`APPROX:UMB-002` is narrowed from "not identified" to what `dis.py` reads in the no-CD `Game.exe`: the scheduling (`0x613EA0`), the carrying out (`0x614010`) and the draw routine (`0x60AE00`) stand down for a guest on a kart or water track cell. `0x67F890` tests the 40-byte record found through the tile record's first word (and an extension record's head), not the tile record itself; `0x685680` tests the 40-byte record of the same cell index. OpenTPW's guests never stand on one, so the test stays unmodelled. The text about the roof no longer claims the roof hides the umbrella exactly where it hides the guest (the umbrella sits 1.5 units nearer to the camera).

The native frame-cost figure now has a complete command: the new Debug flag `--debug-umbrellas-crowd N` stages N extra guests with `UmbrellaPerf.Stage` (the same staging as the browser harness), which is how a crowd of 342 guests is reached (`--perf-arrivals 1` alone gave 18). Re-measured: the umbrellas add under about a millisecond of world render natively; the browser `umbrellaCost` scenario adds about 1.1 ms of median frame time for 400 umbrellas (report kept in the notes folder `umbrella-perf`, outside the repository). Presentation and test code only; no pin moves.
