## Umbrellas in the simulation: the brolly shop, the umbrella states and the balloon trade (October 18, 2026)

Phase U2 of the umbrellas extension ([UMBRELLAS.md](../UMBRELLAS.md)): `opentpw.umbrellas` is registered, on by default, and its four brolly shops are listed in the Theme Park World catalogs; guests buy an umbrella in the rain and carry it through the five states of the Theme Park Inc trace.
Nothing is drawn yet (U3 does the drawing; U4 the rain mood).

- **Extension plumbing.** `OfficialExtensions.Umbrellas` (1.0.0, simulation, on by default, not in a Theme Park Inc run, `FeatureGates.Umbrellas`), `UmbrellaContent.CatalogRoot` in `ModContentRoots.CatalogRoots` and the `ObjectCatalog` cache key. A park save records the extension only while the park has a brolly shop
  or a guest with an umbrella (the pools rule, `ParkResumeMods.UsedOnlyExtensionIds`), with the asset keys of the placed shops only; a recorded extension that is off or in another version refuses the load, and a save with an umbrella but no record is refused.
- **The score.** The host applies, after the decision policy's score (`IGuestDecisions` is untouched), a brolly shop's gate (0 with an umbrella or outside rain, storm and snow), the weather percentage, the held balloon's cut with the original's zero quirk and the generation percentage, for the extension's own objects only; a balloon shop refuses a balloon holder
  and an open or opening umbrella in a park that uses umbrellas (`[EXT:umbrellas-balloon-gate]`: tp.exe's own score reads no appearance word).
- **The states.** A purchase opens the umbrella at once with one world draw for the colour; entering any attraction closes it; on a qualifying day (the first game day, then one in four) the weather schedules, cancels and carries out opening and closing after a delay below 3 guest updates; an umbrella that opens, or a brolly shop that is entered, lets a held balloon go for
  -15 happiness; an open or pending umbrella removes the walking speed term. `APPROX:UMB-001` (the delay is a per-guest hash, no stream draw), `UMB-002` (the track-cell test is left out), `UMB-003` ("inside" is the in-attraction states; the age is the time since creation); all three stay, registered in `UmbrellaApproximations`.
- **Saves and pins.** Optional guest fields `UmbrellaState`, `UmbrellaType`, `UmbrellaDueTurn`, written only for a guest with an umbrella, validated, restored and hashed only then. Every existing pin is unchanged with the extension on or off; the new `UmbrellasFixedRunHash` = `0xE912E7E5D5B6E612`
  covers the fixed run plus a brolly shop and a rain that comes and goes (45 buyers, every state seen). A park saved mid-rain and resumed continues with the same hash.
- **Tests.** `UmbrellaRulesTests` (31: the weather mapping, the gates, the percentages, the balloon factor and its quirk, the day gate, the transition table, the delay bounds, the colour draw, the speed term), `UmbrellasSimulationTests` (20: the score through the host, the purchase and its one draw, the balloon trade, a kid in the rain through a ride and back,
  a pending open, the snapshot round trip and its validator, the hash, a resume), `UmbrellasContentTests` (the registration, the catalogs of the four themes with the extension on and off, a Theme Park Inc run, the save rule), the determinism pins.
- **User-approved extensions (2026-10-18).** `[EXT:umbrellas-weather-map]`, `[EXT:umbrellas-save-due]` and `[EXT:umbrellas-balloon-gate]` are OpenTPW's own choices that U2 implements; the user approved all three on 2026-10-18.
- **Review fixes.** The balloon gate is now per park (a holder count the simulation keeps through purchase, departure and restore, so the test needs no scan); `GiveUmbrella` mirrors `TPI-EXE:0x612ea9` (state 3 first, 4 only when no sprite exists; unreachable in play, so no pin moved); `UmbrellaTerms` is tagged `[EXT:umbrellas-extension]`; the Extensions screen has the umbrellas name and description in all eleven languages, and the brolly shops are named in Italian and Hebrew too. The three approvals are quoted in the decisions of [UMBRELLAS.md](../UMBRELLAS.md).
