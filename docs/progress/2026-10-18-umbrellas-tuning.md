## Umbrellas tuned from captures and measurements (October 18, 2026)

Phase U5 of the umbrellas extension, with the simulation (U2), the drawing (U3) and the rain mood (U4) running together. Captures of all four themes in rain, storm, snow and clear, at both camera pitches and the wide zoom, with the pool guest art on and off, showed the canopy too large (about 1.2 times a kid's height on screen after the 45 degree foreshortening, hiding the head): the quad is now scaled 0.85 about the handle tip `[EXT:umbrellas-size]`, and the pull towards the camera, 10 % of the distance in Theme Park Inc (8 units at zoom 80), is now a fixed margin of 1.5 units: the larger pull drew an umbrella over the roof of a booth or shelter that hides its guest `[EXT:umbrellas-pull-cap]`. Hand anchors, mirroring, colours and the bubble lift (6.0, re-read at `0x60b0a4`) needed no change.

A headless rainy week (fixed seed, a park with a brolly shop, four seeds) gives the numbers for the open question of the kid score: 100 % sells about a tenth more umbrellas than Theme Park Inc's 75 %, but the extra buyers are balloon holders paying -15 happiness for the trade, so the week's mean happiness ends below a park without any shop (51.5 against 52.0, against 52.9 at 75 %). The owner chose 75 % ("75%, zoals TPI"). `GenerationModifier.KidScorePercentage` is 75 in the four `Brolly.sam` files and `tools/umbrellas/brolly.sam` (it had been left at OpenTPW's 100), and `SyntheticPark`'s `brollyKidScore` defaults to 75 so the synthetic brolly shop mirrors the shipped data. The shop pins were recomputed and held: `UmbrellasFixedRunHash` stays `0xE912E7E5D5B6E612` and `RainMoodWithUmbrellasFixedRunHash` stays `0xADEE2FA6AFC2ACE3` (both were those values at 100); `RainMoodFixedRunHash` has no shop. The owner also approved `[EXT:umbrellas-size]` and `[EXT:umbrellas-pull-cap]` ("Beide akkoord"). Frame cost: 400 umbrellas add about 1.1 ms of median frame time in the browser's interpreter build (60 frames a second throughout, `tools/web-perf` scenario `umbrellaCost`), and 342 umbrellas add under about a millisecond of world render natively (a Debug build; the complete command is in docs/UMBRELLAS.md, "Frame cost", with the new `--debug-umbrellas-crowd` flag). The size and the pull move no pin. Details in docs/UMBRELLAS.md ("Tuning").

The original's draw routine also closes an umbrella (`0x60AEDC`), but on a kart or water **track cell**, not under a roof: the same two predicates are UMB-002's "view condition"; none of 71,591 sampled guest positions in Jungle (with and without a go-kart or boat track) was on such a cell, so U2 need not implement it.
