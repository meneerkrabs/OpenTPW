## Umbrellas tuned from captures and measurements (October 18, 2026)

Phase U5 of the umbrellas extension, with the simulation (U2), the drawing (U3) and the rain mood (U4) running together. Captures of all four themes in rain, storm, snow and clear, at both camera pitches and the wide zoom, with the pool guest art on and off, showed the canopy too large (about 1.2 times a kid's height on screen after the 45 degree foreshortening, hiding the head): the quad is now scaled 0.85 about the handle tip `[EXT:umbrellas-size]`, and the pull towards the camera, a share of the distance in Theme Park Inc, stops at 13 units, the 10 % of the original's far zoom, so the wide zoom range does not draw umbrellas over nearer objects `[EXT:umbrellas-pull-cap]`. Hand anchors, mirroring, colours and the bubble lift (6.0, re-read at `0x60b0a4`) needed no change.

A headless rainy week (fixed seed, a park with a brolly shop, four seeds) gives the numbers for the open question of the kid score: 100 % sells about a tenth more umbrellas than Theme Park Inc's 75 %, but the extra buyers are balloon holders paying -15 happiness for the trade, so the week's mean happiness ends below a park without any shop (51.5 against 52.0, against 52.9 at 75 %); the recommendation is to keep 75 %, awaiting the user's choice (the data is unchanged). Frame cost: 400 umbrellas add about 1.4 to 1.7 ms of median frame time in the browser's interpreter build (60 frames a second throughout, `tools/web-perf` scenario `umbrellaCost`), nothing measurable natively. No pin moved. Details in docs/UMBRELLAS.md ("Tuning").
