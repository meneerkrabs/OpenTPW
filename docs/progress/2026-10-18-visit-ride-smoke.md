## A visit's flat rides get riders, and the visit smoke rides one (October 18, 2026)

- The native read-only visit smoke (`--visit-park <file> --smoke-test`) failed on a package with a rideable flat ride, such as one exported from the original Jungle. Since the ride view port, a ride whose model has a camera seat is ridden from the seat node's pose (`RideCameraMode.Advance`), but the smoke still compared the camera with the fallback frame (`RideCameraMode.Frame`). It now asks the view for the seat it rides (`RideCameraMode.CurrentSeat`: the ride model's camera seat, or a coaster's camera car), checks that the camera sits on it and, off a coaster, that it is the model's camera seat (`RideSeat.FindNode`). Only a view without a seat keeps the frame and direction checks, now with the train the view rides. A coaster whose car model has a seat is ridden from that seat too, so the old check would have failed for coasters as well.
- With that fixed, the smoke boarded the Belly Bounce and timed out waiting for it to move. The ride itself was fine: open, its script running, its idle animation advancing. Bouncy.RSE bounces only while `BOUNCING VAR_ONRIDE` finds riders (`LOOPANIM 5`, else the idle `LOOPANIM 2`), and the visit had no guests. A package carries none, and the visit's arrivals counted park turns from 0, so the first group was due only at turn 604, about 150 s in (`Arrival.TimeBetweenArrivals` 150 units of four turns, tp.exe `0x004CF3E0`). A long run showed it: no guest for 150 s, then four off the bus, and at about 193 s one boarded the Belly Bounce and the seat bounced.
- An own park started from the shipped save (Instant Action) reads its economy's turn, which starts at the save's turn (755 in the Jungle), so its first group comes at once; a Full Simulation park has no such save and starts at turn 0. A visit has no economy, so its arrivals now read the turn of the save it is built on plus its guests' own turns (`Level.VisitArrivalClock`, `EXT:online-visit-arrivals`). The first bus comes at once, and in a Jungle export a guest rides the Belly Bounce 40 to 55 s into the visit. A visit without a save keeps the guests' own turns.
- The smoke boards the first open rideable ride (the prototype after the park's objects), since a ride published closed never runs. It logs what it boarded, with the riders and the guests in the park. The pass line reports the riders and how long the ride took to move. The watchdog allows two minutes and names the ride's riders, queue and guests when it fires. The motion check is unchanged: simulation frames must move the camera at least 0.25 units.
- Docs: docs/ONLINE.md (the smoke description, the Ride bullet and tag 069: rides with a camera seat can be ridden in a visit; the visit's arrivals), GUESTS.md (the visit's arrival timer) and reverse/ONLINE-VISIT.md (the visit's rides).
- Verified: `ArrivalVehicleTests.AVisitBuiltOnASaveSendsItsFirstGroupAtOnce`, which fails without the clock (it pins `Level.VisitArrivalClock`; its two-line wiring in `Level.SetupGuests` is covered only by the native visit smoke, since no test builds a visit level without the renderer), and the plain visit smoke with a package exported from the original Jungle (`ParkSharing.CreatePackage( ParkSnapshotBuilder.FromOriginal( park, null ), … )`): "Read-only visit smoke passed: 2501 frames, 14 immutable local objects, rode Belly Bounce through HUD (moved 41.5 s after boarding with 1 rider aboard; camera 8.98,-34.98,2.72 → 8.98,-34.98,2.40), no economy, build/remove/save/load/export rejected, GPU capture 1280x720." With the package of `--export-park … --load-original-level jungle` (its whole cell map and layout digest) the Belly Bounce moved 51.6 s after boarding, with the digest verified. The with-data test suite passed (7271, 84 skipped), and so did the front-end smoke, whose visit now gets its arrivals at once.
