## The waves push the swimmers (October 18, 2026)

A wave machine's waves now carry the free swimmers of their pool, not only lift their drawn height. While a crest passes a swimmer's cell, `PoolWaves.Push` gives it a push along the waves' way
(+x +y): the integer form of the wave at the swimmer's own point, where the drawn crest is (the phase in 64 steps of a period from the park turn and the 16.16 point, the sine from the swimmers' own 64-step heading table, nothing in a trough) times the
envelope and height / 3 of `PoolWaves.MaxPush`, 0.12 cell a turn. At full swell a height-3 crest pushes about 0.085 cell a turn on each axis, about the slowest stroke (0.082), so on the crest a slow swimmer heading against the waves is carried back a little; over a period the push averages a third of that, and height 1 is a third again. The carried distance is no stroke (the swimming animation does not advance for it).
`PoolWaterEffects` is a new `IPoolCurrent`: the swim host adds the push after the swimmer's stroke (`PoolSwimming.Drift`) only where it lands on free water and not within 0.7 cell of another body, so
nobody is pushed through a wall or a crowd, and the swimmer steers back towards its goal on the next turn. Swimmers in an activity (jets, rings, the spa) and swimmers on their way out are not
pushed. `[EXT:pools-waves]`.

This is simulation and deterministic: integers only, no random draw of its own and no new stored field (the position is the guest's). The moved swimmers reach their goals at other turns, so their
goal draws move, and `DeterminismTests.PoolsAttractionsFixedRunHash` moves from `0x5232DC9F32832BC6` to `0xD9F94E889BDAB453` (92 swims; jets 12, river 13, rope swing 5, spa 3 starts). Every other pin is
unchanged: only that run has a wave machine, and a pool without waves pushes nothing.

Checked: `Pools*`, `Determinism*`, `GuestMeasurement*` and `Save*` tests, 905 passed without game data and the pool and determinism tests 321 passed with it; a silent Jungle capture sequence of a
10 x 7 pool with a wave machine and twelve swimmers runs without error. New tests: the push only while the drawn crest at the swimmer's point is up, along the diagonal, by height and envelope, never in a rest or with the waves off; the
drift stopped by the wall and by another body; a swimmer pushed apart from the same swimmer in a flat pool for more than 50 of 500 turns and always in the water; waves that are off move, draw and
pay nothing; a save while the waves push a swimmer restores and continues identically.
