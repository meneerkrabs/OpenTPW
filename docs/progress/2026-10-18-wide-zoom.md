## The park camera zooms out to tp.exe's own 40..300 range (October 18, 2026)

The park camera now has the zoom range tp.exe keeps behind its camera mode flag `0x200`: 40 to 300 instead of the normal 70 to 130 (clamp `0x42c631`, constants `0x74c9d8`/`0x74c9dc`). The shipped
game never sets that flag (its setter was not found, probably a developer mode), so this is an OpenTPW extension, `[EXT:wide-zoom]`, user-approved on 2026-10-18. It is an option, on by default:
"Wide zoom" on the OpenTPW page of the options screen (text in all nine languages), saved with the other options in `opentpw-options.json` on the desktop and in the browser. With it off the range is
70..130 as before, and switching it off while zoomed out brings the zoom back to 130.

The pitch and the eye (`0x42b1c0`) read the zoom value alone, so the original's own formulas run unchanged over the wider range: the pitch line continues to 35 degrees at 40 and stops at the original's
clamp of 84.27 degrees from a zoom value of about 188, so at 300 the camera is 298.5 original units up and 30 behind the target, looking almost straight down. Inside 70..130 the camera, Home (110), the
wheel step (10) and the zoom key rate are unchanged. Checked in Jungle with silent offscreen captures at 40, 70, 110, 130, 200 and 300 and at the map corners: no change was needed for the far plane,
fog, culling, the terrain edge and skirt, the cursor pick (the optical axis meets the ground at the target at every zoom; `WideZoomTests` pick at six zooms and three yaws), the sky (never in view:
the view stays at least 8 degrees below the horizon) or the rain. The pan speed keeps the original's 0.02 x sqrt(zoom) per millisecond, so a screen takes about 1.6 times longer to cross at 300 than
at 110, as in the original with the flag. Frame time on the desktop (Jungle, unthrottled, 1512 x 843): about 3.5 to 4 ms at Home and 6.7 ms at zoom 300 over 1500 frames (3.4 to 3.7 ms against 1.7
to 3.4 ms over 9000 frames), well inside 16.7 ms; in the browser 60 fps with no long task, the main thread 1.3 points busier than at zoom 110 and the frame p95 0.2 ms longer. A crowded park was not measured.
New hooks for measuring: `--perf-zoom <value>` (native) and `--zoom <value>` of `tools/web-perf/run.mjs` (browser). The camera is presentation: no determinism pin moves.
