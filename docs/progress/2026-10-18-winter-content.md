## Winter: the research page and our own art and data pipeline (October 18, 2026)

Phases W0 and W2 of the winter extension ([WINTER.md](../WINTER.md)), documentation and content only; nothing is wired into the game, so no pin, save, park or draw call changes.

- **The research and decisions (W0).** docs/WINTER.md records where it snows (only the Halloween world, about 4 real minutes per 35-minute year, December being row 4 of the season table), that tp.exe has no ground snow (three strings, three `snow.tga` loads, all CRain's), that Theme Park Inc's snowfield is painted terrain, the original drinks shops' data, the free uniform slot of test.shader and the user's decisions of October 18, 2026, with `[EXT:winter-*]` labels.
- **Our own art and data (W2).** `content/winter` holds eight 2 x 2 market huts with pitched roofs (a winter kiosk and a glühwein stall per Theme Park World theme, ids 64010 to 64017 in the range 64010 to 64499; 64000 to 64009 belong to the example lamp mod), their `.sam` (kiosk: `AppearanceEffect` 4, price 25; stall: `ShopType` 4 drinks with a "Spices" ingredient, price 30, `VomitEffect` 5) and shop scripts, the warm item sprite set (bobble hats, earmuffs and mittens in a few colourways, five directions mirrored to eight, 2x at 256 pixels per unit, rendered over a holdout head so the hidden back parts are cut out, 28 KB) and `anchors.json`, schema 2: the head top, head centre, both hands and the hands' visibility for every land slot, frame and direction of the eight 2x kid sets. The umbrellas' schema 1 anchors are emitted by unchanged code and stay byte-identical (`--emit-winter-anchors` writes a separate file). `tools/winter/build-content.sh` rebuilds all of it in about a minute. No Theme Park World or Theme Park Inc bytes are used.
- **Tests.** `WinterContentTests` (10): ids and range overlap with every other content root, `.sam` keys and decisions, models, wall gaps and scripts in all four themes, the sprite banks, anchor coverage and agreement with the umbrellas' table, and that nothing is wired in.
- **Left for later phases.** Snow cover (W1), the simulation behind `opentpw.winter` (W3), drawing with the original sets' head anchors (W4), the cold rule (W5).
- Review follow-up: the shop names are in all eleven interface languages (Italian and Hebrew added), the placeholder ids moved out of the lamp mod's range (64010/64011), and WINTER.md's design section now states the cold rule as replacing the rain mood's snow +1, as decided.
