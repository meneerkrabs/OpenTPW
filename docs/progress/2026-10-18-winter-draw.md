## Guests wear the warm items (October 18, 2026)

Phase W4 of the winter extension: guests whose `WarmItems` are set are drawn with them. `WarmItemRenderer` places the extension's own hat sprite at the head top, earmuffs at the head centre and mittens at the visible hands, read from a table by sprite set, slot, direction and step (`WinterAnchorTable`, parsed into arrays at load, no per-frame allocation), pulls each 1 % towards the camera so it covers the guest and stays behind an umbrella, mirrors five directions to eight and takes the colour from the guest and the item (`WinterRules.SpriteSlotOf`). It shares `GuestRenderer.TryMount` with the umbrellas, so it follows the walk's bob and the swinging arm; it also uses the guests' evening sprite tint (the umbrellas' renderer lacked it and now has it).

The head anchors of the original 1x kid sets and the four costume sets come from `opentpw-kit measure-head-anchors`, which reads the player's own frames and writes numbers only (`content/winter/anchors-original.json`: kids from the 2x rig anchors registered to the original silhouettes, costumes from a rule fitted on the kids, leave-one-set-out error of the head top 2.04 px mean, 5.59 px largest). Mittens are not drawn on the original sets (user-approved 2026-10-18). The browser build embeds `content/winter` (1,322,442 bytes, about 406 KB deflated per file). Presentation only: no random draws, no pin change, nothing saved. Debug builds take `--debug-winter` for captures; the browser harness has a `winter` scenario (`tools/web-perf/run.mjs --scenarios jungle,winter`). Details in docs/WINTER.md ("Drawing").

User decision (October 19, 2026): `[EXT:winter-draw]` is approved, "Alle drie akkoord": the 1 % camera pull (an umbrella falls over the hat), mittens turning with the forearm, and hats and earmuffs on costumed guests too. `[EXT:debug-winter]` is a capture and test hook only. Recorded in docs/WINTER.md.
