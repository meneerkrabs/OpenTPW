## Winter in the simulation: the kiosk, the glühwein stall and wintry Decembers (October 18, 2026)

Phase W3 of the winter extension ([WINTER.md](../WINTER.md#simulation-phase-w3)): `opentpw.winter` is registered, on by default, and its kiosk and glühwein stall of each theme are listed in the Theme Park World catalogs; guests buy from them. The items are not drawn yet (W4).

- **Extension plumbing.** `OfficialExtensions.Winter` (1.0.0, simulation, on by default, used-only in saves, not in a Theme Park Inc run, `FeatureGates.Winter`), `WinterContent.CatalogRoot` in `ModContentRoots.CatalogRoots` and the `ObjectCatalog` cache key, names and descriptions in the eleven interface languages. A park save records the extension only while
  the park has one of its shops or a guest with an item or warmth, with the asset keys of the placed shops only.
- **The kiosk.** `AppearanceEffect` 4: **sells all year** (the user's decision of 2026-10-18/19, "Het hele jaar, vraag via seizoen"; demand follows the season terms, winter 150 / spring and autumn 60 / summer 20, and the snow, 200) and not to a guest with both slots full; a purchase gives a head item (hat or earmuffs from a hash of the guest id) first and then mittens, with colours from a hash of guest id and item and **no random draw**. Guest fields `WarmItems` and `WarmUntilDay` are optional, validated, and saved and hashed only when set.
- **The stall.** A normal drinks shop (`ShopType 4`, Ice mechanics labelled "Spices", no drunk effects) with the weather and season percentages of its `.sam` (`SeasonModifier.*` are our own keys). tp.exe `0x4e16b0` confirms the drinks challenge counts it and the kiosk's `ShopType 1` is counter 5, not costumes.
- **Wintry Decembers.** `opentpw.winter-weather` (off by default, recorded in every save while on): a December roll in the rain band of the jungle and fantasy parks is snow without lightning, with the storm's drops, so umbrellas and the rain mood read snow, and the forecast says snow; Halloween and space are untouched. The winter shops read snow as snow even with lightning (Halloween's snowfall), the umbrellas and the rain mood keep their map. tp.exe's converter `0x512f00` has no calendar input.
- **Pins.** `WinterFixedRunHash` moved `0x8F7023321586F670` to `0xD4E2340C99394B73` (the kiosk sells all year with new season terms; the fixture's calendar is May then December; cause in DETERMINISM.md). New: the guest-level pins of the umbrellas and the rain mood under real jungle weather across a December, option off `0xF4A00471E60B9535` and on `0x296DA4DA6A8BE323`. The jungle's 400-day weather pins off `0x58F5D2429FDCADAA` and on `0x2DC4898B7594C4FA` and every other existing pin are unchanged. Resumes mid-snow continue identically.
- **Tests.** `WinterSimulationTests`, `WinterWiringTests`, `WinterWeatherTests`, the determinism pins; `WinterContentTests` now checks the wiring.
- **Approval.** The labels `[EXT:winter-item-colour]`, `[EXT:winter-spice-label]`, `[EXT:winter-season-terms]`, `[EXT:winter-snow-save]` and the id range are approved by the user ("Alles akkoord", October 18, 2026). `[EXT:winter-kiosk-gate]` is retired: the user decided on 2026-10-18/19 that the kiosk sells all year ("Het hele jaar, vraag via seizoen"). `[EXT:winter-save]` implements the approved rule "recorded in a save only for parks that use them", with the optional guest fields. `[EXT:winter-weather]` is an approved option; its December rain and storms become snow without lightning, which stops the lightning clock and makes the weather stream diverge from the first wintry spell.
- **Golden ticket.** "All researched and built" asks for the shipped buyable objects only; the extensions' `research: false` objects (winter, umbrellas, pools) were already excluded and a test now covers all three.
