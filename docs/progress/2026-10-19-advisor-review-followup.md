## Advisor review follow-up: one telling per event, a full arrival ring for loaded parks (October 19, 2026)

Fixes the findings of the post-merge reviews of the staff (#631), queues, prices and research (#634) and tickets and challenges (#636) advisor batches.

- **A met challenge is told once.** The restored-advice extension no longer says 319 and 320 (the double-or-nothing offer): the original's own path posts 270 for the follow-up offer and 272 for the win, so with the extension on (the default) the same event was told two or three times. The two entries, their trigger and the follow-up count are gone, and the extension restores **43** messages instead of 45 (`restored` tags in `content/data/advisor-producers.toml`, LIPS.md, MODS.md, ADVISOR-SCAN.md, README.md and the website). Principle: the original's own advice wins and a restored line never tells an event the original tells. A test runs a real park with a follow-up chain through `AutomaticAdvisor` with the extension on and asserts the original's lines only.
- **No stray first-group line after loading.** 281 (exactly one arrival group in the ring) fired once after loading an established park, because the advisor's arrival ring restored empty while tp.exe's saved ring is full. A park from an original save now reads its saved ring (`SaveArrivalRing`, `OriginalParkImport.ArrivalGroups`: the analyser serializer `0x4C5D70` writes it after the recent-stays ring through `0x4CE660` as current entry, count, wrapped byte, scratch word and five entries; the sizes come oldest first). A legacy OpenTPW save has no ring: a park with admissions takes it as full (`AdvisorArrivalRing.AssumeFilled`, a stand-in registered as ADVISOR-028, not a port of the original's filled flag; the unknown sizes read as 0 and real sizes replace them). Aggregate admissions cannot recover the original group count or sizes: a legacy park saved after exactly one group can lose 281, while two to four held groups would not qualify for it; hype and big-busload scores can also differ until real arrivals replace the relevant unknown values. A park without admissions keeps its empty ring, and a snapshot that has a non-empty ring is taken as saved.
- **Ticket counts.** `TicketsAfter` took the later wins of a check off the player's total after the ledger had added the park's set, so a later win the ledger already held (a replayed theme) was subtracted although it was never added, one ticket too few. The later wins now leave the park's set before the ledger adds it in (`AdvisorPlayerTickets.Without` is gone), with a replay test.
- **Docs.** The scan notes now read 9 computed callbacks left (the `blocked` scan rows) besides the 21 constant-zero stubs, in place of "27" and "127"; 344 is listed as the one help descriptor that is posted after all (the first challenge offer); the #636 note counts agree with the inventory (234 wired of 351, 30 scanned messages unported: 21 stubs and 9 computed); the #634 note says the booth's perception ring 0 is economy state that enters the world hash (the advisor itself stays outside). `PlayerTicketLedger.cs` is CRLF again, as before #636.
- **`HaveEverTrained` stays in the world hash.** Nothing in the simulation reads it, but `DETERMINISM.md` and `StaffRoleOrderTests` name it as hashed gameplay data, so dropping it is a separate, deliberate change; the doc now says so. No pin moves either way.
- No game data, captions or `Advisor.sam` values were added.
