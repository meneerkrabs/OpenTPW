## Browser transfer and memory (October 19, 2026)

Transfer: the pools sprite files (`.TPC`, 7.9 MB for a Jungle visit) are now published with Brotli and gzip copies (5.0 MB sent), cached for a year by their content hash (`?v=`) instead of being asked again by 48 requests per visit, a browser without Brotli gets the gzip copy instead of a proxy compressing the 44 MB runtime again on every request, and the page no longer asks for `/favicon.ico`. A cold load plus a Jungle park sends 17.4 MB in 104 requests (20.2 MB in 105 before); a reload makes 13 requests, all 304, instead of 61.

Memory: the lobby scene and the terrain released only their buffers, not their materials (and the shader copy, pipeline and uniform buffer in each), so every lobby visit leaked about 160 materials and 5-6 MB; live managed memory in Jungle fell from 72-86 MB (growing from visit to visit) to 61-62 MB (flat), and the renderer's resident memory by 63-270 MB. Equal shader sources share one string and the particle atlas drops its CPU pixels after the upload. Texture and simulation digests are unchanged. Uploading the 8-bit textures as 16-bit formats is not lossless for any real texture (34 of 8,412 are exactly representable) and was not done; GPU block compression, palette atlases and a smaller render-target footprint are measured and recommended in docs/BROWSER-PERFORMANCE.md ("Transfer and memory"), not shipped. The harness gained `--upstream` (the production server's transport), the `reload` and `transfer` scenarios, GPU texture and resident-memory counters, `--track-origins` and `--gc`.
