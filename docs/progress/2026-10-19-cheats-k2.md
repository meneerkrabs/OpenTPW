## Cheats, phase K2: the original cheat mode, its twelve Alt keys and the CHTS chunk (October 19, 2026)

- **The original's cheat mode is ported** ([CHEATS.md](../CHEATS.md#the-original-cheat-mode-k2)) and works with or without the `opentpw.cheats` extension, as the user decided. Renaming the park
  to "Kevin the" / "Cosmic Cow" in the ticket price window turns it on (the rename handler's XOR-by-index compare, `0x4993E0`); the sign keeps the name. From the next park turn the twelve
  Alt keys work: Alt+R makes every research item's object buildable without researching it (`0x503FF0`), Alt+1 credits 10,000 as a prize (`0x4D0190`), Alt+G and Alt+Num1..9 award the ten
  non-secret golden tickets, the global ones with record 100 through the player's holder rule (`0x5AF810`, `0x5AF940`). A cheat fires when its key is released with Alt alone held, as in
  the original (`0x40C990`), and Alt+R and Alt+G no longer open the research and entry price windows (the dispatcher's exact modifier match).
- **Tickets from cheats count**, toward keys and theme unlocks, and are silent: no advisor line, only a short note on the screen, as for every applied cheat (four new UI strings in all eleven
  languages). The player's progress file marks the tickets a cheat earned (`"Cheated": true`), and a file without cheats keeps its bytes.
- **Original saves.** Importing a player's original save applies the `CHTS` chunk's first byte (nonzero marks the park as cheated); exporting writes the park's own chunk, `01 01` for a
  cheated park and `00 00` otherwise. The jungle's shipped `Easymode.TPWI` turned out to carry `01 01` (the eleven Nexus corpus saves carry `00 00`): a park started from it is not marked, so
  Instant Action parks keep their saves and pins.
- **Alt+R survives original saves**: importing a cheated save makes the objects its research cheat marked buildable again, and the export writes the mark back. With Alt alone held the
  keypad's 4 and 6 no longer scroll the camera (they are ticket cheats, as in the original's exact key match), and a key let go together with Alt still fires.
- **Port decisions** recorded with labels: the shipped seed's bytes, the original's three first-use guards that clear the mode once per process run (not reproduced), the turn boundary, and the
  message of type `0x15` (not posted; COMPAT-007 narrowed to it and the online record copies). SAVE-010 keeps only the chunk's second byte.
- **Tests.** `CheatsOriginalTests`: the secret name and its transform, the rename through `Level`, the key table and the exact Alt rule, the real HUD frame firing on the release once and only in
  cheat mode, each cheat's effect, silent tickets and the global holder rule with record 100, Instant Action, the ledger's provenance and file, the notes, and an imported and exported `CHTS`.
