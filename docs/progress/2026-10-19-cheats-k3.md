## Cheats, phase K3: the cheats window, arrivals by vehicle and the weather (October 19, 2026)

- **`opentpw.cheats` can now be switched on** on the Extensions and mods screen (it stays off by default). With it on, **Alt+C** opens the cheats window in a Theme Park World park of the
  player's own ([CHEATS.md](../CHEATS.md#the-cheats-window-k3)). It is built from the options pages' pieces and every button queues a cheat for the next park turn. Its first row turns the
  original's cheat mode on, as the secret park name does; the other buttons work once the mode is on.
- **Visitors by vehicle.** A bus, plane or boat brings a group at the next turn, sized into the dispatch's own bands (bus up to 35, plane 36 to 60, boat 61 or more) from the next natural
  group's size and cut by the park limit as every group is. It is delivered like a natural group, and waits while another group or vehicle is at work.
- **The weather.** Clear, rain, thunderstorm or snow in every theme, applied at once as a forecast with no warning; the natural schedule goes on from it.
- **The original's cheats in the window too:** a chosen golden ticket (the ten that have an Alt key, shown beside each), 10,000 money and every research item's object, all the same cheats
  as the keys, so they mark nothing about the extension.
- **Determinism.** Every cheat still applies at a turn boundary with no draw; a cheat the park cannot take yet waits and is recorded at the turn it applies. A new pin,
  `CheatsFixedRunHash` = `0x1020E490C9B2E247`, runs a scripted sequence (money, research, a ticket, a boat and rain), and a park saved before and after the boat continues identically.
- **Strings.** 32 new UI strings (the window's labels and choices, the extension's name and description) in all eleven languages.
- **Tests.** `CheatsExtensionTests`: the extension and level gate, each vehicle's band, a boat that waits for a group at work, the weather in every theme, the window's rows and Alt+C, the
  pinned run and the save in the middle of it.
