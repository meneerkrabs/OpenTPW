## Cheats, phase K4: a ride's vehicle limit (October 19, 2026)

- **More vehicles on a ride** ([CHEATS.md](../CHEATS.md#the-ride-vehicle-limit-k4)): the cheats window's last two rows pick one of the park's bumper car, kart or tour rides and raise its
  vehicle limit at the next park turn, to 32 cars or karts or 40 tour cars (the user's ceilings). The ride's capacity maximum grows to fill them (a bumper ride 8 → 32, CryptKarts 16 → 64, a
  tour 27 → 360), so the ride window's own slider reaches the new limit; the water rides are not offered, because their boats are already limited only by the module's 64.
- **The real scripts follow.** With the game data, the jungle bumper script launches 32 cars at capacity 32, and every theme's tour script asks for and flies 40 cars; on a test loop 32 karts
  race and all come home, the ones past the eight grid slots starting from the station.
- **Saves and the original format.** A raised ride writes its raised state in the world save (the ride, its bump controller's limit or its tour controller's 40 slots), checked on load to
  agree; every other save keeps its bytes. The original-format export refuses a park with a raised ride whose capacity is above its type's original maximum, because the original keeps a ride's capacity in a byte.
- **Determinism.** No draw; the raised state joins the hash only for a raised ride. A new pin, `CheatsBumperRaisedRunHash` = `0xDF2C86E99A8FB3C0`, runs a raised bumper ride's session
  with 32 cars.
- **Strings.** Four new UI strings (the ride row and the vehicle button) in all eleven languages, and the extension's description names the new cheat; the window's label-fit test covers
  the rows with every shipped bumper, kart and tour ride's name in every language.
