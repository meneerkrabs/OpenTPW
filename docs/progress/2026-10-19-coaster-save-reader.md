## Coaster save reader corrections (October 19, 2026)

Phase P0 of the original coaster import, from a static trace of tp.exe's coaster loader `0x437B60` and saver `0x437300`. Reader and docs only: no coaster is restored yet and no pin moved. `SaveCoaster.RidersOnBoard` is renamed `IntersectionCount` (controller `+0x140` is the track's self-intersection count, not a rider count), the header words gain named accessors (`TypeId`, `Slot`, `TrainsInService`, `SeatsPerTrain`), and the section "links" are now `CollisionOrdinals`: collision lists of chain positions, resolved by `0x43E520`, bounded by the native list's eight slots (the reader refuses more, where the original would overrun the list). SAVE-922 is narrowed to the two placement values `+28`/`+32`: only `+20` (the slot) reaches the slot allocator `0x468760`, and the three globals are its state. docs/TPWS-PAYLOAD.md now documents the loader end to end, including that it binds each block to the controller the RideSystem loader already made through the station's mesh id and that the original crashes when none matches (`0x437CD5`). The queue fields are corrected too: `SaveAttraction.BackOfQueueCell` (`mBackOfQueue`, a cell id, not a guest), `mBeenAdmitted` as the called-forward flag, `mQueuePos` as the sideshow-win flag in state 14 (`SaveGuest.SideshowWon`), and the unsaved join happiness `+0x20C` that the loader zeroes (`0x51861C`).
