## Torches for the evening extension (October 19, 2026)

A buildable torch in all four themes, the user's request of October 19, 2026 now that parks can be dark and the only light a player could build was the pool lamp. Following the user's four decisions ([EVENING.md](../EVENING.md#torches)): the torches are **part of the evening extension** (`opentpw.evening` 1.1.0 is now a simulation extension, recorded in a park save only while the park holds a torch, like the pools, umbrellas and winter extensions; `[EXT:evening-torches-save]`), they give **light and mood only** (no attraction value or bonus, never chosen by a guest, an idle script; their light is a lamp-table row on the flame), they come in **themed variants** (a bamboo Tiki Torch, a wrought-iron Pumpkin Torch, a metal Plasma Torch with a blue flame and a striped Candy Torch with a pink flame, OpenTPW's own Blender art from `tools/blender/opentpw_torches.py` and `tools/evening/build-content.sh`), and they are **cheap**: Theme Park World has no bench, so a torch costs 50, the price of its cheapest shipped scenery (space's Small Bush), with no upkeep and no research, in the Features tab with names in all eleven interface languages.

- `content/evening` (ids 63900 to 63903 in the reserved range 63900..63999, which nothing else used) is a content root of `opentpw.evening` while the extension is on, not in a Theme Park Inc run; the catalog cache key now names that root. MODS.md gained a table of OpenTPW's own id ranges.
- The lamp table has a new match key `mod` (the content root's id), so only the extension's own torches match the four torch rows (25 rows, 38 lights).
- A park without a torch saves byte-identically with the extension on or off; a torch records `opentpw.evening 1.1.0` and the placed torches' asset keys only, and such a save refuses to load with the extension off. Exporting a park with a torch is refused like one with a winter kiosk (packages record no mods yet).
- The browser harness's `PerfEvening("off")` used to take the extension out of the run's mod set, which a simulation extension cannot do; it now sets the run-scoped `Level.EveningLookOff`, which skips the cycle and the lamps and leaves the mod set (and the catalog) alone.
- The browser page embeds `content/evening` (347,862 bytes) with a `RequireExtensionContent` guard, and the server image copies the folder.
- `--debug-torches N` (Debug builds) places torches near the camera target for captures.

Verification: `EveningTorchTests` (10 tests), the updated `EveningTests` and `ModsScreenTests`, and the related content, save and catalog suites pass locally; the world-hash tests of the evening extension (on and off, no torch) are unchanged, and no determinism pin moved. Captures of each theme's torch at day, dusk and night stay outside the repository.
