## Guests lie still and along the sun lounger (October 19, 2026)

A bug report from the user: guests behaved strangely on the pools extension's sun loungers, lying the wrong way and moving all the time. Captures of the debug lounger (`--debug-extras`, which stands it at rotation 90 with its side to the pool) showed three faults, all fixed:

- **The wrong direction.** The lying guest faced the side it had climbed out on (`LoungerActivity`, step 3). The placement rule lets a lounger stand with its side or head end to the water, and a guest who came in from there lay across the seat over the backrest. It now lies along the lounger's own back direction (`LoungerActivity.LieDirection`: the feet towards the foot end, the head on the raised end, as the model has it), and is turned so on the turn it lies down, so the first lying turn no longer keeps the heading of the step onto the seat.
- **The constant movement.** The two lying frames are two resting poses, but the swimmers' renderer played every activity slot at six frames a second, so a lying guest flapped its arms for the whole lie. A pose is now held seven seconds (`PoolSwimSlots.ActivityStep`, `LyingPoseSeconds`); the other slots are unchanged.
- **Hidden by the lounger.** The lounger's raised head end and the front of its seat hid the head and the near side of the body. A lying guest is drawn pulled 0.75 engine units towards the camera (`PoolExtraSettings.LoungerDepthPull`, as the flume riders are).

Checked on screen in all four camera yaws (captures kept outside the repository, `OpenTPW-notes/pools-captures/lounger/before` and `after`). The heading fix changes the hashed heading for a guest that climbed out at a lounger's side or head end, and for every lounger guest on the one turn it lies down; nothing in the simulation reads the heading, the pinned extras run's lounger has its back to the pool, and no determinism pin moved. New tests in `PoolsExtrasTests`: the lie direction, the still point and the cushion height for every rotation, the shipped models' head end, the held pose and the depth pull. Open: the kid art is longer than the seat, so the feet reach past the cushion's end.
