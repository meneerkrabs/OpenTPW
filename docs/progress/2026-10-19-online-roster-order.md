## Chat rosters arrive in the order a room changed (October 19, 2026)

- **Root cause of the online test flakes.** `SessionWalksAChatParkAndReportsBuddyEntries` (Ubuntu) and `ADroppedBuddyAskKeepsTheBuddyList` (macOS) failed now and then on CI and passed on a re-run. The chat server handles each connection's requests on that connection's own thread, and `ChatHub.EnterRoom` took a room's roster and queued it without holding anything. When two players entered a room together, one thread could take its roster before the other player arrived and queue it after the other thread's newer roster, so a player ended with a roster that left out someone standing in the room. A visit only draws players named in the roster, so the two visitors never saw each other (the macOS failure), and Bob's roster of the chat park lacked Ann although her steps reached him (the Ubuntu failure).
- **Fix.** The hub now holds one lock while a player connects, enters a room or leaves, and while a roster for `/who` is taken and queued. Every outbox therefore receives a room's rosters in the order its membership changed. Each change builds its roster once and sends that one to everyone in the room, so the hub-wide lock does not serialize one roster per recipient. Buddy entry notices are sent after the lock is released.
- **Regression test.** `OnlineServerTests.PlayersJoiningARoomTogetherAllEndWithTheFullRoster` has twelve players join the same room at once, round after round, and fences each round with two `hearing` replies instead of sleeps. While players only arrive, each player's rosters must grow and the last one must name everyone. Without the lock it failed 5 runs out of 5, within the first six rounds; with it, it passes.
- **Waits.** Every wait in `OnlineIntegrationTests` (and the chat reads of `OnlineServerTests`) now uses one shared 60-second deadline instead of 5 to 20 seconds. Waits still return as soon as their condition holds, and no assertion changed. Fixed observation windows that check something does not happen stay as they were. Each test already ran its own loopback server on an ephemeral port.
