## Original saves keep their guests off the paths (October 19, 2026)

Known-gaps task S5. The import of an original save used to drop a park guest (states 6–10) whose cell was not a path. tp.exe does nothing special for such a guest on load: the guest serializer `0x4FB530` reads its position and state straight in, and the person loader routes a walking guest again to its saved accurate destination (`0x4FA5F0` at `0x4F9407`), a walk search that starts from any cell. The guest's own state handlers then recover it: a stuck walk falls back to deciding, and the wander `0x4F9490` searches a guest without links back onto a path. OpenTPW now imports such a guest where it stands, routes states 7, 9 and 10 again with the original walk set-up (no draw at load; a failed search leaves the walk stuck and is reported as "could not renav"), imports the stranded mark as 0 (the route counter it is compared with is not saved and restarts with the process) and takes the walk mask from the saved navigation state's nav_mode (reader `0x50D150`, called at `0x4F925E` with ECX = person + 0xD4). A state-10 guest now also walks on to its saved accurate destination at the import, like every person in tp.exe's loader, instead of asking for the queue's back cell on its first update; the walk mask of every imported guest, a leaving one on a path included, is the saved one. SAVE-002 no longer covers park guests off the paths. The eleven corpus saves hold no such guest and their nav_mode values equal the masks OpenTPW gave before, so no pin moved.
