## Original saves: the Sound block's volumes play for the visit (October 19, 2026)

The user's decision (2026-10-19): a park loaded from a player's original save takes the save's volumes, as tp.exe does.

- **What tp.exe does.** The payload loader calls the Sound block loader `0x51C400` at `0x415BA2`; it copies the saved 84 bytes back into the sound-service globals `0x803AA0`, and with the saved dirty word set the next sound tick `0x51BD70` pushes the master (`+0x18`), effects (`+0x1C`), music (`+0x20`) and speech (`+0x24`) percents and the three on-flags to the mixer. The profile options (`0x78D8E8`) are a separate struct that the load does not touch and that the profile file is written from (`0x423E90`), so the options screen keeps showing the player's values. The options take over again per channel when a slider or toggle calls that channel's setter (`0x4A2BF0`), for every channel on OK (`0x4237F0` → `0x423DD0`) and at park exit (state 12 of `0x54E360` writes the profile through `0x5C8650`; the serializer ends in `0x423DD0`). The master volume has no option (start-up sets 100 at `0x54DE31`), so a save's master holds for the process.
- **OpenTPW.** `GameAudio.ApplySavedSound` / `SavedSoundVolumes` apply a player's save's values after park entry and report them in the import evidence; `ApplyOptions` plays them until the player's option for that channel changes, OK on the options screen, or leaving the park. `GameOptions` and `opentpw-options.json` are never changed.
- **Typed view.** `SaveSoundSettings` (`SaveSoundData.SettingsView`) names the 21 words; `OriginalParkImport.Sound` reads the block without ever refusing a save for it. The "QMixer chunk" is now described as what it is: tp.exe's own sound library's voice list behind three listener floats.
- **Fidelity.** SAVE-010 is narrowed (the volumes are applied; the flags, handles, crowd, rain, reverb, dirty/duck/freeze words and the voice list stay `[EXT:original-engine-state-dumps]`). New `[APPROX:SAVE-016]`: a park started from the shipped `Easymode.TPWI` (Instant Action, the reference start) keeps the options' volumes, although the original's park entry `0x5ACCF0` loads the park's newest save, so its default volumes would play for the visit.
- **Audible change.** None with the corpus: all twelve files hold the defaults (master 100, 75/60/75, all on), so with default options nothing changes. No RNG draw and no simulation state; no determinism pin moves.
