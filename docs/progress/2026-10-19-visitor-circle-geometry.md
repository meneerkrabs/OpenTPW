## Bounded ordinary visitor circle picking (October 19, 2026)

The [UI-930 implementation](../proposals/ui-930-match-visitor-sprite-pick-geometry.md) uses the source-backed strict circle only for registered ordinary kid/costume bodies with bounded, non-reconstructed scale-one history and exact canonical camera matrices. It preserves current renderer-selected frames, projected-Z depth, synchronous timing, ID ties, swimmer precedence, post-winner neighborhood rejection, HUD ownership and unsupported-source rectangle fallback. An eligible circle miss cannot retry the rectangle. Shared world sprite sizing is unchanged.

Added synthetic production-query, HUD, nonmutation and geometry tests, including actual host projection/rotated-view adapters, finite/overflow validation and fractional drawable-pointer quantization. Local source/arithmetic and documentation checks do not execute C#: the SDK and rendered-view validation remain unavailable in the authoring environment. Required runtime CI and independent review remain gates; UI-930 stays open for frame/source coverage, native result timing/order, cross-kind competition, clipping, rounding and rendered-view residuals. The original October 18 proposal progress entry is preserved.
