## Always winter (October 19, 2026)

A new option of the winter family, `opentpw.winter-always` ("Always winter", `[EXT:winter-always]`), off by default, user-approved on October 19, 2026 as the variant "Winterweer hele jaar". Every month rolls its weather from December's season row (tp.exe's roll `0x512e10` takes the row from the month through `0x4f8770`; December is the row past the table at `0x7854c4`, its average the count slot at `0x7854f4`) and every roll is wintry, so the rain and thunderstorms of the jungle and fantasy parks are snow without lightning all year, as in a wintry December, and the Halloween park snows by its own band all year. Space has neither a rain nor a snow band, so its weather is left alone. The forecast follows the option. The winter kiosk and the glühwein stall sell at their winter demand all year (`WinterRules.DemandMonthOf`), and the snow cover settles whenever it snows.

The option implies Wintry Decembers with no double effect, is recorded in a save while it is on (a save made with it is refused with it off and the other way round; older saves load unchanged) and has its row, with its description in the eleven interface languages, on the Extensions and mods screen beside the other winter options. With it off every existing pin stands. New pins: `WinterAlwaysTests.JungleSeed21AlwaysWinterHash` = `0x71A3DF8635ACF9B9` (the jungle's weather, 378 snowy days of 400) and `DeterminismTests.WinterAlwaysFixedRunHash` = `0xAD0FF92A6B42F187` (a brolly shop, the winter shops and the rain mood under the jungle's real weather from May to mid-October). Details in docs/WINTER.md ("Always winter") and docs/DETERMINISM.md ("Always winter").

Review changes: in Halloween, where every snowfall has lightning, the forecast and the current-weather banner now say snow instead of thunderstorms while the option is on (presentation only; the weather's flags, the umbrellas and the rain mood are unchanged); space parks keep their month-by-month winter shop demand, as they keep their weather; WINTER.md mentions Halloween's occasional rain.
