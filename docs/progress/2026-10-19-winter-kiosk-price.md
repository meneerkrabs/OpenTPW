## Winter kiosk price checked in a real park (October 19, 2026)

A review of the kiosk's return rule (#663) reported that the shipped winter kiosk (price 25, cost of goods 10, happiness effect 15) could never sell, because the price test's willingness to pay peaks at about 22 for those terms, and that the measurement harness had run the shops without price terms (the price test read a price of 0). Measured in the level gate's park of every theme with game data, the report does not reproduce: the price test also multiplies by the per-type word `UsageInfo.RipOffOK`, which every winter shop inherits as 100 from its theme's `Shops.sam`, so the kiosk's willingness is 24 to 44 and only a guest below happiness 5 is refused. In snowy January parks the kiosk sold 18 to 31 times and the glühwein stall 15 to 23 times per three seeds with no price refusal, as at the game's own drinks shop. The report's 0 sales are the case without the word, which the headless harness now reproduces (0 kiosk sales, 62 refusals). No price, effect or .sam byte changed (a .sam change would break saves with a placed kiosk), and no pin moved.

The headless harness now runs the kiosk and the stall with their shipped price terms (`WinterTuningMeasureTests.ShippedPrices`, `SyntheticPark`'s `winterPrices`), and `WinterPriceMeasureTests` checks with game data that every theme's merged terms equal the harness's and that the shops sell; the level gate gained `M3GateOptions.ExtraObjects` for such measurements. The return rule now also requires that the guest could pay the kiosk's price (`QueueJoinRules.TooExpensive`, the pure test), so a guest with too little money is no longer drawn back to be refused again. The tuning tables in docs/WINTER.md were measured again with the price terms, and the broken "Share dressed" bullet was restored.
