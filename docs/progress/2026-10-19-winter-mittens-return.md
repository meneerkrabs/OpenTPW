## Guests come back for the second winter item (October 19, 2026)

[EXT:winter-kiosk-return], user-approved on October 19, 2026 ("Laten terugkomen"). The W6 measurement found that nobody bought a second winter item in about 1,740 guests: a guest who bought a hat or earmuffs never came back for mittens. Measured cause: right after a purchase the kiosk is the thing the guest used last, which the game's score rates 0 outright (same type as the last used), and afterwards it stays in the used history, which divides its score by 5, 4, 3 or 2; either rule alone leaves the other one in place (13 complete outfits in a snowy January only when both are skipped, 0 or 1 otherwise). While a guest's slots are not full, the winter kiosk now does not count as recently used for that guest (`GuestSimulation.WinterScoreOf`); the temporary history, other attractions, the umbrellas and the balloon rules are untouched, and a guest with full slots still scores the kiosk 0.

Measured over 17 snowy January days (three seeds): complete outfits 0 of 146 admitted guests before, 13 of 144 after (9.0 %); the kiosk sells 44 to 48 times and fewer guests hold an item (42 to 35), since a returning guest takes a turn at the kiosk. `WinterKioskReturnTests` cover the score and `WinterTuningMeasureTests` the before and after.

Pins: `WinterFixedRunHash` `0xD4E2340C99394B73` -> `0xA66540FF8C2AC6DE`, `WinterAlwaysFixedRunHash` `0xAD0FF92A6B42F187` -> `0x5F767F69EF5CBF07` and `WinterColdEverythingFixedRunHash` `0xCCEA2E164DF6888A` -> `0x45FCB3FC30C601FC` (all three are parks with a winter kiosk; cause in DETERMINISM.md). No other pin moved.

Docs and tools: docs/WINTER.md "Share dressed" has the new numbers (a complete outfit is no longer rare), and the kiosk decision is listed under Decisions. The browser scenario `winterCost` now names its windows honestly (every window runs in rain, so the first is `rain`, not "bare"), measures the umbrellas and the warm items in separate steps (the items cost about +1.7 ms for roughly 760 items), records whether it rained and what was drawn, and takes its screenshot of a fresh dressed crowd in the open (`--follow`) instead of the one under the entrance canopy. `WinterTuningMeasureTests`: the cold test now asserts that dressed guests lose less than undressed ones, the holding column is named for what it counts, and the duplicate weather helper is gone.

Mittens: the palm of the redesigned mitten is widened from radius 0.22 to 0.24 in `tools/blender/opentpw_winter_items.py` (a few skin pixels showed at the mitten edges at zoom 110); `SPR_WI.TPC` is rebuilt with `tools/winter/build-content.sh` (a second run is byte-identical, ESP and anchors unchanged).
