## The winter mittens look like mittens (October 19, 2026)

At the default zoom the winter mittens read as small cups: a round blob with a pale rim on top, held at the hand. `tools/blender/opentpw_winter_items.py` now builds a short, elongated knitted mitten that continues the forearm line (a rounded tip, a palm that is widest a third up, a narrower wrist), a thumb bump on the inner side and a ribbed knit cuff in the same wool tone as the body (the contrasting rim is gone; the cuff is closed on top because the forearm is drawn under it). The colourways (red, blue, green) are unchanged. The slot layout, the hotspot (the hand end point), the frame order (frame 0 the right mitten, frame 1 the left) and the sizes (about 13 x 18 pixels at 2x) are compatible with `WarmItemRenderer`, so no code or anchor changed. `tools/winter/build-content.sh` regenerates `content/winter/esprites/Winter/SPR_WI.TPC` byte-identically on a second run. Presentation only: no pin, save or simulation change.
