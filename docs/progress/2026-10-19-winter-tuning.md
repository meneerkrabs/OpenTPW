## Winter tuning from captures and measurements (October 19, 2026)

Phase W6 of the winter extension. With snow, the kiosk and the stall, dressed guests, umbrellas, balloons and costumes running together, the Halloween park and the jungle with wintry Decembers were captured in day, dusk and night, near and far, with pool guest art on and off. The captures showed no fault that needed a model, anchor, draw-order or shader change (kiosk and stall scale and ground contact, hat and earmuff placement on both art sets, umbrella over hat over guest, no green specks through full snow cover, slush paths, items dimming with their wearers at night), so nothing in the shipped art or code moved and no pin changed. Sheltered-ground masking was judged not cheap and stays open.

New measurement aids: `WinterTuningMeasureTests` (headless, fixed seeds, nothing pinned; `OPENTPW_WINTER_MEASURE=<file>` writes the tables) and the browser harness scenario `winterCost` (`tools/web-perf/run.mjs --scenarios jungle,winterCost`). Findings, all recorded in docs/WINTER.md ("Tuning"): the kiosk sells 0 times in a clear July at the shipped summer percentage of 20 (the user decided to keep the shipped 20, "20% laten"; no data or pin change); snow does not lift the kiosk in the winter months because the stall's 250 % outscores it; the cold rule costs a bare guest 0.7 mood points and a dressed one about 0.1 over a snowy Halloween week; settled snow costs nothing measurable in the browser and 400 guests with umbrellas and about 830 warm items cost 3.6 ms a frame more at 59.5 fps.
