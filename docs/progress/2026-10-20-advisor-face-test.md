## Advisor drawn with the original's face test (October 20, 2026)

The advisor was drawn double-sided on the strength of tp.exe's only Direct3D cull-mode write (`D3DCULL_NONE`, `0x566957`). The engine culls in software before Direct3D, though: every mesh is queued by the submit `0x581CD0` (its only caller is `0x57A530`) into `[0x8BCBB8]`, which the scene draw flushes (`0x576A00` → `0x582170` → `0x581DB0`), and the primitive builder `0x580530` keeps a triangle only when it runs clockwise on the screen (`0x581320`, the determinant of its corners' x, y and w). The advisor's material now culls back faces with clockwise front faces, as the park's objects do. `AdvisorTests.TheOriginalsFaceTestKeepsTheAdvisorsFacesTowardsTheViewer` checks all five advisor models at rest: 2,468 of 3,890 triangles are kept, no mesh is drawn inside out, and 90 % agree with their stored (smoothed) normals. docs/LIPS.md is corrected. Captures before and after are in the notes folder (`winter-captures/advisor-face-test`); the advisor looks the same, as its hidden sides were covered anyway. Presentation only: no determinism pin moved.
