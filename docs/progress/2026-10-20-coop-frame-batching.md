## Co-op: the park after a tick no longer depends on how ticks fall into frames (October 20, 2026)

The co-op lockstep needs every player's park after a tick to depend only on the ticks and the commands ([COOP.md](../COOP.md); phase C1b). Until now a few things a frame did outside the fixed tick could change the park, so a replay matched live play only at one tick a frame. Each of them is now audited ([DETERMINISM.md](../DETERMINISM.md), "Frame batching"):

- The pools' drawing, the evening, the snow's easing, the scene clock and the update of every entity are presentation: they write nothing the state hash covers, the simulation reads or a save keeps for the simulation. A test lists the entity types that have an update, so a new one must be audited.
- The ride bodies' pose phase, which asks the guests' body players for templates and creates and deletes bodies, ran once per frame after the frame's ticks. It now runs at the end of every tick. At one tick a frame (60 Hz at normal speed) nothing changes; at faster speeds a body request lands at its tick instead of at the frame's end.
- The staff host's bookkeeping (a member left picked up with no hand goes back to idle, a member without a position is put on the gate path) ran at the start of every frame and changes hashed staff state. It now runs at the start of every tick, after the tick's commands. The HUD's staff locator only reads the park now.

The level's frame step is one method (`Level.AdvanceFrame`), which the new `FrameBatchingTests` drive: a scripted park without game data gives the same world hash after every tick, the same command log and the same save in frames of 1 tick, 16 ticks and seeded random sizes, with its edits issued live between frames or replayed inside them. With game data, the park-resume smoke's restore phase now continues the saved Jungle park in seeded random frames with the continuation's edits replayed inside them, and must still match the capture phase tick for tick. No pin moved.
