## Cross-platform coaster pins (October 20, 2026)

Coaster content is now pinned on every runtime. `DetMatrix` (`source/OpenTPW/Utils/DetMatrix.cs`) holds plain-arithmetic versions of the matrix product, the point transform, the lerp and the slerp (with DetMath's acos and sin). The station frame of a placed station model (`CoasterStationLayout.FromModel`) composes the dummy's parent chain (`ObjectAnimator.LocalTransforms`) and the placement with it instead of System.Numerics' possibly fused product, and the pylon rig blends its clip keys with it instead of `Vector3.Lerp` and the platform's acos and sin. The imported coaster's pin from #702 is back (`0x8A1279434220868B`, moved by the coaster hash's two new fields), and two built-coaster runs are pinned, one with a reopen and its catch-up tick. `CrossRuntimeMathProbe` now also reports the System.Numerics matrix product, `Vector3.Transform` and `Vector3.Lerp` and `DetMatrix`'s; `DetMathTests` pins `DetMatrix`'s everywhere; the fused System.Numerics values differ under the browser's Mono AOT build, so that comparison is informative there. The browser pin check now also runs `OriginalCoasterImportTests`.
