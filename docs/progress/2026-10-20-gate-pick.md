## The park gates cannot be clicked, in the original either (October 20, 2026)

After the object-pick fix (#680) the question was whether the original lets a player hover or click the park gates. It does not, and OpenTPW already matches: docs/UI.md now records the evidence next to the UI-027 retirement. A gate is a type-3 thing of kind 4, for which the window dispatcher `0x486920` opens nothing (`0x486948`); and no cell names it, because world setup creates it through `0x4db090` without stamping cells, the stamping `0x528a70` runs only from the build tools, and the world init types a level's cells from its MAP bytes as 0, 1, 2, 7 or 30 only. In the shipped Jungle save the gate's record stands at cell (0, 0) and no owner word names that cell. Docs only: no code, pin or save change.
