## Buy preview drawn with a depth buffer and the original's camera (October 20, 2026)

The buy window's turning preview (`PreviewIcon`) was a painter-sorted CPU projection: on the Orbiter, the jungle and Halloween puzzle sideshows, the Halloween Tour ride, the Fruit Shy and others, faces drew over the faces in front of them at some turns. It is now projected with tp.exe's own steps and drawn with a depth buffer. The projection was traced in tp.exe: the preview object has its own view, an identity matrix flipped in y (`0x45bd30`, `0x57a260`), used instead of the park camera for model flag `0x200` (`0x57a750`); the viewport maps it straight to the screen with the depth × 0.1 and no perspective divide (`0x579690` mode 2, `0x582f70`); the matrix `0x468e50` rebuilds each frame now has its offset terms ported (the footprint centre placed in the frame, minus `0.35 · share · reach`); and the primitive builder keeps only the triangles that run clockwise on the screen (`0x581320` in `0x580530`), which is why the Direct3D cull mode can stay `D3DCULL_NONE`. Depth is LESSEQUAL (the device init `0x573080` sets ZFUNC with the depth test, `0x57360f`). Each new preview also plays the model's animation 5 (`<model>m1`, else `<model>m`, looping; `0x4732a0` from `0x4689f0`).

The kept triangles are drawn into an image of their own (`UiDepthImage`, `UiRenderer.PrepareDepthImages`, the new `ui-depth.shader` with its WebGL2 copy), which the interface draws where the preview stands; the object windows use the same image path with their own 30° view. UI-026 is narrowed to the preview's lighting (`0x57aa10`, drawn unlit here) and the footprint cells' pixel edges; `[EXT:preview-icon-cpu-projection]` now names only the CPU projection and the separate image. No new approximation.

Measured by the new `PreviewDepthOrderTests` over all 346 preview models (274 original, 72 from the extensions) at 16 turns: the drawn image shows the same face as a depth-tested reference at every one of 53.5 million covered pixels; the old painter's order showed the wrong face on 3.2 % of them, in 332 models. The original's face test would drop the downward-facing `PURPLE_BASE` ground plates of the Space Racers and The Blobulator previews; at the user's decision ("Grondplaten toch tonen (EXT)") they are drawn anyway (`[EXT:preview-ground-plates]`), and every other triangle keeps the original's test. The winter kiosk's roof shows its tiles at every turn. `--debug-hud buyitem:` now makes a type still in the lab available first, so every preview can be captured. Captures before and after are in the notes folder (`winter-captures/preview-depth`). Presentation only: no determinism pin moved.
