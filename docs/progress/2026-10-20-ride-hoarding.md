## Ride hoarding: closed, broken down, condemned and upgrading rides are boarded up (October 20, 2026)

Ports tp.exe's object model state and its fade: the boards that rise around a closed ride and sink when it opens. It was neither ported nor registered. Presentation only: nothing in the simulation or a state hash reads it, and no pin moves.

- **Variants.** The model call `0x454550` picks one of four texture sets of the level's `MiscMesh\textures` (Closed, Hoarding, Condemn, Upgrade; `.wct` in Theme Park World, `.fsh` in Theme Park Inc): mode 1 the closed set (unless another set stands and is not sinking), 2 the broken-down set, 4 the condemned set, 8 the upgrade set. It sets the rise rate 0.2 per second and the thing flags standing and rising; the opening's call `0x4547C0` makes a standing hoarding sink at 0.3 per second. The frame update `0x4548F0` moves the level 0..1 every frame by the frame's real time, stops a risen hoarding and takes a sunk one out of the scene, back to the closed set.
- **Boards.** The builder `0x452E70` makes one board per open edge of the type's `Info.Hoarding` grid (the map symbols of `0x402720`), sorted round the grid by angle, corners pulled towards the ground node's vertices, both faces drawn; `0x454190` sets each board's bottom on the terrain and its top 10 above; the rise `0x4543C0` raises the boards in a staggered wave (even boards to the full height, odd ones to 0.8). All 129 shipped types with `Info.Hoarding` get one.
- **Calls.** Every closing the original pairs with the model call makes it: a new ride that starts closed, the player's close, a breakdown (broken-down set, or condemned for a worn-out ride), a blocked exit, a repair that recloses and an upgrade (upgrade set); every opening sinks it. Loads and snapshots keep the flags and level (the RideSystem record's packed bits `0x4647A0`, then the restore `0x4547F0`), and a restore makes no model call.
- **Presentation, also when saved.** A loaded level outside 0..1 is held to it, so the snapshot stays valid; the park-resume smoke compares the runtimes without the hoarding, whose level follows real frame time (docs/DETERMINISM.md). A level builds each type's boards and loads its four textures once (`ObjectHoardingCache`).
- **QUEUE-023** is narrowed: the opening's model call is made; what stays open is the unhooked re-evaluation sites.
- Documented in `docs/OBJECTS.md`, "Hoarding". Unit tests cover the map parser, the board order, the corner pulls, the rise and the state machine; with-data tests pin the totem's boards and every shipped type's board count, and the textures of both games.
