## Co-op: a restart never cuts a guest off for its queue (October 21, 2026)

A server restart stores every shared park's frame position and then closes the co-op sockets after their queues. The host's socket closes first, and the news that the host went is sent to its guests; that message could cut off a guest whose queue stood at its limit, dropping the frames whose position the restart had just stored. Every queue now stops taking messages before any closes, a closing queue is never cut off for its size, and a hello or a request that comes during the stop is turned away (the game resumes on the new server), so the stored positions and memberships stay the last. The test that restarts the server while a host streams frames also failed now and then on the macOS runners, when a slow guest was rightly cut off for slowness before the restart; its guest's queue now takes the whole burst.
