## Arrival vehicles and the finale's End get their economy record when they are made (October 21, 2026)

The bus, seaplane and Ferry are placed at their first trip, and the Instant Action finale's End when the research is complete; neither path gave the object an economy record, though the fixed items at the park's start get one. The original builds all of them with the common object constructor (tp.exe `0x004DB090`, called at `0x51A57E` and `0x516B8B`), which counts the type and stamps its first-purchase turn, and every shipped TPWS saves an attraction record for the Bus, Seaplane and Ferry. So a single-player save of a park whose vehicle had come logged a repair warning and registered the record only then, and in a co-op session the host could neither save the park nor capture it for a join once a vehicle had come ("Seaplane (attraction 24) has no economy binding, which a shared park cannot repair"); the co-op join smoke found it. A fixed item made in play now gets its uncharged record at once (`Level.BindFixedItemMadeInPlay`). Two new tests make a vehicle through the level's own wiring and complete the research, and check the binding, the uncharged record, the type count and that a save finds nothing to repair.
