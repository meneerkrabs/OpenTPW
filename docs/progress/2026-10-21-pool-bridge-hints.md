## Pool bridge placement hints (October 21, 2026)

A player could not find a spot that the pool bridge accepted. The tool gave no preview, and several refusals only said "you cannot build here". The bridge tool now previews the whole bridge on the blueprint: both outside path ends and the seven bridge cells, blue when the bridge can be built. When it cannot, the cell to blame turns red, and while the line reaches pool water the hover help names the reason. The new reasons are an outside end without a path, water where an approach cell's land must be (the pool is wider than three cells there, or the pointer is not on the first land cell), something in the way (a ride, shop, scenery, slide, queue, track, fence post or protected cell), another bridge, and land the park does not own. Each has its own message in all eleven languages, as do the flat-land rule and the existing refusals; a click on a refused place posts the same message. `PoolBridgeResult` gains `EndNotPath`, `LandIsWater`, `Blocked` and `BridgeInTheWay`, appended after the existing values. The placement rules themselves, saves, hashes and the co-op command funnel are unchanged.
