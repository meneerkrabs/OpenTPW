## An original save's advisor counts from the park's saved construction and hire stamps (October 22, 2026)

The advisor's days since the newest construction and the newest hire (tp.exe `0x4C9070` and `0x4C90D0`, read by the scan callbacks of the messages 7, 11, 13, 60, 71, 73-75, 77-79 and 81) count from the park's `mLastObjectBuilt` (`+0x21C30`) and `mLastStaffHired` (`+0x21C38`). For a park from an original save OpenTPW derived both at attach from the newest standing object and the newest employed member, because the save's words were not read. They are now read and used.

- **The words.** The analyser serializer `0x4C5D70` writes six raw 8-byte stamps (`0x4CEDE0`) after the counters `mMisbehavingKids` to `mLifetimeVisitors` and before `mMostPaidForTicket`, `mLargestBusLoad` and `mLongestStay`: `mLastStrike`, `mLastPuke`, `mLastPrank`, `mParkLastOpened`, `mLastObjectBuilt` (`0x4C6B9A`) and `mLastStaffHired` (`0x4C6BC4`). Its load reads them back in the same order (`0x510240`, `0x4C7289`, `0x4C72B3`). The field names come from the serializer's strings. `SaveAnalyserStamps` reads them at body bytes 73,476 to 73,523, the 48 bytes of the analyser's `16 + 48 + 12` tail, and `OriginalParkImport.AnalyserStamps` carries them. The reader refuses a byte ring before them whose capacity is not 50, since `0x4CEA00` writes as many entries as a ring's `mNumEntries` holds and another size would move the stamps.
- **Nothing restamps them on a load.** The loader `0x5179C0` zeroes the six (`0x518A43`-`0x518A85`) and runs the serializer's load (`0x518ABA`, mode 0). The analyser constructor `0x4C58C0`, which stamps a new park's time, has its only caller in the new-world setup `0x5156A0`. The loader builds its staff through `0x518E20` and the role serializers, not the staff init `0x504B90`, the only caller of the hire stamp `0x4C76D0`, and it builds no object through `0x4DB090`, whose exit `0x4DB7A3` is the only caller of the object stamp `0x4C7680`. REA's callers agree with a byte scan for call instructions, and with a scan for the stamps' displacements, which finds no other access to `+0x21C30` or `+0x21C38`.
- **Turns.** A stamp is the park time `0x4F8690`: the calendar's `FunnyTimeStart` plus floor(turn x rate / 4) x 10^7 in 100 ns units (`0x5FC530`). `OriginalEconomyImport.AnalyserStampTurns` takes both stamps as the turns of the save's calendar, its start and its rate, whose park time they are. `AutomaticAdvisor.AttachLevel` takes them for a park from an original save, a player's own or a shipped seed. Only the calendar constructor `0x4F7E80` and the loader write the start and the rate (15000), so every stamp an original save holds is a whole turn at or before the park's turn, and for those the callbacks' days are exactly tp.exe's, (now - stamp) / 864,000,000,000. A modified save can hold other words. A stamp between two turns' times is taken at the earlier turn and reported in the import evidence; that can move the count by the day its seconds cross. A stamp before the calendar's start or past the park's turn keeps its turn, and the conversion cannot overflow for any saved word. The days since a stamp are now signed as `__alldiv` divides them, so a stamp still to come counts negative days where OpenTPW counted 0. The advisor snapshot of an OpenTPW save still replaces the stamps after the attach, so a resumed park keeps its own. Snapshot validation now refuses a snapshot that holds only one of the two stamps; the capture always writes both. Only an OpenTPW save from before the snapshot kept them, and a save that names no analyser, still derive them. ADVISOR-009, SAVE-010, `docs/reverse/ADVISOR-SCAN.md`, `docs/TPWS-PAYLOAD.md`, `docs/SAVE-THINGS.md` and the producers' `firstStamps` tag say so.
- **The corpus.** In all eleven private saves the six stamps lie whole turns from the calendar's start, at or before the save's turn. The four restart saves stamp turn 0. In the seven played saves `mLastObjectBuilt` lies at turns 1,169 to 1,777 and `mLastStaffHired` at 2,609 to 3,702. The derivation gave the hire stamp turn 0 in all of them, because no member is employed, and the object stamp 3 to 21 turns off. A side finding behind those object turns: the attraction stamp reader takes the day of the week as the hour. tp.exe's serializer writes `FileTimeToSystemTime`'s words in the order year, month, day, day of the week, hour, minute, second and milliseconds, and its loader skips the fourth (`0x4DC8A1`). With that order every save's newest attraction time equals its `mLastObjectBuilt` to the minute. The fix is left to its own change.

Tests: `AdvisorSavedStampsTests` checks the stamps' offset with independent arithmetic against the analyser body, the serializer's order slot by slot, and the refusals, a resized byte ring among them. It checks the import from two calendar starts and three other rates, a stamp between turns (also one whose seconds cross a day), stamps before the calendar's start and past the park's turn with their signed days, and the extreme words. It also checks a save that names no analyser, an advisor that takes the saved stamps over a park whose newest construction no longer stands and counts the original's days from them, the snapshot's priority, a snapshot with only one stamp, and the derivation of a resumed park. A corpus case, inconclusive without `OPENTPW_TPWS_SAVES`, checks the eleven saves through the economy import. An independent Codex review found nothing blocking; its three should-fix points (the byte-ring capacities, the calendar's own rate, the half-recorded snapshot) and its wording nit are in.
