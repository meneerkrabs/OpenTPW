## A joined park's objects no longer stall the page on a type's first use (October 23, 2026)

A guest's build of a shared park now reads what each saved type's first object reads in steps of its own, before it places the objects ([COOP.md](../COOP.md), "The game's join" and "Measurements"; `[EXT:online-coop-snapshot-join]`). This is the fifth part of C2c-2b-3.

- The build reads every type's model first, with its sign when its parts show one; then its clips, its textures and its hoarding boards, a step each. In the page, which has no sign worker, a type's sign is composed over the composition's own steps and handed to its first object as the desktop's worker hands over its signs: the same pixels, its log written when the object uses it.
- The restore's checks are a step of their own, and the layout's checks format a message only when one fails. The cell map's checks had made about ten strings for each of its 9,642 records, three times a join.
- In the page compiled ahead of time the restore's first object takes 12 ms instead of 94 with the checks, a type's first object at most 21 ms instead of 62, and the validation stage's longest step 7 ms instead of 27. No frame of the objects' part of the build reaches 30 ms.
- The measuring join smoke now records every build frame of 30 ms or more with what it was made of, so each long step can be named.
