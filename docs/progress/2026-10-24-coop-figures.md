## Co-builders walk as figures in a shared park (October 24, 2026)

In a shared park every player now walks as a figure with their name over their head, as the players of a visited park do ([COOP.md](../COOP.md), "Co-builders as figures"; `[EXT:online-coop-figures]`). This is C2d-4, decision 8 of the shared-park plan.

- Your own figure follows your view: it walks to where you look, and jumps there when your view jumps (a locate, a map click). In the free walk it follows you; in the ride view it is hidden for the others until the ride ends.
- The others see your figure and name, and you see theirs. A figure goes round what the host builds on its way, and through what the host opens.
- A shared park starts in its HUD. F4 or Enter switches to the chat mode, which builds nothing: entering it ends any tool you had armed.
- In the chat's name popup, Go to moves your view to that player's figure, instead of telling you to leave the park first.
- Figures move only once the chat is in the shared park's room, and a shared park's figures do not sparkle, so nothing local reaches the park the players share.
