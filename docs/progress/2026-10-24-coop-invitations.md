## Invitations and the open list of shared parks (October 24, 2026)

A host's invitation now reaches a player anywhere in the lobby, and the lobby can list the parks shared with everyone ([COOP.md](../COOP.md), "Invitations and the open list"; `[EXT:online-coop-session]`). This is C2d-3b, the second part of joining from the game's own screens.

- While you are logged in and in the lobby, the game keeps its link to shared parks open, so a host's invitation can reach you on any lobby screen. In your own park the link is closed, and a host who invites you is told to give you the code instead.
- An invitation shows as a dialog: Join joins the host's park, Not now puts it aside. One shows at a time; others wait for up to a minute, and a logout or leaving the lobby forgets them.
- The hub's new Find a shared park line opens the open list: the parks their hosts share with everyone, with their hosts and players, and a Refresh button. A park that ended or filled after the list came is refused in words.
- The game never takes the link from another of your games on its own, and never ends a park that another of your games shares. On the join screen, Use this game takes the link over, after it says that the other game then leaves its shared park.
- A game that cannot join a shared park says so before anything is sent, and opens no connection for it.
- A link lost for long retries now and then while you stay in the lobby, and every game's reconnects are spread out, so a server restart does not bring every game back at the same moment.
- All new texts are in eleven languages.
