## Sharing a saved park from the lobby (October 24, 2026)

The lobby's Escape menu now has a Build Together line that shares one of the player's own saved parks with other players ([COOP.md](../COOP.md), "Sharing a saved park from the lobby"; `[EXT:online-coop-session]`). This is C2d-2b, the second half of hosting from the game's own co-op screens.

- The screen lists the player's own saved parks as the Load Park screen does. The sandbox and the shipped parks are not offered. The same choices as in the park follow: the title, whether the park is on the open list, and how many players it takes.
- Share loads the chosen park, asking about its extensions first when they differ, and shares it once it is the open park. A failed load shares nothing and leaves the lobby as it was.
- The park's Build Together screen then opens over the park, with the code once the park is shared, or with why it could not be shared and the player's choices kept.
- The screen's new introduction is in all eleven languages.
