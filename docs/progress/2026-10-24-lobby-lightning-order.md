## The lobby's lightning flash is drawn under the bolts and the rain (October 24, 2026)

Part of #829 (Halloween island flicker). On a strike the front end inverts the picture for one frame; the bolt and the rain now stay bright on that frame, as in the original.

- **Original:** the lightning draw (`0x57ee30`, reached from the rain render `0x57ff60`, called by the scene `0x576a00` at `0x576dab`) draws the full-screen quad first (`0x57ed50`, state `0x9A1`: source blend INVDESTCOLOR, destination ZERO), then the bolts (`0x57ee4d`); the raindrops follow (after `0x57ffec`). Call order alone does not decide the picture: the scene queues its meshes (`[0x8BCBB8]`, submitted via `0x581cd0`) and flushes them at `0x576dbe` -> `0x582170` -> `0x581db0`, after the rain call, so call order alone does not decide it. `[BIN:TPW-EXE:0x582ad0 deferred sort]` Primitives whose state lacks 0x1000/0x2000 are not drawn when submitted (`0x565590`); `0x582ad0` draws them back to front by mean depth (+0x30, sort `0x5829e0`). The quad's key is about 0.001 (its z); the bolts' key is forced to 0 (`0x5658c0(0)` in `0x57ec50`) and the drops' key is 0 (`0x57efd0`), so the bolts and drops draw after the quad. Opaque meshes (`0x1256`) draw in `0x581db0` before that flush; translucent meshes and the sky have larger keys, so they draw before the quad and are inverted. The reverse-sort condition `0x5828f0` (`([0x8BCBC8] & 0xC) == 0xC` plus bit 0x20000000) is not verified.
- **Before:** OpenTPW drew every entity, including the lobby's weather renderer, and the invert quad after them, so the bolt and the rain came out dark.
- **Now:** the other entities, then the flash quad, then the weather renderer (`GameFlow.RenderLobbyLayers`). The strike rate and the one-frame duration are unchanged.
- `docs/AUDIO.md` no longer says the flash is an opaque white frame.
- A test pins the order.
