## The online login dialog follows tp.exe's own table and keeps its additions inside frames (October 24, 2026)

The login dialog's fields for the server address and the authenticator code, and its Register and Forgot password buttons, sat below the login window and covered the form and the pause menu behind it. The check button was a small square with its label, and the Back button clipped its label over a round model.

- **The dialog is built from tp.exe's table 0x7585a8** (`OriginalLayoutTables.LoginWindow`, generated by `tpw_exe_layouts.py`; built by 0x4bb070 with UITEXT 0xec-0xee and 16-character fields, the twin of the dial-up details dialog's table 0x7583e8, which the generator checks and ties to the builder's push at 0x4bb08f): the `wdialogw` window, the two `!frame` fields with their labels and insets, the title, the check button (`b_okay`, -1) and the cross (`b_exit`, -2), both bare round buttons with hover help.
- **OpenTPW's own controls** (server address, authenticator code, Register, Forgot password) sit in a framed panel of their own (`w_med`) under the original window, and the status line sits in the panel under the code field (the original window has no room for a line). Nothing leaves a frame or covers an original control.
- **The Back button of the other online dialogs** (and the two-step setup dialog's) is the round `b_exit` cross at the window's bottom right corner, as the original dialogs have it, instead of `b_listpark` with a clipped label.

**Verified:** `LoginFormLayoutTests` checks the table, the containment in the window and in the panel, that nothing overlaps and the Back button's place; the front-end smoke test checks the same on the live screen and captures the dialog from the lobby and from the Publish Park form.
