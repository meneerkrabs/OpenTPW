## The Publish Park form shows its check button and follows tp.exe's own table (October 24, 2026)

The Publish Park form, opened from the pause menu, had no visible submit button. Its check button had no model, so it drew nothing but a faint label, and the close button's "Back" label was clipped over its round model. OpenTPW's website toggle and export button sat below the window and covered the pause menu's lines behind it.

- **The form is built from tp.exe's table 0x7595b0** (`OriginalLayoutTables.PublishWindow`, generated by `tpw_exe_layouts.py`, built by 0x4c4670, handled by 0x4c4480), as the postcard window is: `window2`, the two `!frame` fields with their inner insets, the title, the check button (`b_okay`, -1) and the cross (`b_exit`, -2), both bare round buttons with hover help.
- **The original's field behaviour is ported.** The name field holds 15 characters and starts with the localized theme name of the park's level; the description holds 126 and starts with UITEXT 0xdc; the title is UITEXT 0xda. Enter in the name field and Tab move the focus to the description; Escape and the cross close the form.
- **The check button has no enabled test, as in the original.** It publishes and closes the form once the server has the park. While logged out a click opens the login dialog.
- **OpenTPW's own controls.** The website toggle and the export button sit side by side in the strip of the window's green area below the description field; the status line moved to the free column left of the fields. No control covers an original one or leaves the window.
- **Mod-recording parks.** The refusal text is now a localized label in all nine languages and says "exported or published". `ShowPublish` checks it before the city globe opens (a dialog), the pause menu's Publish Park line is disabled for such a park, and the form's status column shows the refusal rather than a stale status.
- **The close buttons of the other online dialogs** now use `b_exit` without the clipped "Back" label.

**Verified:** the front-end smoke test opens the form from the park (English and Dutch captures, plus the login dialog a logged-out tick opens), checks the models, the limits and that the controls lie inside the window; `PublishFormLayoutTests` checks the table-derived layout and the localized refusal.
