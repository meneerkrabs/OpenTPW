## The river and the harbour's glass roof draw like the original's translucent pass (October 24, 2026)

Part of issue 829 ("multicoloured river", "harbour glass roof"). OpenTPW put every terrain mesh and every object part into batches in file order, with depth writes on, so a translucent mesh drawn before the geometry behind it hid that geometry: the Jungle river showed a flat blue sheet with the bed painted over it in patches, and the glass of the arrival and departure roofs hid the roof's supports and the plaza under it.

- **Original.** tp.exe gives each face a state word from its texture's own alpha type (0x581790 -> 0x590C40). The renderer's mask `[[0x879AB0]+0xC]` is 0x3000 (0x5667D0): the frame batcher 0x581DB0 draws faces whose state has bit 0x1000 or 0x2000 (opaque 0x1256, alpha-test-0xF0 0x2756) at once, and 0x565590 keeps the rest (0x556, 0x200552), sorting each record's triangles by the sum of their vertex depths, largest first (0x565490). 0x582AD0, called after the batcher by 0x582170 at the end of the scene pass 0x576A00, draws those records last, sorted by 0x5829E0 on the average vertex depth, largest first. The state decoder 0x567620 gives 0x556 an alpha blend with an alpha test at 0x10/255 and keeps depth writes on. Both halves of 0x582170 run on the Direct3D path (mode 1 of `DAT_008BD524`, 0x44DD50).
- **What decides translucency.** The header of the `.wct`: the loader 0x564C00 loads the alpha chunk only at 32 bits per pixel, 0x574D3A sets the type word 0x25 for that and 0x27 when header byte 1 is also non-zero, 0x5755F0 turns them into texture flags 0x100 and 0x200. `TextureFile.ReadAlphaType` reads it (opaque, cut-out, translucent); in the corpus 2,480 of the textures that carry an alpha plane are translucent and 236 are cut-outs. `wr_water3` and `gte_rof2` are translucent; their low-detail copies in `stexture` have no alpha plane and stay opaque.
- **Port.** `WorldTranslucency` splits a part's faces by their texture slot. `OriginalTerrain` keeps the opaque batches as before and makes translucent batches of their own; `OriginalObject` splits a mixed part into two models on the same node. Translucent models are `ModelEntity.Translucent`; `Level.WorldPassOrder` draws them after every other entity, farthest first by the mean vertex depth (clip-space w of the vertex mean), and the particle and weather renderers follow them in their existing order. A translucent model sorts its own triangles farthest first by the sum of their corner depths (`Model.OrderTriangles`, a dynamic index buffer, redone only when the model-view-projection changes). `test.shader` tests the vertices of translucent faces at 0x10/255 (vertex bit 0x20000000) and everything else at 0.1 as before; the precompiled browser shader is regenerated.
- **Before and after.** Native captures of the Jungle river (`--capture-camera -18,0,30,0`) and the harbour roof (`-10,-76,40,-90`): the river bed shows through the water and the cyan halo around leaves is gone; the roof panes show the supports, the road markings and the plaza.
- **Tests.** `WorldTranslucencyTests` (header classification, state words and the 0x3000 mask, the face split, the triangle and entity sort order, the level's draw sequence) and `TextureAlphaTypeCorpusTests` (every texture of the data, the named river and roof textures).

Still open:
- The ground heightfield, the path layer, coaster and track pieces, pools and the lobby keep their entity order.
- The original's deferred particle batches (0x556, 0x200552) join the same far-to-near list as the world; OpenTPW keeps the particles a pass of their own (PTCL-905).
- Cut-out textures (an alpha plane with a zero alpha byte, 236 files such as `jri_bnk4`) draw opaque: the decoder reads an alpha plane only when the alpha byte is 1.
- The alpha-reference index (0x10/0xF0 needs index 2, which 0x567270 sets when its probe passes) and the reverse sort 0x5828F0 (gated on `[0x8BCBC8] & 0xC` and 0x20000000; no writer of bits 0x4 and 0x8 was found) are read from the code, not observed on a device.
- The state bits that depend on model and texture flags (0x400000, a model's 0x800, 0x5156) and the atlas-page grouping of the batcher have no counterpart here.
