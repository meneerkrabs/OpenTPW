## A guest's tools build in the shared park (October 25, 2026)

A shared park's guests can now build with their own tools once the host lets them ([COOP.md](../COOP.md), "Building by every player"; `[EXT:online-coop-commands]`, `[EXT:online-coop-permissions]`). This is C3a-2, on top of the requests of C3a-1.

- The host's Build Together screen has a switch for each guest, "Builds" or "Watches", beside the removal. It shows the shared park's grant, and a click turns it over.
- A granted guest's path and queue tool, its placement of an object and its remove click send their commands to the host and wait. While a tool waits, it keeps its ghost, takes no other click and asks the player to wait. When the command comes back in the host's stream, the tool goes on as the host's does: a line from its end, a placed ride into its queue tool, the build tool kept for exactly Ctrl at the click.
- A command the host could not take leaves the click as if it was never made, with a note to try again. A refused line or placement says its reason, as the host's do, and each refusal is said. A tool that was switched or ended while it waited, or a park a resync replaced, turns the outcome into a message only; the session's end ends every wait.
- For now a granted guest builds only what those tools do: lines, objects whose own tool does not follow them (no coasters, kart, water or bumper rides, slide towers or track add-ons), and single removals. Land, the queue's edit, the area clear, the backtrack key and undo stay the host's, as the guest is told.
- Each player hears and reads only their own build: the host hears no cue of a guest's placement or demolition and shows none of its messages, and a guest hears its own when its command applies in its park.
- The sharing and joining screens now say that the host decides who builds, in every language.
