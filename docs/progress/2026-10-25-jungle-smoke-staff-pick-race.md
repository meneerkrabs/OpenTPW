## The jungle smoke's staff clicks no longer race the Splish Splash animation (October 25, 2026)

The native jungle smoke (`--load-original-level jungle --smoke-test`) failed now and then with "the Place tool is still armed over its cell after the probes (Place, (63, 32))". The cause was the cursor pick, not a staff probe or a guest. The cursor pick tests the owner's model at its current animation pose (`ObjectRayPick`, tp.exe `0x45bf90`). The staff phase picked cell (63, 33) at frame 8, and the ray at that pixel passes the height window of the water track's Splish Splash station at (63, 29), which the water-track phase builds just before. Whether the station's animated model crossed the ray at the check (frame 18) depended on the wall-clock time at that frame, so under load the same pixel resolved to (63, 32), an invalid cell. Diagnostic runs showed that camera, matrices, terrain and object list were identical in passing and failing runs. Only `Time.Now` at the check differed, and the ray hit Splish Splash in exactly the failing runs.

The game's pick is unchanged, because tp.exe also picks the model at its current pose. The smoke now uses only pointers whose pick cannot depend on a pose: the cell centres and their three probe points must have no cell on the walk that names an owner whose height window the ray passes. That test is the new internal `Level.CursorPickTestsObjectModels`, which runs the same walk with a stand-in shape that meets every line. It is pose-independent because only the final model line test reads the pose. The Place and Carry checks are as strict as before, and their messages now also print `HoverValid` and the wanted cell. `Level.ComputeGroundHit`'s terrain step moved into `Level.TerrainHit` so the two share it. An independent review pointed out a second ordering gap in the same phase. The coaster checks waited only while a staff tool was active. Between the placement and the pick-up no tool runs, so a coaster check whose train left during the Place tool would run in that gap and could scroll the camera (`TryClickPoint`) under the drop point that was projected at the phase's start. The coaster checks now wait for the whole staff phase. In all observed runs they had run after the pick-up anyway.

A new test, `ObjectRayPickTests.APointerWhosePickTestsAModelIsNamedWhateverThePose`, checks that the answer stays true when the model moves off the ray, and that it is false beside the tower.

Verification under load (load averages 9 to 23 plus two to six busy-loop processes): pristine main (22a35e03d) failed 5 of 10 jungle smokes, all with this message. The fixed build passed 12 of 12 in a row. After merging main, the branch passed 10 of 10. The final commit, with main merged again (#843, #846, #849, #850), passed another 10 of 10 in a row. The smoke now picks (69, 32) and (69, 35), and neither pointer's pick tests a model at any point up to the drop (frame 38, object version unchanged).
