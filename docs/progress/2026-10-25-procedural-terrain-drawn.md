## The ground is drawn with the original's procedural textures (October 25, 2026)

Part of #829 (package E, steps E4 to E7). With PROCEDURALTEXTURING on, tp.exe gives every visible terrain cell its own N × N texture built from the theme's `base.lnd`; OpenTPW drew one repeated static texture per slot. The ground of a level is now drawn the original's way, the options are applied, and the open questions of the research are answered from tp.exe or documented. Details and addresses are in [LND.md](../LND.md).

- **The visible-cell cache is copied, not a full atlas** (the user's decision). `ProceduralTextureCache` is the 128 × 128 key table and ring of nodes of `0x572110` / `0x572440` move for move, 1500, 2000 or 2500 slots; `ProceduralTerrainAtlas` packs the slots' tiles into RGBA pages of at most 2048 texels (N = 32 with 2000 cells: 8 MB; N = 64 with 2500: 41 MB). Each frame `ProceduralTerrain` takes the cells of the original's visible spans, looks them up (every frame, as the original does: a hit moves its node), builds the textures of the cells that took a new slot (the byte-exact texturer, written straight as RGBA with alpha 255) and rewrites one dynamic mesh. Every listed cell is built before the frame draws, on the desktop and in the browser alike, as the original does.
- **The visible cells, the fog and the far plane are the original's.** `TerrainVisibleSpans` is `0x56BB00` and the routines under it (four corner rays, their ground points, the convex hull, the clip, the rasteriser) in single precision, operation by operation, fed what the original feeds it: the camera's pose, the 90 degree rays, the heightfield header's stored lowest and highest height and the ray distance of FIRSTPERSONVIEWDISTANCE (200, 325, 450, 700); the x86 oracle (406 cameras, every hull and clip branch) and a 66-camera golden pin it bit for bit, and a second oracle target pins the cache lookup `0x572440` on random frame sequences (slots, builds, ring, key table); the gate in front of the list (`0x56F0A2`, `0x576EC0`) is ported too. FIRSTPERSONVIEWDISTANCE also sets the fog range of every view (`0x44DBB0` to `SetFog` `0x567AD0`: linear, 50 to 60, 100 to 110, 135 to 150 or 230 to 250 original units in the projection's depth) and the park view's far plane is the span selection's farthest corner distance (`0x578CA0`); the shaders fog like that, the lobby with its own 50 to 300. The ride and walk views' far plane (`0x56EA90`'s cell boxes) is read, not ported.
- **Options, at the next level load.** PROCEDURALTEXTURING 0, 1, 2, 3 give off, 16, 32, 64 texels and PROCEDURALTEXTURECACHE 0, 1, 2 give 1500, 2000, 2500 cells, with the memory clamp of `0x572110` (below 48 MB every size is 16; below 64 MB a 64 becomes 32). The presets follow the original files (Low off, Medium and High 32 texels, Enhanced 64). The lobby and a level without `base.lnd` stay static. The support strings, the COMPATIBILITY.md rows and `CompatibilityTests` say "applied".
- **Open questions, answered.**
  - *The rectangle (OPEN-2):* the cells from (XStart, YStart) to (XStart + max(0, offset) + Width, YStart + max(0, offset) + Height), inclusive, from the level's `MapInfo.Heightfield*` keys (the sam parser writes them; no store to them exists in the code). That is cells 0 to 95 by 0 to 84, the whole heightfield. **Column 95** is inside it, and the original builds it from the weight grid's flat reads (the last samples are the first of the next sample row); the x86 oracle agrees with the C# texturer on the shipped jungle file and on new synthetic cells. The texturer used to refuse column 95.
  - *Pages, UVs, mips and address mode (OPEN-3):* the original packs its small textures into shared pages (`0x58F190`, `0x590350`), takes the texel centre of the first and last texel as the coordinates (half a texel inset, scale 1), has no gutter, creates no mip levels for the pages (`0x58B0B0`), and draws the ground with state word 0x1256: clamp, linear magnification, point minification, no alpha test, no blending. OpenTPW uses the same inset and no mips and draws through the preset's sampler, the difference [APPROX:COMPAT-010] already records.
  - *Camera modes (OPEN-1):* entering a view from inside the park (the mode bits 0x2 and 0x4 of `[0x790AB0]`: the terrain camcorder, and the follow of a ride) switches the textures off (`0x457A40`, `0x46DED0(0)` brings the turn and mirror bits back) and leaving switches them on, `0x457A00` only when `[0x78D8D8]` is 1. Bit 0x2 is the terrain camcorder and bit 0x4 the follow of a ride (record flag 0x400000, `0x4E15B0`). OpenTPW draws the static ground in the ride camera and the free walk camera.
  - *Alpha (OPEN-6):* the terrain pass has no alpha test, so the original's alpha 0 never mattered; the shader is unchanged and the textures carry 255. `0x562F70` (16-bit pages) was read, not run, and is not ported.
- **Frame cost** (Release, Jungle, 9000 frames, 150 guests, two alternating rounds, High preset = 32 texels, ENGINE-PERFORMANCE.md): still at the Home zoom 1.72 and 1.77 ms against the main build's 1.72 and 1.76, panning 1.51 and 1.53 against 1.39 and 1.41. At zoom 300 the original's span selection lists 2500 cells, more than the 2000 slot cache, so every frame builds 2000 textures: 56 to 72 ms against 3.4 (the original does the same with its wide zoom flag, and the view is all fog colour at the High preset). The Home view lists 898 cells and builds them in 37 ms on the first frame; in the browser (AOT) the entering frame is about 28 ms longer, once, and the steady window is unchanged.
- **Tests.** `ProceduralTextureCacheTests` (slot order, hits, eviction, dirty rebuild, overflow, key, option mapping, memory clamp, rectangle, page counts), `TerrainVisibleSpansTests` and `ProceduralTextureCacheTests` replay oracle-generated cameras and frame sequences, `ProceduralTerrainMeshTests` (cells listed, builds, a lookup in every frame, hole answers kept per version, quad corner order for both diagonals, texture coordinates, render holes, on stand-in pages without a device), the RGBA writer against the original's bytes, the column after the weight grid (81 synthetic cells and six jungle cells from the oracle), and the jungle smoke checks that the cells in view are drawn.

Still open (and written down in LND.md):
- The far plane of the ride and walk views is the greatest depth of the cell boxes `0x56EA90` selects through `0x56E7E0` and `0x561BC0` (at least 100 original units); not ported, those views keep the default. The second span path itself draws the static ground in those modes.
- The device's fog depth (w or z of the transformed vertices) is taken as w, from the rhw the vertex projection stores; the vendor workaround that turns the table fog off (bits 0xC000 of `[0x8BCBC8]`) is not applied.
- The original's mip filter field for the terrain pass and its option; the names of the two camera modes; the "not allowed" device check; the floor-art tail (package F).
