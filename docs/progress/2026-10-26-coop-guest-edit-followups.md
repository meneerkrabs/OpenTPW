## Guest building follow-ups: claimed queues, slide towers and the session log's replay (October 26, 2026)

These follow up on a guest's building in a shared park (C3b; [COOP.md](../COOP.md), "Building by every player"; `[EXT:online-coop-commands]`, `[EXT:online-coop-permissions]`).

- **Claimed rides' queues.** A guest no longer edits, lays, removes or backtracks the queue of a coaster or a track ride's station, draws a line across it, places an object over it, or cuts it off from its path. Nor does a guest's placement demolish an object the guest may not remove. Those rides' edits need claims, which come later, so their queues stay the host's until then.
- **Slide towers.** A guest no longer sells, removes, clears or moves a pool slide tower, since a slide belongs to the pool tools, as bridges and pool water do.
- **The session log's replay.** A session's own log now replays under the session's rights, and refuses to replay without them. A guest's command the session refused stays refused in the replay, so its checkpoints match.
- **The object window's erase button.** It is now off while a guest's sale of that object waits for the host.
- **The attractions list.** It drops a check that could never fire: the list never holds a fixed item.
