## Object floors: the skirt, the edge conform, opaque skirt slots, holes, the composite and the depth compare (October 26, 2026)

Issue 829 (ground z-fighting under rides and shops), package F steps 1 to 5 and the depth compare. The original has no "ground flattening" under a placed object; what happens where a floor meets the ground was traced in tp.exe and ported. The research is in `OpenTPW-notes/plans/procedural-floors-plan.md`; the oracle ran the routines (X28 to X32 in [ORACLE.md](../reverse/ORACLE.md)).

- **F1, the skirt read at type load** (`ObjectSkirt`, `SkirtReading`): the footprint edge mask (`0x0052C420`), the best axis-aligned horizontal floor face of every edge cell and the entry `{orientation code, texture slot}` it gives (`0x0046AA10`), the node's texture coordinates after the snap, per-face shift and wrap pass (`0x00467310`), the base record's flag 0x40 (MD2 header `+0x30` bit 0x08 with a ground node, or header bit 0x80). 60/57/61/59 types per theme get cells, 394/370/437/437 cells in all (88/97/106/129 flipped), Belly Bounce's ten entries are `C8 E0 90 A0 88 A0 88 80 90 E0` (`ObjectSkirtCorpusTests`). Open question F3 of the plan is settled: the instance grid `+0xCC` is the object's height map (`.hmp`), not `Info.Shape` itself, but its size must equal the shape's (`0x00451640` regenerates a map of another size) and every shipped map has it.
- **F2, the edge conform at every placement** (`ObjectGroundConform`, `ObjectFloorMath`): each ground vertex the counting walk listed (`ObjectHoardingGeometry.ScanGround` now also returns the list) is moved onto the nearest terrain grid corner, whatever PROCEDURALTEXTURING says: its ground coordinates onto the grid line and its height onto that corner's height, in the instance's own copy of the node's positions (the shared model never changes) through the original's float LU inverse, in float arithmetic: the thread runs the x87 at the 24-bit word 0x7F (COAST-032). A vertex past the last corner ends its node. The cursor pick reads the copy.
- **F3, opaque skirt slots**: the slots of the floor faces in edge cells carry the runtime slot flag 0x800 and draw with state 0x1256 (`0x00581790`): `WorldTranslucency.OpaqueSlotFlag` (vertex bit 0x08000000) keeps them out of the translucent pass and `test.shader` neither tests alpha nor blends them; the slots `0x00467310` clears the wrap bits of clamp.
- **Depth compare (F8)**: the device set-up sets `D3DRS_ZFUNC` once, to LESSEQUAL (`0x0057360F`); no other code writes it (the state decoder `0x00567620` writes ZENABLE, ZWRITEENABLE, alpha and blend only). `Material.DepthComparison` ports it for every depth-tested material (Theme Park Inc's no-CD `Game.exe` sets the same LESSEQUAL at `0x006D6574` and `0x006D5C1E`, so both games take it, and the skirt and conform too: its `0x66D146` mask code, `0x6EFCA0` slot test and the conform tail at `0x4CE588` are the same). In captures the floors under Belly Bounce's entrance now win against the terrain they lie in, where before they striped; some pixels elsewhere change because floors and fringe art become visible.
- **RIDES-014 retired.** The "blends the ground flat (`0x467030`, `0x57ACE0`)" clause was wrong (`0x467030` places and stores the footprint box, `0x57ACE0` builds the floor composite), `+0xF0/+0xF4` are the shape size, and `0x00466D70` run in the oracle (`tpw-exe-base-height`) returns `ParkObjects.GetBaseHeight` on all 500 synthetic inputs. The PATH-015 text that called the loader flag 1 an `.hmp` "ground deformation" is corrected.
- **Presentation only.** No simulation state, world hash or save field reads an object's vertices; the conform works on copies, so `DeterminismTests` are unchanged.
- **Tests.** `ObjectSkirtGoldenTests` (mask, coordinate pass, skirt reader, conform and base height against the oracle's results, bit for bit, `ObjectSkirtSyntheticGolden`), `ObjectSkirtCorpusTests`, `ObjectFloorCorpusTests` (every listed vertex of every type at four rotations on a sloped heightfield lands on the corner; the walk ends past the edge), `WorldTranslucencyTests` and `TextureSlotAddressingTests` (the opaque flag, the shader contract, the depth compare), `ObjectFloorBaseTests` (the composite against the oracle's goldens, the orientation table, the skirt words and their turns, the landscape words, the holes' types, the composites over a box, a neighbour's cell, a slot named twice), `ProceduralTerrainHoleRestoreTests` (a cell that stops being a hole is rebuilt once) and `ObjectFloorBaseCorpusTests` (the orientation and the art's rows against the shipped floors, the seam, a composite for every skirt cell). Captures before and after, Jungle and Space, flat and sloped ground, procedural textures on and off.
- **F4, no ground under a footprint** (`ObjectCellHoles`, `OriginalCellWords`): the cell refresh (`0x5365D0` through the chooser `0x535DD0`, then `0x46DF50` mode 8) writes a hole word (`|= 0x101`, `&= ~0x80`) into every cell of map type 2, 7, 0x1E, 4, 9, 10 or 0x15, and the terrain cell list (`0x56F090`) skips a cell with bit 0x01 in both texturing modes: the ground under a ride, shop or entrance is not drawn at all, the floor stands on nothing, and the painted grass of its edge art is meant to be seen so (static) or to be replaced by the composite (procedural). The earlier reading that the static ground stays under a floor was wrong. A cell back at type 0 takes its backup word with the dirty bit: the procedural terrain draws it again and rebuilds its texture once. With the procedural textures on, the skirt cells also take their word `(old & 6) | orientation | 0x281` and the entry's slot (`0x46336C` to `0x463580`; the origin cell, the turn tables `0x46395C`, `0x463994`, `0x4639CC`), written when the object is placed and restored when it is removed.
- **F5, the composite** (`ProceduralTerrainTexturer.Compose`, `ObjectFloorBase`): the tail of `0x55F780` with a floor descriptor, run in the oracle as X33 (56 of 56 cases with 32-bit and 4444 art, every orientation word, art sizes that are not multiples of N; 42 cases as `ProceduralTerrainComposeGolden`). One composite per skirt cell and placed instance (`0x57ACE0` over the footprint box, so another object's skirt cell inside the box is composed too), made once at placement, no cache slots, a texture of its own without mips (E's OPEN-3), freed with the instance. The art's rows: `0x590DA0` turns the art over, and the data confirms it (OPEN-F1: the base orientation word puts the base pixel of the ground under 97% of the texels of every shipped floor at every turn, the turned art is transparent along edges to open ground and opaque along edges to the floor, and the transparent edge texels are the neighbouring ground's pixels). Belly Bounce, the coconut shop and every other floor now blend into the procedural ground.
- **Cost** (level load, about 57 objects, 430 to 470 composites): 0.4 to 0.5 s in the test host, mostly the second decode of the floor art (cached per type); 0.4, 1.7 and 7 MB of RGBA for N = 16, 32 and 64 as one texture per composite, no draw call is added.

Still open:
- Queue models get a skirt, a composite and a hole in the original too (OPEN-F6 answered: every queue model has the base flag and one skirt entry); OpenTPW bakes them into the path layer, where a texture per queue cell does not fit (PATH-015 names it).
- Path art over procedural ground (F9): the original composites a path tile into the terrain cell's own texture, point sampled at N × N; OpenTPW draws the path as a layer over the ground.
