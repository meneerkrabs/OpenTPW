## Object floors: the skirt, the edge conform, opaque skirt slots and the depth compare (October 26, 2026)

Issue 829 (ground z-fighting under rides and shops), package F steps 1 to 3 and the depth compare. The original has no "ground flattening" under a placed object; what happens where a floor meets the ground was traced in tp.exe and ported where it does not need the procedural terrain texturer (PR 843). The research is in `OpenTPW-notes/plans/procedural-floors-plan.md`; the oracle ran the routines (X28 to X32 in [ORACLE.md](../reverse/ORACLE.md)).

- **F1, the skirt read at type load** (`ObjectSkirt`, `SkirtReading`): the footprint edge mask (`0x0052C420`), the best axis-aligned horizontal floor face of every edge cell and the entry `{orientation code, texture slot}` it gives (`0x0046AA10`), the node's texture coordinates after the snap, per-face shift and wrap pass (`0x00467310`), the base record's flag 0x40 (MD2 header `+0x30` bit 0x08 with a ground node, or header bit 0x80). 60/57/61/59 types per theme get cells, 394/370/437/437 cells in all (88/97/106/129 flipped), Belly Bounce's ten entries are `C8 E0 90 A0 88 A0 88 80 90 E0` (`ObjectSkirtCorpusTests`). Open question F3 of the plan is settled: the instance grid `+0xCC` is the object's height map (`.hmp`), not `Info.Shape` itself, but its size must equal the shape's (`0x00451640` regenerates a map of another size) and every shipped map has it.
- **F2, the edge conform at every placement** (`ObjectGroundConform`, `ObjectFloorMath`): each ground vertex the counting walk listed (`ObjectHoardingGeometry.ScanGround` now also returns the list) is moved onto the nearest terrain grid corner, whatever PROCEDURALTEXTURING says: its ground coordinates onto the grid line and its height onto that corner's height, in the instance's own copy of the node's positions (the shared model never changes) through the original's float LU inverse. A vertex past the last corner ends its node. The cursor pick reads the copy.
- **F3, opaque skirt slots**: the slots of the floor faces in edge cells carry the runtime slot flag 0x800 and draw with state 0x1256 (`0x00581790`): `WorldTranslucency.OpaqueSlotFlag` (vertex bit 0x08000000) keeps them out of the translucent pass and `test.shader` neither tests alpha nor blends them; the slots `0x00467310` clears the wrap bits of clamp.
- **Depth compare (F8)**: the device set-up sets `D3DRS_ZFUNC` once, to LESSEQUAL (`0x0057360F`); no other code writes it (the state decoder `0x00567620` writes ZENABLE, ZWRITEENABLE, alpha and blend only). `Material.DepthComparison` ports it for every depth-tested material (a Theme Park Inc run keeps the strict compare: its executable was not traced). In captures the floors under Belly Bounce's entrance now win against the terrain they lie in, where before they striped.
- **RIDES-014 retired.** The "blends the ground flat (`0x467030`, `0x57ACE0`)" clause was wrong (`0x467030` places and stores the footprint box, `0x57ACE0` builds the floor composite), `+0xF0/+0xF4` are the shape size, and `0x00466D70` run in the oracle (`tpw-exe-base-height`) returns `ParkObjects.GetBaseHeight` on all 500 synthetic inputs. The PATH-015 text that called the loader flag 1 an `.hmp` "ground deformation" is corrected.
- **Presentation only.** No simulation state, world hash or save field reads an object's vertices; the conform works on copies, so `DeterminismTests` are unchanged.
- **Tests.** `ObjectSkirtGoldenTests` (mask, coordinate pass, skirt reader, conform and base height against the oracle's results, bit for bit, `ObjectSkirtSyntheticGolden`), `ObjectSkirtCorpusTests`, `ObjectFloorCorpusTests` (every listed vertex of every type at four rotations on a sloped heightfield lands on the corner; the walk ends past the edge), `WorldTranslucencyTests` and `TextureSlotAddressingTests` (the opaque flag, the shader contract, the depth compare). Captures before and after, Jungle and Space, flat and sloped ground.

Still open:
- Under procedural texturing the original also makes each skirt cell a terrain hole and builds a composite floor texture per cell (F4, F5); with a 32-bit float depth buffer a floor coplanar with the terrain can keep some stripes in static mode until then (OPEN-F2: the original's own behaviour there was not observed).
- Queue models get a skirt in the original too (OPEN-F6); path art over procedural ground (F9).
