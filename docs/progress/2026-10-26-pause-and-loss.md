## Pause owners, the lost park and the first day (October 26, 2026)

Fidelity round 9, package A (ECON-030, UI-096, ADVISOR-017, PATH-017, ECON-063).

- **ECON-063 retired: a new park posts its first day message.** tp.exe's calendar constructor `0x4F7E80` stores day cache -1 and the world init `0x5156A0` syncs only the month and year caches, so the first update `0x4F8260` posts one day message (`0x40F4C0`) whatever the date. `ParkEconomy.PendingInitialDay`, which only the restart imports and own saves made before the first turn carried, is now also set for a new Theme Park World park (Theme Park Inc keeps its synchronised start). The first turn therefore rolls every object's six rings and runs the day listeners once more than before, which moves the fixed-run determinism pins (below).
- **UI-096 narrowed to the sound side; the pause owners are exact.** The game clock's freeze (`0x4092A0`) has eight callers and the P key toggles the same flag: the Escape menu (and the load, save and Game Options windows opened from it, `0x4A3A6C`), the map window, the postcard, the generic message boxes `0x47F020` (27 call sites, tabulated in docs/UI.md "Pause owners"), application deactivation and the P window. `ParkHud.ClockPaused` and `TimeScaleFor` follow them; the research, finance, loans, object and visitor windows do not pause. `[0xF82884]`, which `0x47F26C` tests, is non-zero only in the front end, so every park, a logged-in player's own included, pauses on its boxes; only shared parks and read-only visits are gated off (`[EXT:online-coop-lockstep]`). Research refusals are real message boxes, opening the lab with no researcher is refused as in tp.exe (`UIStrings.NeedToHireScientists`), and the postcard composer's refusal is the alert queue's pausing box (ERRORMSG `0x4E`, `0x4A8C89`). A box that paused resumes the game when it closes, ending a P pause and its backdrop (`0x47EFAC`-`0x47EFDA`), and the P backdrop holds the focus, so no HUD shortcut or Escape works under it. Sound/crowd keep the narrowed text (`0x598960`, `0x51C060`).
- **ADVISOR-017 retired: the advisor queue steps once per park turn.** `0x59A550` runs once per thing-loop turn; `AutomaticAdvisor.Update` now serves the queue after each scan call instead of once per frame, and the postcard screen joins the silent advisor stop.
- **PATH-017 narrowed: the P window's backdrop follows the window manager's hit test.** The pause backdrop captures the mouse (`0x42A760` callers) and the camera takes no input under it, wherever a later screen sits.
- **ECON-030 narrowed: the lost-park scene is ported.** A bankrupt park opens the loss window `0x7472` with its gate camera, Escape or a right release (after 2 s) leave into the hidden-HUD overlay (`0x4A29D0`), a loaded lost park restores the overlay without the camera (`0x4815E0` -> `0x48ADB0`), and Escape in the overlay opens the five-entry pause menu. The banner model and its 24-step table come from the generated `LossScreen` layout (`0x74FB20`, `0x74FC38`). Open: an audit of the direct economic guards under the overlay and the exact banner draw.
- **Co-op gate.** The new pauses (message boxes, alert queue, app deactivation) and the loss scene apply only without a `CoopSession` and outside read-only visits, so single player matches tp.exe and shared parks keep their lockstep clock until the co-op owner decides.
- **Determinism.** The ECON-063 change moves every fixed-run pin; the old -> new table is in docs/DETERMINISM.md ("First day message of a new park"). The loss state round-trips in the existing save fields; old saves load unchanged.
- **Save form of the walk-away history.** The first day's zero entry exposed that a restored all-zero walk-away history differs from the live one at the next day roll; the saved form now leaves out the oldest zero days, so the park-resume smoke matches again and no pin moves.
- **Findings.** UI-094's "0x48A740 ride screen with a 2 s right-click delay" was a misattribution: the ride view's procedure `0x488A00` leaves on a right click with RMBCancel and `gui_CameraFlags & 0x16` and reads no clock (`RideRightClickDelaySeconds` is gone); the 2000 ms guard belongs to the loss window's `0x48A740`. A deactivation during the loss window gives the focus to the P window, which handles only P. Theme Park Inc has no `bankrupt.MD2`, so its lost park keeps the old behaviour.
