## The queue tool, its re-evaluation and its removals follow tp.exe (October 26, 2026)

Fidelity round 9, package B. This read the queue tool's remaining routines in tp.exe (`0x536100`, `0x524960`, `0x52F200`, `0x52F580`, `0x532FC0`, `0x5346D0`, `0x5367A0`, `0x527EE0`, `0x52FE50`, `0x530120`, `0x4DE040`, `0x4DE130`, `0x4DE1F0`, `0x4DE4A0`, `0x4FB360`, `0x501390`) and ported what they showed. HEADS-008, QUEUE-014 and QUEUE-023 are retired; QUEUE-003, QUEUE-010 and QUEUE-015 are narrowed.

- **QUEUE-023, the re-evaluation `0x4DE1F0`, runs at every caller.** A placed ride with a queue is evaluated twice (inside the placement commit at `0x529890`, and at the click's tail at `0x52537A` or the Move tool's `0x5259AE`), each raising the stamp counter and stamping the front cell's block. A path cell's clear beside an entrance cell with an owner word (`0x5367A0` at `0x53694B`) re-evaluates that ride on every route that clears a path cell (the bulldozer, a queue laid over a path at a placement, an `@` footprint cell, a coaster marker, the guest grid's queue setter), stamping the block of the back cell only when the front the selector finds is not 0. Back re-evaluates after every undo that popped a point, also when the prior corner is a path cell and the second click is suppressed (`0x52FFEC`). The path tool's Back passes no ride (`0x40BEB3`), so it re-evaluates the last placed or edited ride (`0x8186AC`, written at `0x524C3D`, `0x5277A1` and `0x527831`, cleared only by the level's start `0x52F16F`, never saved): that is `Level.LastRide`, session state in the join snapshot (version 5) and the session digest, in no save and no world hash. A ride's demolition runs Back in a loop (`0x527FC1`, `0x527FD0`): every pop re-evaluates the ride with the room its shrunken queue leaves, so the guests beyond it are dismissed in place with thought 13 (ids divisible by 3) before the removal message `0x4FB360` (`QueuePaths.ClearAll`; the bridges without placed cells keep the one clear after the message). The queue edit click re-evaluates whether or not the walk cleared a link (`0x526118`). A placement cannot connect a queue: the outside cell is cleared and typed 3 with only the link toward the entrance. The old note that the clear of a queue cell calls the re-evaluation was wrong: only the clear of a path cell does.
- **QUEUE-010 and QUEUE-015, the clear writes no guest.** The queue tool's undo no longer sets the guests on the emptied cells down on the nearest path. The re-evaluation after it dismisses by place alone (`0x501390`), in place, and a guest within the room keeps its place. A guest leaving a queue (`0x5004AA`, the edit's dismissal, a removed ride's message `0x4FB360`) stays in state 6 where it stands on every placed ride, and decides from its cell by the ported wander (`0x4F9490`). Bridges without placed cells (the gates, the test rigs, the Theme Park Inc slice) keep the old relocation. The labelled `[EXT:dev-one-click-remove]` and `[EXT:queue-backtrack]` keep both labels and take the original's guest policy by the user's decision: the guests stay in place and the re-evaluation dismisses them.
- **QUEUE-015, counters, the undo and demolition.** The queue edit's corner walk now writes the placement counter `+0x20` (1 on a corner, 0 on every other cell it visits and on the entrance cell, `0x5302C2`, `0x5302C9`), and the tool the edit click enters takes the segments of the existing queue back through the native undo (`0x52FE50`, mode `0x34`: the second click clears the line from the popped point to the new top cell by cell, so a corner whose next segment is gone has lost its second link and keeps its counter 1 - 1). A ride whose front holds no queue cell is demolished with one cell's debit, as the original counts the chain from the front whatever its type (`0x527FB4`).
- **QUEUE-003, the zero front.** An entry without a cardinal link bit has no front (`0x4DE040` writes 0): its demolition has no count and its re-evaluation raises the counter without stamping a block (`0x4DE4A0` reads such a ride as unconnected, but the opening guard keeps ECON-049's permissive answer: the sandbox smoke's track rides are placed without their access cells and rely on it, so that rule is package C's). The guests' joining and scoring of such a ride still use the host's one-cell fallback.
- **QUEUE-014, the commit.** Mode 3 always has a line start (`0x52A050` after a placement, `0x530120` after the edit click), so the tool starting at the front or back cell is the original's and no first click without a start reaches it. The native segment route now runs the commit's overwrite pass `0x87`: an object of a lower overwrite priority on the line is demolished before the type pass (`0x53452F`, `0x527EE0`), where such lines used to take the per-cell route.
- **HEADS-008.** A bounce rider keeps the node its slot stored when BOUNCE filled it (`0x555742`: the script's `+0x70` plus the index). The saved form carries the nodes only when a script changed the base while riders bounced, and the canonical hash is unchanged.
- **#755 folded in.** A queue line ending on an initial path cell stays on the native route, as the validator returns 8 for a terminal path before the NoModify test (`0x535C57..0x535C99`). QUEUE-012 itself stays with package H.
- **Co-op.** The entry points and the command shapes are unchanged (`EditQueueCommand`, `UndoLineCommand`, `UndoQueueSegmentCommand`, `QueueSegmentCommand`, `RemoveQueueCellCommand`). The queue edit now also writes the walk's counters, and a segment over a low-priority object is a `QueueSegmentCommand` where it used to be a `LayLineCommand`. Old logs decode and take the same route, but their checkpoints from earlier builds mismatch, as every recorded log is guaranteed only on the build that recorded it (docs/COOP.md). The join state is version 5 (it carries the last ride).
- **Tests.** `QueueRemovalCommandTests` (the clear writes no guest, the re-evaluation dismisses in place, the wander from the emptied cell, the front-less demolition debit), `QueueSegmentReducerTests` (the demolition pass, Back's re-evaluation), `QueueUndoTests` (the walk's counters), `TypedQueueFrontTests` (the zero front, the stamp), `ObjectAccessCellsTests` (the placement's two evaluations, the clear beside an entrance), `BounceRiderTests` (the stored node, the save), and #755's `QueueTerrainValidationTests`.
