## Self-lit materials draw full-bright: the Gift Shop chest glows again (October 26, 2026)

Issue 829 reported that the space between the Souvenir Shop's base and its lid is darkened instead of looking like it glows. The Gift Shops of Jungle and Hallow are treasure chests whose opening shows a gold glow and a gold interior; OpenTPW lit those parts like the wood and metal around them. This traced the vertex fill of tp.exe's mesh draw (`0x580530`, `0x580C51`), the lighting pass (`0x57AA10`, `0x574660`, `0x5741B0`), the state builder and decoder (`0x581790`, `0x590C40`, `0x567620`) and the texture-flag builder `0x5755F0` for those parts.

- **The parts.** Jungle `giftshop.md2`: the node `jt_head1` (the lid and the opening; materials `b_glow2` and `jt_box1a`, the gold interior) and `jt_head03` (four faces of `b_glow2`). Hallow `giftshop.MD2`: the node `glow01` (14 faces of `glow2`), `h_glow2` and `treasure` in the node `chest`, and the three `web2` quads. The glow textures are one flat gold (green for `h_glow2`) with an alpha that ramps from clear to opaque; their `.wct` headers carry an alpha plane with a non-zero alpha byte, so the state is 0x556 (source alpha over inverse source alpha, alpha test 0x10/255, no additive blend), drawn in the sorted translucent pass, which OpenTPW already did right. The slots carry the flags 0x13 and 0x33, no UV animation exists, and the collect clips toggle the glow nodes' visibility only (node flag tracks).
- **The cause.** The material record's last dword (runtime +0xC, `MaterialData.Pad`) has bit 0x1 on exactly these materials. The original reads it at `0x580C51`: with it set the corner's diffuse colour is the draw's constant colour (0xFFFFFFFF), with it clear the lit colour of `0x574660` ANDed with that mask. The texture stage modulates, so a self-lit texture shows exactly. OpenTPW read the word and wrote it back for its own models but nothing honoured it, so every self-lit material was shaded by the sun and the 0.4 ambient: the gold interior came out near black and the glow a dull orange.
- **The fix.** `TextureSlotAddressing.VertexFlags( material )` carries bit 0x1 as the vertex flag `SelfLitFlag` (0x04000000) and `test.shader` shades such a corner with 1: no sun, no ambient, no point light (at day; see the night rule below). The four vertex builders of `test.shader` pass the material (placed objects, the original terrain meshes in two places, the lobby model scenes). Over the corpus 920 of 12,951 materials are self-lit (11 of them the Gift Shops'); the others are in ride and sideshow models, the jungle terrain base, the lobby islands, the interface models and the advisors. The audit of every other shader that draws MD2 materials is in `docs/MD2-MODELS.md` ("Self-lit materials").
- **Also corrected.** `docs/MD2-MODELS.md` said the slot flag 0x2 becomes state bit 0x800000 in `0x581790`; the builder tests bit 0x2 of the material word +0xC, which no shipped material sets (every word is 0 or 1). UI-034 repeated the slot-flag reading; its text now says the bit is material word bit 0x2, which no shipped material sets, and keeps the ID for the one open piece (a capture of the original lobby panel and gauges for the 0xF0 test). No label added.
- **Captures.** Silent, offscreen, 1280x720, objects placed with a local-only patch (never committed): the three Gift Shops and the jungle Inca god, the hallow candle and cauldron, the space hover bot and a night torch (whose flame is a self-lit material of OpenTPW's own, `EveningTorchTests`), each before and after, in `reports/issue829/glow/`.
- **Tests.** `SelfLitMaterialTests`: the flag from material bit 0x1 only, the slot flags kept, a free vertex bit, the shader's constants and the corpus pins (every material word is 0 or 1, 920 of 12,951; the chests' glow, treasure and gold self-lit and their wood, metal and lid lit; the glow slots' flags and textures); `NativeWorldShaderRenderTests` draws a lit and a self-lit white texel (about 102 against 255 under the ambient only; Metal or Direct3D 11).
- **Advisor.** Its models go through the same fill (0x581DB0 -> 0x580530), so `advisor.shader` honours `SelfLitFlag` too: the five `Mouth` meshes and the gold ticket meshes (7 materials per theme model). Its captures (`jungle-advisor-idle-*`, `jungle-advisor-speaking-*`) show no pixel difference because a mouth facing the light is lit to white anyway; the native shader test (`ASelfLitCornerIsDrawnFullBrightAndALitOneAtTheAmbient`, both shaders) and `TheAdvisorsMouthsAndGoldTicketsAreTheSelfLitMeshesAndTheirVerticesCarryIt` carry the proof.
- **Other shaders.** The interface draws through the sprite pass and the build-icon preview unlit (UI-026), the pool, water, sprite, particle and weather shaders draw no MD2 material from the data; none changed.

Review round (October 27, 2026):
- **Night rule ([EXT:evening], user decision).** At dusk and night a self-lit material that is no light source takes the night tint of the extension's other unlit content, the pool water's (`EveningLook.UnlitTint`; the sprites' tint is brighter and made the night river lighter than it was), written by `ModelEntity` into the camera position `test.shader` never reads and applied only while the tinted ambient is set, without the soft shoulder. At day nothing changes: full-bright as in tp.exe. Real light sources stay full-bright: a self-lit material whose texture name contains `glow`, `flame`, `fire`, `torch`, `lamp` or `light` (`TextureSlotAddressing.IsLightSource`, vertex flag `LightSourceFlag`). Over the original files these are `b_glow2`, `fire0`, `flame`, `GEM_GLOW`, `glow2`, `h_flame`, `h_glow`, `h_glow2`, `h_light`, `s_lights`, `sc_glow`, and the evening extension's own torch flames (`tt_*_flame`) and the hallow torch's `tt_hal_glow`; the list is pinned over every model of the archives (`SelfLitMaterialTests`, `EveningTorchTests`) and documented in `docs/EVENING.md`. The fire pit's coals, lava and the beams are tinted, not lights. No label added; the wording of `[EXT:evening]` (`FeatureGates.Evening`) is extended.
- **Point lights.** `test.shader` keeps its one literal `if ( g_oUbo.g_flLightCount > 0.5 )` (the self-lit override comes after it), so `PointLightTests` holds.
- **Mask writers.** The draw mask `[[0x87A248] + 0x2C]` is written only by the anaglyph stereo mode (0x577120, 0x57715D, 0x57718C) and the software renderer's 0x57AB61 (restored at 0x57AB78 before the fill); the docs and the code comment say so.
- **0x5630B0 post-multiply.** Per-corner `(c x m) >> 8` runs only for a texture object without an image page (the guard at 0x580D28 to 0x580D45 skips it when `[texture + 0x1C]` is non-null); a shipped slot's loaded image has its page, so neither the 254/255 factor nor the 0x10000000 colour reaches a corner OpenTPW draws and nothing was ported (the writer of that page flag was not traced, said so in `docs/MD2-MODELS.md`).
- **Captures** (`reports/issue829/glow/`, silent, offscreen, local-only placement patch): the jungle river and terrain base day and night (`jungle-river-*`), the lobby (`lobby-*`), the Gift Shop chests, the candle and the Inca god (ride signs and cars) at night (`*-night-*`), and the torch at night (`jungle-torches-night-*`), each before (self-lit shader switched off for the run) and after.
