## Guests manage the staff of a shared park (October 27, 2026)

The host of a shared park can now give a guest the staff right, the last of the five rights ([COOP.md](../COOP.md), "Money and rights (C4)", "A guest's staff (C4c-2)"; `[EXT:online-coop-commands]`, `[EXT:online-coop-permissions]`). This is C4c-2, the second half of C4c.

- **Hiring and moving staff.** A guest hires from the staff window and places the new employee with a click, and picks an employee up from its window and drops it elsewhere, as the host does. Each step waits for the host's answer; the tool appears once the host agreed, and comes back by itself after a resync.
- **Changing your mind.** Escape, a right click or another tool lets go of the staff tool at once, also while a hire is still on its way; the park then cancels the hire or puts the employee back where it was picked up.
- **The staff windows.** A guest renames employees, sets and clears their patrol areas, sets the training budgets and dismisses employees; the windows show what the guest asked for until the host's answer arrives.
- **Clear refusals.** A guest without the right hears so before anything is sent; dismissing an employee another player carries says "That employee is being moved.", on the host's computer too.
- **Live acceptance.** A new `staff` scenario of the live co-op test hires, places, moves, renames, trains and dismisses a guard from a guest, with two resyncs forced along the way, on native and browser games.
