## Guests run the research lab and the money when the host lets them (October 27, 2026)

The host of a shared park can now give each guest the research and finance rights besides building ([COOP.md](../COOP.md), "Money and rights (C4)", "The rights dialog (C4b-1)" and "A guest's management windows (C4b-1)"; `[EXT:online-coop-permissions]`, `[EXT:online-coop-commands]`, `[EXT:online-coop-money]`). This is C4b-1, the first half of C4b; the attractions follow in C4b-2 and staff in C4c.

- **A rights dialog per guest.** On Build Together, a guest's button now names its rights ("Building, Finance", or "Watches"). It opens a dialog with one switch per right the host can give: building, research and finance. The dialog closes by itself when the guest leaves or the sharing ends.
- **The research lab.** With the research right, a guest's effort sliders, workload knob and project arrows act as the host's do. A drag sends its latest value once. The control shows what the guest asked for until the host's answer comes, then the park's value again. An arrow names the project its window shows, so two players never skip a project between them.
- **The money.** With the finance right, a guest can open and close the park, set the entrance fee, rename the park, and take or repay loans. A loan's confirmation waits for the host's answer and then closes, or says why it failed, just as the host's window would ("insufficient funds", "not available"). An accepted open or close gets the advisor's remark, as on the host's screen.
- **One queue for these requests.** A guest's management actions go to the host in order, one at a time for each part of the park, at most five a second, well inside what the host takes. A request the host could not take anyway says so, and the player tries again. When a right is taken back while an action is on its way, the guest hears that the host has not given that right, once.
- **Messages the guest can read.** A guest's note waits while a full-screen view, the object window or an opened message hides the message lines, and then shows for its full time.
- **No surprises for old logs.** The host's own records and every older session log keep their exact bytes. Only a guest's research project choices (and, from C4b-2, its upgrade choices) carry the extra "what I saw" fields, in a new version of the command format.
- Without a right, a guest's click still says that the host has not given it that right, and nothing goes to the host.
