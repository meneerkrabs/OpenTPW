## Every seat of a shared park keeps its own staff state (October 27, 2026)

The park now keeps the staff of every player of a shared park apart, ready for the guests' staff right ([COOP.md](../COOP.md), "Money and rights (C4)", "A seat's staff (C4c-1)"; `[EXT:online-coop-commands]`, `[EXT:online-coop-permissions]`). This is C4c-1, the first half of C4c; C4c-2 gives guests the staff tools and windows and lets the host give them the right.

- **A hire per player.** Each player's hire reserves its candidate, so two players never place the same one, and the pool keeps every reserved candidate until its player places it or lets it go.
- **One thing at a time for a guest.** A guest's new hire or pick-up puts back what it was holding, as the original's tools replace each other.
- **Carried employees.** An employee another player carries cannot be dismissed; dismissing the one you carry empties your hand. In a single-player game the carry tool of such an employee now ends at once.
- **Leaving.** A player who leaves, or loses the staff right, has their hire cancelled and their carried employee put back where it was picked up, on every computer alike, also when the host closes the shared park.
- **Saves.** A save made while a guest carries an employee stores that employee standing where it was picked up, while the shared park keeps it in the guest's hand.
