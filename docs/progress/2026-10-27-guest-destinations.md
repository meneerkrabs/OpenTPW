## Guests keep their destination, detour to a nearer ride and skip a ride without a front (October 27, 2026)

Fidelity round 9, package G. This read how tp.exe keeps and uses a guest's target (`0x4FCB10`, `0x500900`, `0x4FD570`, `0x4FFBC0`, `0x5012F0`, `0x502305`, `0x4FB360`), where a thing's access cell comes from (`0x4DB090`, `0x4DB33C`, `0x413410`) and what the edge test lets a walker enter (`0x4D8750`, `0x42A440`), and ported what it showed. GUEST-004, GUEST-005 and GUEST-019 are retired; GUEST-002, GUEST-006, QUEUE-003 and WALK-005 are narrowed.

- **GUEST-004, the target and the major destination.** The target `+0x1DC` is no longer cleared when a visit ends: it lives through the exit walk (state 15) and state 6 or 7 until the next major choice (`0x4FCB21`), so the removal of a ride now also reaches a guest that has just left it (the small happiness step, state 6). The leaving state's entry clears it (`0x502305`), as a held guest's does. A walk that finds the back of the queue moved and cannot reach the new one (`0x4FFE4A`) keeps the target too. The major destination `+0x1DE` is new (`Guest.MajorAttractionId`): the minor decision sets it to the old target, the exit walk takes it up again and walks to its back cell in state 10 ("Left minor destination, found old major"), and the removal of an attraction clears it from every guest.
- **GUEST-019 and GUEST-005, the access cell `+0x36`.** The constructor `0x4DB090` is the only writer of `+0x36`: the shape's first entrance cell, inside the footprint (the Jungle save's entry and exit words of all 11 placed objects equal the shape's cells, `SavedAccessCellTests`). The minor decision lists the rides on the cell their thing stands on (`+5`, `+7`) in the 4 x 4 window, measures the route lengths from the target's access cell and sends the guest to the best's access cell, where the guest stands in the footprint's entrance cell and then asks for the back cell again, as state 10 does. A guest's bin walk ends in the bin's own entrance cell. A world that keeps the cells it was given (a visit, Theme Park Inc) and the bridges without a placed object keep their entrance cell (QUEUE-010).
- **QUEUE-003, a ride without a front.** An entrance without a cardinal link bit has back cell 0 and size 0: guests neither score, choose nor join it, a guest walking to it falls to state 6 with its target kept, and its queued guests but the called one leave at the re-evaluation. The ride's saved queue gains the optional `ZeroFront` flag; a save from before loads with its old one-cell queue, which turns into the zero front at the next recompute.
- **GUEST-002.** The minor decision now scores the same candidates as the major choice (a disconnected real queue back counts, a ride without a front does not). The one shipped save with a queue holds the same back cell and size as OpenTPW computes; the 11 corpus saves place no ride. A stale cached back (`0x4DE130`) is not reproduced.
- **WALK-005.** The mask-2 rule for a type 9 target (the owner cell's ride must be rideable, `0x42A440`, with CannotRide 0) is ported (`ParkCellMap.EntranceRideOpens`), but its only callers in tp.exe are the camera's (`0x42C093`, `0x42C290`), so no simulated walker meets it. The underground link byte is read only inside the land tools' calls. Type 17 and zero-parent track records remain.
- **GUEST-006, the voice.** The needs-phase thought 4 plays cat_kids `0x85` on a thing id with its low nibble 0 (`0x4FCA6F`), a presentation event that draws nothing; the voice table gives the other two voices to thoughts 12, 13 and 15, which the pick never returns. The stale register text that said it was not played is corrected.
- **Determinism.** The pinned fixed run moves from `0x1AABD1698E2E75BE` to `0x5946C7CB09C6EEE7` and every pin that runs a park with it ([DETERMINISM.md](../DETERMINISM.md), "Guest destinations"). The cross grid pin of `PathBuilderTests` does not move. No draw is added or removed.
- **Saves and co-op.** Both new fields (`MajorAttractionId` on a guest, `ZeroFront` on a ride's queue) are optional and written only while set; old saves load. The command shapes are unchanged and every peer runs the same guest simulation.
- **Tests.** `GuestDestinationTests` (the minor decision on cell lists and access cells, the detour's walk into the entrance cell, promotion and its edge cases, removal, the saves, the zero front, the bin walk, the mask-2 rule), the updated exit, refused-target, pool and typed-front tests, and `SavedAccessCellTests` with game data.
