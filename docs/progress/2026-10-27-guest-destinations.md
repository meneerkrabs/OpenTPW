## Guests keep their destination, detour to a nearer ride and skip a ride without a front (October 27, 2026)

Fidelity round 9, package G. This read how tp.exe keeps and uses a guest's target (`0x4FCB10`, `0x500900`, `0x4FD570`, `0x4FFBC0`, `0x5012F0`, `0x502305`, `0x4FB360`), where a thing's access cell comes from (`0x4DB090`, `0x4DB33C`, `0x413410`) and what the edge test lets a walker enter (`0x4D8750`, `0x42A440`), and ported what it showed. GUEST-004, GUEST-005 and GUEST-019 are retired; GUEST-002, GUEST-006, QUEUE-003 and WALK-005 are narrowed.

- **GUEST-004, the target and the major destination.** The target `+0x1DC` is no longer cleared when a visit ends: it lives through the exit walk (state 15) and state 6 or 7 until the next major choice (`0x4FCB21`), so the removal of a ride now also reaches a guest that has just left it (the small happiness step, state 6). The leaving state's entry clears it (`0x502305`), as a held guest's does. A walk that finds the back of the queue moved and cannot reach the new one (`0x4FFE4A`) keeps the target too. The major destination `+0x1DE` is new (`Guest.SavedMajorAttractionId`): the minor decision sets it to the old target, the exit walk takes it up again and walks to its back cell in state 10 ("Left minor destination, found old major"), and the removal of an attraction clears it from every guest.
- **GUEST-019 and GUEST-005, the access cell `+0x36`.** The constructor `0x4DB090` is the only writer of `+0x36`: the shape's first entrance cell, inside the footprint (the Jungle save's entry and exit words of all 11 placed objects equal the shape's cells, `SavedAccessCellTests`). The minor decision lists the rides on the cell their thing stands on (`+5`, `+7`) in the 4 x 4 window, measures the route lengths from the target's access cell and sends the guest to the best's access cell, where the guest stands in the footprint's entrance cell and then asks for the back cell again, as state 10 does. A guest's bin walk ends in the bin's own entrance cell. A world that keeps the cells it was given (a visit, Theme Park Inc) and the bridges without a placed object keep their entrance cell (QUEUE-010).
- **QUEUE-003, a ride without a front.** An entrance without a cardinal link bit has back cell 0 and size 0: guests neither score, choose nor join it, a guest walking to it falls to state 6 with its target kept, and its queued guests but the called one leave at the re-evaluation. The ride's saved queue gains the optional `ZeroFront` flag; a save from before loads with its old one-cell queue, which turns into the zero front at the next recompute.
- **The queue tool and the entrance's link.** Laying the first queue cell of a ride now also links the ride's entrance cell back to it (the link writer links the first cell and the entrance both ways, `0x53525A..0x535366`), so a ride whose entrance lost its link when its queue was removed has a front again once a queue is laid. The sandbox smoke's tour ride is given its access cells and a path under its queue, as a player's placement would.
- **GUEST-002.** The minor decision now scores the same candidates as the major choice (a disconnected real queue back counts, a ride without a front does not). The one shipped save with a queue holds the same back cell and size as OpenTPW computes; the 11 corpus saves place no ride. A front the entry cell's link byte names counts even on empty ground, as `0x4DE130` does: the route request fails and the guest is stuck on the way (tested). `GUEST-002` is retired: the cached back and size (`+0x3A`, `+0x40`) are kept until a re-evaluation, the untyped host-fallback back is a host-model boundary (host harnesses, read-only visits, Theme Park Inc worlds) and the smarter-guests join-cell filter is the labelled extension's explicit choice.
- **WALK-005.** The mask-2 rule for a type 9 target (the owner cell's ride must be rideable, `0x42A440`, with CannotRide 0) is ported (`ParkCellMap.EntranceRideOpens`), but its only callers in tp.exe are the camera's (`0x42C093`, `0x42C290`), so no simulated walker meets it. The underground link byte is read only inside the land tools' calls. Type 17 and zero-parent track records remain.
- **GUEST-006, the voice.** The needs-phase thought 4 plays cat_kids `0x85` on a thing id with its low nibble 0 (`0x4FCA6F`), a presentation event that draws nothing; the voice table gives the other two voices to thoughts 12, 13 and 15, which the pick never returns. The stale register text that said it was not played is corrected.
- **Determinism.** The pinned fixed run moves from `0x1AABD1698E2E75BE` to `0x5946C7CB09C6EEE7` and every pin that runs a park with it ([DETERMINISM.md](../DETERMINISM.md), "Guest destinations"). The cross grid pin of `PathBuilderTests` does not move. No draw is added or removed.
- **Saves and co-op.** Both new fields (`SavedMajorAttractionId` on a guest, `ZeroFront` on a ride's queue) are optional and written only while set; old saves load. The command shapes are unchanged and every peer runs the same guest simulation.
- **Tests.** `GuestDestinationTests` (the minor decision on cell lists and access cells, the detour's walk into the entrance cell, promotion and its edge cases, removal, the saves, the zero front, the bin walk, the mask-2 rule), the updated exit, refused-target, pool and typed-front tests, and `SavedAccessCellTests` with game data.
- **Review follow-up.** An original save now imports both destinations: `mMajorDest` (`+0x1DC`) stays the target of a guest in state 6, 7 or 8 and `mSavedMajorDest` (`+0x1DE`) becomes `SavedMajorAttractionId` of every imported guest (the loader `0x4FB530` leaves both as read; states 11 to 17 are still dropped by SAVE-002, so a kept target of 15 or 16 cannot occur); the import reports the counts as evidence and is tested with synthetic saves (the 11 corpus saves place no ride). `Guest.MajorAttractionId` is now `SavedMajorAttractionId` (snapshot field included). The queue entrance resolver takes the shape's first entrance cell, as `0x4DE040` reads the link byte of `+0x36`; a corpus test covers all queued shapes of the four themes (72, none with several entrances) at four rotations. The removal message (`0x4FB360`) tests only `+0x1DC`, so a wandering guest (state 7) that targets the removed attraction now stops where it walks as a guest leaving a ride does.
- **Round 2.** The litter bin a guest last used stays its target (`+0x1DC`) after the walk (`0x4FFF20` writes no `+0x1DC`) until the next major choice, a bin search, the leaving state or a hold; the removal message hits every holder (small step, state 6, a walking guest stops where it stands) and an original save imports a bin `mMajorDest` for states 6, 7 and 8. The cached back (`+0x3A`) and size (`+0x40`) of a ride are kept until a re-evaluation (`0x4DE1F0`): the park turn's sync, a load and a saved cache keep them (optional `CachedBack` and `CachedBackSize` on a ride's snapshot, written only while they differ from the queue). The first-entrance scan (`0x413410`, columns outer) and the placement's last entrance or exit (`0x528A70`, rows outer) follow tp.exe's orders; no shipped shape has two of either. `ScoresDisconnectedQueues` is explicit in the smarter-guests policy.
- **Mono interpreter hazard (test side).** The browser pins (interpreter) failed in the projection helper of the guest tests: in a method called about 70 times (the interpreter's tier-up) a `finally` with a `for` or `foreach` loop over an array of value tuples overwrote the low half of the locals the `try` had assigned and the method returned (a `(ulong, ulong)` came back as `0x111122220000000B, 0x555566660000000C`; every return form, with and without Jiterpreter traces; AOT and desktop were right). The helper has no try/finally any more; `MonoInterpreterTryFinallyProbeTests` keeps the product-shaped controls (finally blocks that only call or subtract a field, as in the decisions and `ScriptHost`) hot, and no finally in the simulation code has the failing shape.
