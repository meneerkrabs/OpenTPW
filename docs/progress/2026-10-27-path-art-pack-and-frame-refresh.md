## Path art follows texture packs and the first suspended frame (October 27, 2026)

- The procedural path compositor invalidates its decoded CPU art after a completed texture-pack switch. A pack generation distinguishes replacement pixels even when their dimensions, path tile and cell word are unchanged; every path cell is resynchronized, so an offscreen cached cell rebuilds when it returns. Cancelled/replaced switches do not announce completion, and deleting the terrain unsubscribes its cache.
- An unreadable or malformed replacement is diagnosed and keeps that tile's previous art, like the GPU switch, without blocking other tiles or later subscribers. Each texture is attempted once per completed generation and retried on the next one; an explicit missing-art result removes it rather than reviving older pixels. Permission/security failures and out-of-memory errors are not converted to stale or missing art.
- The lazy static ground and path batches are prepared before the level snapshots its entities. Entering a camera mode that suspends procedural texturing, including the first frame after path edits, therefore includes the replacement batches before hiding the procedural cells. Queue models and object-floor composition are unchanged.
