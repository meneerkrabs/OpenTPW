## Round 10 quick ports: saved message tube, staff variants and creation draws, Ctrl copy (October 27, 2026)

Fidelity round 10, package Q. The tpw-decomp research only pointed to where to look; every behaviour below was read in tp.exe at instruction level and is tagged with its tp.exe address.

- **UI-025, the message tube is saved with the park.** The tube holds ten rows and refuses an eleventh without eviction (`0x48EB20`, cap word `0x750610` = 10); the register text still said five with eviction and is corrected. The tube is now an optional, validated `MessageTube` part of the park-resume save (`0x48EE50`): plain messages, challenge offers (their Accept and Decline actions are rebuilt), progress rows and terminal rows with their timers (the elapsed time, so a loaded terminal expires when it would have). A save without the part loads with an empty tube and a park without messages saves the same bytes as before. OpenTPW writes its own JSON part and does not import an original save's tag block (UI-025 stays narrowed: offer removal, the timer queue, other action-bearing sources).
- **QUEUE-010 narrowed.** PR #876 had already ported the removal message's semantics (`0x4FB360`). The last relocation left for a guest whose attraction is gone (a safety net, since the original has no such poll) now also stays where it stands in state 6. Only the bridges without placed cells (gates, test rigs, Theme Park Inc) keep the front-cell relocation.
- **UI-042 narrowed.** The arm drawer `0x4A14F0` places the panel at the arm's left less the panel's own width and a horizontal gap of 50, centred vertically on the arm, and moves it right by a tenth of (the HUD window's right - the arm's left + the panel's width + 100) each step. The register no longer says the HUD's width or a vertical 50. The end of the slide and the vertical place stay the table's until the two windows' runtime rectangles are read.
- **UI-024 narrowed.** `0x4CD880` was already ported (the mean over the 3 x 3 cells, the unsigned clamp of the high edge); the text keeps only the thing's byte pair and the low edge's wrap (not reproduced).
- **GUEST-014 retired (one pin moves).** The person serializer `0x4F8B10` saves `mSpriteID` and reduces it by the category's bank count on load, so a member of an original save now keeps his sprite variant (`(SpriteId mod banks)`); the name-and-id key only serves OpenTPW park saves from before the variant was kept.
- **DET-015 narrowed.** No staff constructor reseeds: `0x4F7B7E` is the online avatar's constructor `0x4F7B20`. A hired member now takes the base constructor's base-speed draw (`0x4F89E1`, R mod 5 into {60, 80, 100, 120, 140}) right after its thing id; the entertainer constructor then fixes the speed at 60 (`0x4D4340`) and the guard constructor draws its own sprite variant (`0x4D5E02`). A pool-less economy takes no draw. The constructors' role searches (`0x4D46D0`, `0x502600`, `0x4D5E68`, `0x4D7100`, `0x4DA5B0`) still run on the member's first update, a turn later than the original's, and stay the open part of the entry. The pinned fixed run does not hire staff and does not move.
- **SAVE-922 and SAVE-923 retired.** Header `+28`/`+32` are the station's placement cell (`0x466F90`), stored by `0x4368F0`, handed by the loader to `0x436AC0`, which never reads them, and written back by the saver (`0x4374F7`): dead data. The train words `+0x38`, `+0x64`, `+0x68` are the sound state and handles of `0x439780` (the loader restores them raw, `0x4383FE`), and car `+0x44` is the seat share `0x43BA90` computes (the loader runs it without the share pass), which the import's seats per train reproduce; no coaster sound is played here, so nothing reads them.
- **Ctrl and a click copies an object.** The click dispatcher `0x4879D0` (mode 4) tests Ctrl: without it the object's window opens, with it the object's type record is read, a balance below the price plays cat_ui `0x1D` and arms nothing, else the mode 4 build tool of that Info id (`0x46C5A0`, as the buy row does after the same comparison at `0x4AC6C7`) is armed at the object's own angle (`0x52F1B0`) and no window opens. A guest of a shared park is asked for its rights at the placement, as for every placement.
- **Register text.** ECON-022 no longer says guests do not turn to watch performers (they do, `0x4FEFF4` with the state-14 test `0x4FF04A`, as GUEST-010 says); ARRIVAL-006 now says the game loop catches up at most 2000 ms in 31 ms ticks (`0x54F493`, `0x54F4C4`), takes a turn on every 8th tick and runs at most 3 per pass (`0x54F668`, `0x54F680`), so a pass can drop more than 3.
- **COMPAT-032 narrowed, not settled.** The binder `0x575C60` has one branch that builds a texture from an image record with the entry flags `0x1000240` (alpha type `0x200`, blend with an alpha test at 0x10/255); whether the sign slots take it depends on bit `0x80` of their flag dword, which the shipped models set or not and the sign builder does not. The question is left open.
- **Tests.** `MessageTubeSaveTests`, `StaffCreationDrawTests`, `StaffVariantTests` (the saved variant, with the corpus), `AttractionRemovalGuestTests` (the orphan exit) and three Ctrl-copy cases in `ObjectRayPickTests`.
- **Pins.** The determinism hash of the fixed run (`0x5946C7CB09C6EEE7`) does not move. The one pin that does is the Instant Action economy digest of `BuildStepsTests`: `E19E5729D4A8EE5A` to `4DCABA51A693C7D7`, because the seed's imported staff now carry their saved sprite variant in the park save (the Full Simulation digest `D2F1349AA44D6545` has no staff and is unchanged).
