## Track edit and station as tp.exe clears and samples them (October 27, 2026)

Package R9-F. The kart and water track tools clear, cut and replay cells with the original's clear op, the vehicles sample the owner object of each probe, the station placement lays its exit and entry tiles as the original's op list does, and the edit hover is drawn as the original draws it. TRACK-001, 004, 005, 007 and 018 are retired.

- **TRACK-004 retired:** `ClearCell` is the full port of `0x53B280` (pair clear `0x522730` + link `0x522850`, forced, pass (-1, -1) and line-step forms, the exit tail with its non-track branch, replay flag `0x81B46C`).
- **TRACK-007 retired:** the demolition and Backspace replay the corner stacks of `0x530830` and `0x530B70` line by line (`0x52FE50`, mode `0x35`) with the station repair after every pop and the last-line flag (`0x530005`, `0x52FF73`). A junction a chain passes keeps its other arms, a full crossing takes the line step's rule. Backspace clears the station tiles a line covers, and a stack that ends at the exit keeps it twice (the zero-length line is taken back with the step (0, 0)).
- **The exit globals:** `0x7639E0/E4` is the exit tile for the line tool, Backspace and the demolition and the ride's anchor cell during the edit tool's hover (`0x527A17`), so a cut can clear the exit tile and its repair does nothing; the repair `0x52FC80` fixes that tile (not the first station tile) and has its corner flag tail (`0x52FD94`). The undo command carries the last-line flag (`UndoTrackLineCommand`, one more byte on the wire; the golden pins are updated).
- **TRACK-018 retired:** the tool stack holds 1025 points (`0x52FBF0`) and a full stack lets the tool go on (`0x5252D9`); the hover marks the pick (m_break), the cut path (m_erase) and a refused tile (red) with one 20-unit face per tile at the greatest raise of its four cells (`0x536CA0`) with the walls of `0x5370E0`, its faces glide behind the cursor (`0x53C3F0`, `0x53DF30`) and every red face blinks (hidden two frames, shown seven). BLUEPRINT-003 is narrowed to the other track symbols and the arrow models.
- **TRACK-001 retired:** a cell without a piece (no track, a station tile, an add-on tile) is sampled through its owner object (`0x452A60`) and the vehicle keeps its height without one. The Halloween and space stations read their faces (node translations baked), the directed edge gate runs in host float (equal to the x87 sequence at control word `0x7F` for normal-range values), and the instance mesh (mode 4) is the faces of the ground node the type loader's search chose (`0x46A5A0`, the existing `ObjectHoardingGeometry.SelectGroundNode`).
- **TRACK-005 retired:** the placement runs the station line, then 0x87, the type op, 0x80, 0x82, 0x83, 0x85, 0x86, 0x81 on the exit and on the entry. The exit's arms depend on its type op; the entry's arm and the first station tile's arm back are written whatever the entry's type op answered; the tiles are free but refused while the cash is below zero; op 0x87 demolishes the objects standing on their cells.
- Determinism pins are unchanged. No save format change; the park command wire gains one byte for the undo's last-line flag.
