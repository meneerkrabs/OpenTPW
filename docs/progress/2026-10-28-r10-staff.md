## Staff constructors, claims, bank limit and the hire figure (October 28, 2026)

Round 10 package C reads the staff constructors and their neighbours in tp.exe and ports them.
The tpw-decomp research only pointed to where to look; every behaviour below was read at instruction level in tp.exe.

- **DET-015 (narrowed).** The five role constructors (0x4D4340, 0x4D5D40, 0x4D6B60, 0x4D9EB0, 0x502600) run the role's first search (0x4D46D0, 0x4D63D0, 0x4D7100, 0x4DA5B0, 0x502C70) right after the base constructor's draws. `PlaceStaffCommand` now runs it through `StaffHands` and `Level.StaffWorld` right after the put-down (`StaffWorldSimulation.Constructed`), so its draws come at the click and the first update goes on from the chosen job instead of searching again. Headless economies keep the first-update search. Still open: the researcher constructor's last call 0x5041F0 (a flag byte and message 5 or 6, which the advisor's subscription 0x599EDB receives; no draw) is not posted: a lead for the advisor package.
- **SAVE-006 (narrowed).** Every staff claim (an attraction's claimant word and turn, 0x4E01F0) of an original save is imported as saved, whoever the claimant is; a claimant the import does not hold is a negative word (allocated to nothing). The lazy query 0x4E0220 (`ParkEconomy.Claimant`) lets it lapse 100 turns after its stamp. A stamp after the save's turn is taken as made on it. What stays open: the saved open word, the sign lines other than the gate's and the raw links.
- **GUEST-015 (retired).** The performance's script is 13 + (r mod (n - 1)) from the search's second draw (0x4D47B5..0x4D47E4); the draw is kept per member (`StaffMember.PerformanceDraw`, saved only while set) and `StaffSpriteRules.Perform` follows it.
- **GUEST-017 (narrowed).** The burst's first frame already waits for the next due script pass (0x4FE950, 0x475B80, strict test 0x475360, created invisible 0x4758F0); the declaration now names only the host clock and SAVE-013.
- **GUEST-020 (narrowed).** The folders handymen, mechanics, guards and researchers stop at the limit of 0x41AA40: 1 bank for the preset's `GameOptions.NUMKIDS` 0 (low.sam), else 2; banks sorted by name (0x541210), entertainers unflagged, loaded once per program run. `StaffSpriteSet.Load` takes the limit from the current preset when a level builds its staff; a variant draws its bank modulo the loaded count, so the simulation does not depend on a local graphics setting. The same word limits the kid sets to 2, 4, 6 and 8 (0x41A9D0), which is not ported yet.
- **UI-082 (narrowed).** The hire figure follows the native phases of 0x49BA00 and the close 0x49B7B4: a new role or variant starts a figure that shows nothing before the first due pass, an applicant the pool does not return keeps the figure, the close ends it.
