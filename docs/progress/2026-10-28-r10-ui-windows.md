## UI window fidelity from tp.exe (October 28, 2026)

The HUD arm's drawer geometry now comes from the runtime rectangles tp.exe uses (arm procedure
0x4A14F0, HUD table 0x752940): a slide-out panel starts at the arm's left less its width less 50,
is centred vertically on the arm, and moves by the truncated integer step per timer tick for ten
ticks plus a finalizing tick. The camera panel and the message panel share it, and the arm art is
drawn under the HUD (UI-042 narrowed: the camcorder arming and postcard remain).

Message tube offers keep a 90 s timer, stay when read and are declined when removed (UI-025).
Ride and shop object states 2 and 3 are stored in the economy record rather than derived, and a
requested upgrade overwrites a breakdown as the original does (ECON-023). The lost-park overlay
audit found no economic guard in tp.exe (ECON-030). The local happiness walk wraps on the 16-bit
cell id (UI-024). UI-941 is retired (the lobby generator is seeded from the clock); the 12 year
graph's 144 samples are the ring's months one to one (UI-060).

Message-tube saves keep offer rows (the offer kind already existed; the schema is unchanged). The roster membership (GUEST-920), the moon billboard (UI-107), the model objects'
messages 0x80 and 8 (UI-914, UI-064) and several other rows stay narrowed; see the PR for details.
