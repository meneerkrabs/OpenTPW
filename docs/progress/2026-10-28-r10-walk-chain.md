## Walk chains, wander links and the sub-turn draw (October 28, 2026)

Round 10 package A reads the walk chain, the sub-turn interpolation and the wander
candidate routine in tp.exe and ports them. The tpw-decomp research only pointed
to where to look; every behaviour below was read at instruction level in tp.exe.

- **WALK-003 (retired).** One chain per map cell holds every thing, guests and
  staff interleaved by entry time, head insertion (tp.exe 0x4D91F0, 0x50B6A0).
  Positions change only in the walk step and the teleport (0x4FA2A0, 0x4FA930);
  riding and carried guests keep their last walked point. Staff share the chain
  through `StaffCellChain`; both sides take entry numbers from one counter.
- **GUEST-016 (narrowed).** People are drawn at the per-frame lerp between the
  turn-start point (+0x190/+0x194) and the current point over 248 ms
  (0x4F9F00, 0x4FA030, 0x518F90); speed smoothing `(3*old + sum/100)/4` with the
  x87 24-bit rounding and the x99/100 boost decay (0x4FA870). The sprite clock
  remains open.
- **GUEST-010 (retired).** Wander candidates come from every link bit of the
  cell with the four removals and the original's counter quirk (0x4F9490,
  0x4F97E3, stranded 0x4F9D64); proven against the real routine in the x86
  oracle with a C# golden replay.
- **WALK-010, ARRIVAL-001, GUEST-007, GATE-006** narrowed where the cell chains
  settle them; GUEST-007 stays open on what reads the advice record's id and cell.

Finding: following the original's links faithfully, a guest can stand on a link
that leads to bare ground (7 of 188 path links in the original Jungle save) and
strand. The 120-minute Jungle run shows no visible regression.

Pins (largest move of the round): the fixed run `0x5946C7CB09C6EEE7` moved to
`0xB4AEC10551DF2738`; every other pin, with old and new values, is listed in
docs/DETERMINISM.md. Saves: the optional `StaffActorSnapshot.CellSerial` is new;
old saves load.
