## Advisor inputs: the last nine scan callbacks and the researchers' flag (October 29, 2026)

Round 10 package H reads the inputs of the nine advisor callbacks that were still blocked and ports them. The tpw-decomp research only pointed to where to look; every behaviour below was read at instruction level in tp.exe.

- **ADVISOR-009 (narrowed).** The nine blocked scan callbacks are ported (`AdvisorInputsCallbacks`): 12 (hire guards after kids chased), 19, 21 and 27 (patrol coverage of the mechanics, handymen and entertainers), 22 (build a camera) and 98 (lax security) after kids that got away, 113 (a queue that joins no path), 141 (the rides are too exciting) and 264 (many pranksters). Every computed callback of the 155 scanned descriptors is ported now; the other 21 scanned messages are the original's constant-zero stub.
- **The chase counters (0x4C74F0).** A guard's chase (0x4D6790) raises the analyser's month and lifetime words when the kid is caught (0x4D691B) and also the got-away word when the chase is given up (0x4D6A1F, the one tail every way out reaches); the month close zeroes the month and got-away words (0x4C79EB). They are `ParkEconomy.MisbehavingKids` and `MisbehavingKidsEver`, saved with the park (`ParkSaveFile.Data.AnalyserChase`) and read from an original save (`SaveAnalyserCounters`).
- **The patrol coverage (0x4CD500).** The rectangles of a role's staff mark the cells (0x4CD340); the four classes count the land, the built cells, the entrance cells and the path and queue cells; the percent is marked x 100 / counted (`ParkEconomy.PatrolCoveragePercent`). Handymen and entertainers need one of the role, no minimum and no area test; the mechanics need the minimum and an area.
- **The queue and the excitement.** 0x4CCB00 takes the first (newest) choosable queue ride that `ParkGate.QueueConnected` calls unconnected, the selected ride left out (`Level.FirstUnconnectedQueue`); 0x4CCBF0 caches for 40 turns the mean of the low byte of each ride's live excitement (`ParkEconomy.AverageRideExcitement`, not saved).
- **DET-015 (narrowed).** The researchers' flag 0x5041F0 runs at a researcher's hire and removal (`ParkStaff.LabActive`, `LabMessage`): the ResearchLab byte +0x1468 (the saved `mLabActive`) and message 5 or 6 on a change, no draw. Only the advisor subscribes (0x599EDB) and only to 5; its handler (0x59B060, case 5) reads an advice record out of the plain message, so it takes the poster's stack frame (message id -1) for one. Whether that record enters the queue is not traced, so the reaction is not ported and stays in ADVISOR-009's evidence needed. The headless economy's first-update search remains.
- **Unread.** The subject word (+4 of the scan object) that 113 and 141 write reaches only the queued advice record's builder (0x59B6BE); its readers belong to GUEST-007 and it is not kept.
- **Determinism and saves.** The advisor stays presentation state and no pin moves: the chase words are read only by the advisor, so the optional `AnalyserChase` field of the park save (a save without it starts at 0) is left out of the run hash like the analyser's caches (docs/DETERMINISM.md); the excitement cache is not saved.
