## Coaster presentation follow-ups (October 30, 2026)

- **The track mesh reads its cross section from the original's run table (COAST-026 narrowed).** The builder `0x4402D0` steps the sample coordinate in 0.25 and writes a run at every change of the select answer (`0x43E9C0`, now with the speed, direction, pitch and bank windows of the sam's selects), with the transition sections of a type whose sections blend: the Jungle flume's dry and wet sections blend their points over the run's length (`0x43FD60`), the slice lists hold the run keys (`0x43F030`) and the previous ring is emitted again when the run moves. The run table is compared bit-exactly with isolated tp.exe runs in the Unicorn oracle (ORACLE X42, `CoasterRunsGolden`, 60 rows including the merge of two blends that meet). Chains with no sample list here (cut open by demolish) keep the per-segment pitch selection. The pylons' clips now also add their texture-coordinate keys (`0x4745C0`), so a lofted pylon's texture coordinates run past 1 along its height: slots with the wrap flag repeat, the others clamp as tp.exe does everywhere (`0x575E9C`, `0x567620`; most PC pylon slots clamp).
- **The bend car's tables are compared with tp.exe (COAST-034 narrowed).** `CoasterCarBend.Tables` writes the rear count and the yaw, pitch and elevation entries of `0x438AB0` bit for bit (ORACLE X43, `CoasterBendGolden`, 120 rows: the angle lerp `0x43E480` and the PC acos at single precision were off by an ulp before). The sockets of riders stay unbent (the bend writes only the body mesh's vertices), and no PC bend car has a vertex beyond its tables. Open: the flume boats' own clips (no clip start behind the bend flag was found) and the culling bounds a bend type widens.
