## Audio: the pause's sound side, the group enable and the Doppler reading (October 31, 2026)

- **The pause's sound side (UI-096 retired).** A game pause (the pause menu and the
  screens opened from it, the map, the postcard, the message boxes, the P key) now
  pauses the advisor's speech and lifts the listener 10000 units, so every
  positioned sound goes silent while the flat voices and the land's ambient
  rectangles do not (`0x4092A0`, `0x598960`, `0x51C1C0`, `0x51C1D0`). The window
  losing focus pauses the whole mixer instead, in a park (`0x46B74C`) and in the
  front end (`0x46B70A`), and the resume undoes what the last pause did, quirks
  included (a focus loss over a P pause leaves the listener up until the next
  level start). `GamePauseSound` and `ParkHud.ObservePauseSound` carry it;
  `AudioMixer.Pause` and `AudioVoice.Paused` keep the samples where they are.
  Docs: "Pause sound" in `docs/AUDIO.md`.
- **The group enable (AUDIO-038 narrowed).** The sound service's on-flags (music,
  speech, effects) now stop the voices of a switched-off group and start them
  again from the beginning when it is back on, with fresh draws, as the pool tick
  `0x6BC760` does; a chain makes no child while its group is off. The flags come
  from the options screen's OK, leaving a park and a save's Sound block, not from
  a toggle, which sets only the volume. The look-ahead of a stopped sentence is
  read in its structure (blocks of `0x2400` bytes, `0x6BB510`, `0x6BB590`) but not
  to its depth, which is QMixer's mixing lead.
- **Doppler (AUDIO-018 narrowed, still off).** Read again, no gate found. New in
  the register text: the channel class's vtable confirms that a recompute ends in
  the update `0x18005830` and the frequency stage; `0x1800bd40` is the
  end-of-stream test, not a readiness gate; `0x6C64F0` copies a loop count and
  gives no non-loop route; and the library's volume goes through the table
  `0x782e40` (100 is 0.787).
- **The volume chain (AUDIO-018, review).** Every voice of the sound event system
  now takes the original's chain in place of the master and channel gains: its
  percent times the group floats of its mask (`0x6BB860`, rounded to even), the
  driver's table (`0x6D2980`: 258 times the index, 32767 for 127; an index
  outside 0..127 sends nothing), QMixer's `/32767` (`0x18007A1C`) and one
  decibel quantisation of volume times distance gain (`0x1800586B`,
  `0x1800CA40`; flat voices use distance gain 1). A voice at 100 percent is now
  0.787 before the quantisation, about 2.1 dB quieter than before, and group
  volume changes move the index at the next mixed block.
- Round 2: the advisor's clip is played as the event voice it is in tp.exe (volume 100, mask 0x10005), the speech duck truncates the option times the duck percent as the sound service does, and a channel whose first index is refused holds QMixer's default 10000 / 32767.
- Review fixes: P ends a focus-loss pause in one press, a park drops the front
  end's focus latch, the pause object is reset between tests, and the smoke now
  requires the pause menu to have paused a running advisor speech.
- No pin moves; saves are unchanged.
