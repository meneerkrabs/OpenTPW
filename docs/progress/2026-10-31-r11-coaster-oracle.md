## Coaster rating pass, train placement and ride motion follow tp.exe (October 31, 2026)

- **Rating pass, step and final.** The coaster's rating pass (0x43c450), its step (0x43dc50) and final (0x43df90) now run in the order tp.exe's x87 code takes at the thread's 24-bit precision, with no fused multiply-adds: the force is a x v x 0.1, the speed step multiplies the height difference by the modifier and the per-height factor before the add, the step sums the vertical and depth force first and adds the inverted term u where the Mac added a stale force, the final keeps a negative zero and the train count divides by the release rate times 0.001. Compared bit for bit with 840 isolated tp.exe runs in the Unicorn oracle (ORACLE X46).
- **Train placement and motion.** The placement after the pass (0x43c1f0 and the layout) and the motion tick (0x43ce20: car points, the start and half point of each car, the braking blend, partial hops, the collision test, the bumped flag) are single-precision ports too, and the trains wrap by the controller's end coordinate (+0xa8) instead of the sample count. Compared with 400 placement and 150 tick oracle rows.
- **Validator, link test, collision keys.** The placement validator's turn angle and distance window (0x434740) are single steps (1,488 oracle rows including the verdict at limits a few ulps from the angle). The link test skips a pair whose end node shares its cell descriptor with the other link's first node or the reverse (0x439c5f..0x439c81; the descriptor is the cell's pylon column, so stacked pylons share it), the `asTrackCollisionPoints[i].fCollisionX/Y` keys are read, and the four unread loads (0x430867, 0x43420a, 0x443087, 0x44309c) are a position pair of a pylon control or cell descriptor, not the collision list.
