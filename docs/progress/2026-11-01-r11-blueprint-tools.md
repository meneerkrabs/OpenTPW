## Blueprint and build tools (November 1, 2026)

Round 11 package F reads the build tools' ghosts in tp.exe (REA, `dis.py`, the x86 oracle; the Mac decomp only pointed to the code) and replays the walkers `0x536100` and `0x53AAC0` against the ports (ORACLE.md X50 to X52).

- **BLUEPRINT-001 (narrowed)**: the raise of a marker is ported for a typed TPW world: the ghost cell routine `0x532FC0` takes 10 x ((int)H + 9) / 10 of the cell lookup `0x452AE0` for a map type 4, 7, 9, 10 or 30 and a record type 0xB, 0xC, 0x10, 0x11 or 0x19, and the tile ghost's `0x536CA0` takes the last writer of the tile's four cells; the walls follow from the neighbours. Left: untyped harness worlds, Theme Park Inc and the ground of a fence post's own cell.
- **BLUEPRINT-003 (narrowed)**: a path or queue line cell takes the validator's answer (`m_link`, `m_end`, also for a cell that already holds the line's type, `0x535670` after `0x5358B5`), the kart and water lines and the Buy Land fence draw the tile chooser's `0x539760` tiles (blue, red, `m_end`; the fence over four edge passes), a footprint's track end gets `m_direct` and the kinds '@', '+' and '#' no front arrow. `garrow.MD2` and `rarrow.MD2` are never drawn by tp.exe. Left: the refused access markers' wider cases, the area clear's edge cells and `m_inout`.
- **UI-100 (narrowed)**: the track chooser's end word `0x816D4C` (answer 0xB at `0x53A1C6`) and cash word `0x816D5C` (test `0x539F95`, write `0x539FB6`) now give the kart and water lines their end and cash pointers; only the crosshair art (`CCro.tga`, absent from the game data) remains.
- **UI-031 (narrowed)**: the pass 0x81 returns 0 for a line whose last cell met an existing path or queue cell (`0x5340A5`, the flag `0x820ABC`, which `0x5345AD` sets only while the last-cell word `0x820AB8` is nonzero (the gate at `0x534584..0x534593`) and the cell is a path or queue cell), which takes `0x526568` (the handover to the queue tool while the queue start `0x8186C0` is pending, else the tool ends at `0x5266BC`); any other result falls to `0x526527`. The original's coaster placement enters the queue tool; its track tool (mode 0x15, coaster sub-mode 3, `0x497D20`) is entered by the ride window's track button (`0x4AF200` to `0x4AF275` to `0x4988C0`), by a click on the coaster in the park (class 6, `0x487E56` to `0x487EEB` to `0x4988C0`, while `0x441910` says the track is not complete) and by the coaster window's button 0x1F (`0x498333`); OpenTPW still enters it at the placement. A tool armed from the buy list calls no angle setter (`0x4AC683`) and so starts at the angle the placement kept. Left: porting the coaster flow (a design decision for the user) and the path-start queue cell.
