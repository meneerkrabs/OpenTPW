## Lobby key panel and the lobby advisor read (November 1, 2026)

Round 11 package V, lobby part, read in tp.exe (REA, `dis.py`; the Mac decomp only pointed to the code).

- **ADVISOR-025 (narrowed), PTCL-908 (lobby part retired)**: the lobby's key panel is ported as the static reading has it (`FrontEndMenu.KeyPanel.cs`, `LobbyKeyPanelEffect`, [the contract](../reverse/LOBBY-KEY-PANEL.md)). The key frame `s_key` (EA) and its cost digit `s_X1` (EB) are gold while the player's keys meet the selected island's `global.sam` `Keys.CostToEnter` (Jungle 1, Halloween 1, Fantasy 3, Space 5) and grey below it (frame 0 and cost - 1, or frame 1 and cost + 4: `0x4B9340`), the screen-space effect `0x61` runs while they do (created once, ended below the cost and with the lobby), every island change repeats that step with the stored count (`0x4B90F0`), and the counter's icon is at the frame the creation sets (3, `0x4B8D65`), with `"%d x"` written only for keys above 0. The panel's remaining callers are the lobby advisor's timer and the dialogs that hide and re-create the window, which OpenTPW does not have; `0x4B8EE0`'s show branch has no caller.
- **ADVISOR-005 (narrowed)**: the original runs and draws the advisor in every frame of the front-end state, lit by the active lobby screen's ambient (black by the lobby scene, 0.267 grey by the island screens, 0.125 grey by the globe) and with the fog on for the island screens and off for the globe; which lobby screen each writer is, and that every screen shows the advisor, are read ([the account](../reverse/LOBBY-ADVISOR.md)). Not ported, as OpenTPW has no lobby advisor; open: whether the advisor's materials take the fog bit.
- **Lobby sequences 28 and 29**: only sequence 28 is reachable in the lobby, through response 393 (`0x4A6B4F`, when the first player's dialog closes after `0x5AFC30` added the first key); the idle lines (394 and 395 after 90 s, `0x5E1830`) and the dialog lines are generated or plain. Sequence 29 is the park's golden ticket lines.
- **Open for a decision**: the lobby advisor itself (a talking advisor in the lobby, about 600 to 900 lines, with the first-key line tied to New Full Simulation's starter key).
