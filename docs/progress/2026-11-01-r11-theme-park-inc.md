## Theme Park Inc readings, round 11 (November 1, 2026)

- **Theme Park Inc's guest price test (ECON-062 narrowed).** The routine `0x6120C0` of the no-CD Theme Park Inc `Game.exe` is ported (`ThemeParkIncPriceTest`) and replayed on the executable in the x86 oracle (finding X62, 490 cases, no mismatch; 98 rows pinned in `ThemeParkIncPriceGoldenTests`): a ride, shop or sideshow with a price is refused when the price lies above the guest's willingness to pay (`Info.ExpectedPrice`, scaled by needs, `RipOffOK`, happiness and the rating byte's fit to the guest's expected byte) or above its purse, the perception statistics are recorded (category 10 for a sideshow, 1..4 and 6..8 for a shop) and the attraction's cheap, expensive and declined rings rise. Theme Park Inc's own shops and sideshows no longer go through Theme Park World's formula (115 percent, sideshow addend). Left: the rating byte's coaster and ride-handle branches and the guest type draw.

- **Theme Park Inc's month message order (ECON-060 narrowed).** The month message reaches its listeners in thing-id order (a per-message-type ordered set, walked in order), and the prebuilt park's ids are staff HQ 1, mechanic HQ 2, advisor 3, tag system 4, park analyser 5, research lab 6, weather 8, bank 9 and the attractions 14 and up: the bank's whole month handler (`0x5C0B10`: batch credit, the eight loan instalments, the red-month test) therefore runs before any object's debit and month roll. OpenTPW's Theme Park Inc month end now does the same (it paid the running costs between the batch credit and the instalments, and rolled the object months before the red-month test). Left: the park analyser's handler (id 5), which still closes the ledger after the objects, and the monthly rings' readers.

- **Theme Park Inc's weather (ARRIVAL-008 narrowed).** The object `0xA06558` that the arrival routine reads is the startup dialog's theme selection (Arabian 0, Science 1, Water 2, Intro World 3; the radio group `0x498940..0x4989CA`, the setter `0x42A360`): mode 3, the Intro World (`levels/wIntro`), has no arrivals, so the guest simulation takes an `IntroWorld` gate (no OpenTPW level sets it: the front end draws no Intro World). The three words after the Seasons rows are read: the settings loader writes the array count (4 for the files' four rows, `0x419FB4`) and the online-only keys stay 0 offline. Left: the weather thing's own per-turn effects (sky quality, drops, lightning, thunder); no shipped object sets `ClosedDuring*`, so the attractions' weather closing (`0x5DEEBF`) has no data to run on.

- **The umbrella day gate's age (UMB-003 retired).** `Game.exe 0x611050` subtracts two 32-bit world turns (the world object's turn and the guest's creation stamp `+0x1FC`, which the constructor `0x60C589` writes once), cuts the difference to a multiple of four and converts it at 3750 seconds a turn; the gate, the rain mood and the cold rule now read that integer difference (`Guest.DisplayTimeInParkTurns`, which GUEST-920 keeps) instead of the floor of the seconds. Seven extension pins move (docs/DETERMINISM.md); the fixed run and the brolly shop pin do not.
