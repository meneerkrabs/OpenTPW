- **GUEST-006 (retired)**: `0x50BE80` skips the bubble work while a bit of `0x16` of the camera word `0x790AB0` is set. All 85 references to the word were read: bit 4 is the ride camera (`0x42AB20` mode 4, called at `0x42A6BA`; the test `0x42A410` beside the followed ride `0x790A44`), bit 2 the walk camera (mode 2, `0x42AF26` from `0x42AE70`), and no store through the word's address sets bit `0x10`. A park saved after that (variable 2) now marks the show spent, as the destructor `0x4DD0A0` reads it (row 7 only while the variable is below 2).
- **ECON-022 (narrowed)**: the stranded stamp (`+0x198`) is a guest field: the wander's dead end (`0x4F9D77`) sends the staff (kinds 4 to 8) to the patrol area search `0x506F30` and stamps no one but a guest, and both callers of `0x4FA670` clear it, so the staff are never stranded (no change, now recorded).
