## World presentation (November 1, 2026)

Round 11 package O reads the world's presentation routines in tp.exe (REA, `dis.py`; the Mac decomp only pointed to the code) and ports what it finds.

- **RIDES-032 (narrowed)**: the drawn reset of the list pass's return (`0x44E380` -> `0x4726D0`) is kept until the next writer, as tp.exe keeps it: the reset changes the instance's own state words and nothing puts them back, so a node-state bit, a texture slot's frame or a vertex pose stays as the reset left it until a toggle track's sample, a key or a bind writes it (`ObjectAnimator.BeginWrite` / `EndWrite`; saves, picks and the hash still see the simulation's own state, so no pin moves). The render callback belongs to the whole mesh object (`0x57A750` calls it before any mesh test, `0x57A7E6`..`0x57A7FC`): a part offers it before its own hidden and view tests and the object is in view when any of its parts is (`OriginalObject.PresentIfInView`). Left: the original's scene pass offers the callback to the objects of the terrain cells the view covers (`0x576910`), which is coarser than the parts' view bounds.
- **PTCL-908 (narrowed)**: the first-draw beam 15 is every vehicle's (`0x549DB0` sets flag `0x4000000` at `0x54A009`, the per-vehicle draw `0x546280` clears it and raises the beam at `0x546A89`..`0x546AC7`, no family check), raised in the tick's pose phase and not at creation; boats and bumper cars beam too. A guest made off the park cells beams at creation (`0x4FAEC0` at `0x4FB26A`: every arrival vehicle's guest), a guest boarding the arrival vehicle beams as it goes (`0x500BD0` state 21, `0x500C35`) and a dismissed employee beams (`0x505790`, `0x5059C3`): particle 19 at the thing's place (`0x4FA470`). The 25-guest population (`0x516700`, caller `0x516791`) belongs to the online chat handler. The scoped message after the build effect (`0x51C640`) is read to the sound library (a four-float record in a list sorted by its fourth float, `0x6C49B0`); its readers are not.
- **PATH-015 (retired)**: the queue models' animation clips are loaded and played in the original, so they are here: the loader reads the `m` member of every model (the letter table `0x6FE6BC`, group 5; `0x4623A2`, `0x4623D1`), the creation flags `0x33A` leave the idle restart of `0x473C70` armed, and every frame an idle channel starts group 5 (`0x473CAB`): fresh the first time from the none animation 12 (`0x472FBB`), later restarts keep the overshoot as a carry (`0x47300E`, `0x472C12`), so a cycle is exactly the clip's duration. The fire0 to fire04 torches of queend and quebin1/2 and Space's scrolling coordinates now play, cells made in one frame share one player and one dynamic batch (`QueueClipPlayer`, `QueueAnimationGroup`: a Space queue of 1,024 cells went from about 24 ms to 18 ms per frame and from 224 to 23 drawn parts in an offscreen run), and the cells a path edit remakes (a cell that appears or goes with the 3 x 3 cells around it, the 2 x 2 blocks of the walk `0x5311C0`; a cell that only turns alone) start again as a group of their own (`QueueBirths`; presentation only, nothing in a save or a hash). The two `quedead` rows (flag word `0x100`, flags `0x112423`) register their instances for the colour pulse (`0x469580`, `0x469840` from `0x54E301`): one colour for every vertex, red full and green and blue `255 * triangle(x)` with `x = fmod(x + 2 dt, 2)`, lit by nothing, so a dead end blinks between red and white once a second (`QueueModelPulse`, `TextureSlotAddressing.PulseFlag`, `ModelEntity.PulseTint`, `test.shader` and its web build).
- **MD2-901**: already retired (`[EXT:original-undefined-reads]`, #921); nothing to do here.

No saved field changes and no hash input changes: the first-draw state is in no save and no hash, the pulse and the drawn reset are presentation. Old saves and command logs load and replay as before.
