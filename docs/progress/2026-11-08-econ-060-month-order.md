## Theme Park Inc's month order: the analyser closes before the bank (November 8, 2026)

Theme Park Inc delivers the month message to its listeners in **ascending thing-id order** through an id-keyed tree walked in order (`0x436DD0`, successor `0x407EA0` → `0x437EF0`), and the level setup `0x648070` gives the analyser id 5 and the bank id 9, with the model-3 objects from 14 up. The analyser's handler `0x5AF220` therefore runs before the bank: it closes its rings, samples the bank balance through `0x5C0740` (`return [bank+0x14]`) and zeroes the accumulators, all before the bank credits the batch and takes its instalments and before the objects debit their running costs.

OpenTPW closed the ledger and sampled **last**, so a month's batch credit, instalments and running costs were counted in that same month instead of the next. `ParkEconomy.EndMonth` now routes the analyser block through `CloseAnalyserMonth`, called last under Theme Park World's rules and, under Theme Park Inc's, after the staff headquarters' own month work — staff HQ is thing id 1, ahead of the analyser's 5, so wages, training and the strike check keep their closing month while the analyser's close and sample precede the bank's credit (id 9). It samples the bank balance there instead of `NetWorth(0)`; the twin handler `0x004C7720` in `tp.exe` reads a different getter (`0x6AD810`, `[park+0xC]`), which is why the value is rule-dependent.

`ThemeParkIncClosesTheAnalyserMonthBeforeTheBankCreditsTheBatch` pins both directions: the batch appears in the month that opens under Theme Park Inc's rules, and in the closing month under Theme Park World's.
