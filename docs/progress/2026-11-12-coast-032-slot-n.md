## COAST-032: the slot-n copy is not a divergence (November 12, 2026)

The entry listed three things that were not compared with an oracle run. Two are rendering-only: the posed track tags of a pylon model and the cars' curve point and pose. The third — a car or release walk that reaches slot `n` past the sample list, where the original reads the sampler's pre-rating copy of the last sample (`0x441783`) and OpenTPW reads the rated sample — is not a divergence at all.

The copy is made before the rating pass, and the only field its reader takes is the **position**, used in the car point's lerp (`0x43cf6c` for the current point, `0x43d10d` for the half-sample point). The rating pass rewrites speeds, forces and flags but never a sample's position: no assignment to a sample's position exists anywhere in the coaster path. So the pre-rating copy and the array's last sample carry the same position, and `CoasterRide.Slot` answering with the last sample is the same answer.

That leaves COAST-032 with its rendering-only items, which need a loaded model in the oracle rather than a port change.
