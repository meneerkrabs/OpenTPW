## Theme Park Inc Science continuation and year-end bank report (November 17, 2026)

The Theme Park Inc calendar dispatches the month message at `0x60859B` before the year message at `0x6086B3`. The analyser closes December before the bank posts its batch credit and loan instalments, and before the attractions pay their running costs. The bank's year handler at `0x5C0A36` then clears its signed profit accumulator.

OpenTPW's Theme Park Inc `YearEnded` event now reports the bank's profit and balance after those December postings and before the reset, instead of deriving annual profit from the analyser's closed months. The analyser history and Theme Park World reporting are unchanged. The regression exercises a December close with a pending batch credit, a live loan and attraction running costs.

The host economy snapshot also now keeps the Theme Park Inc rules profile, booked monthly object costs, the plant script condition, price counters and the active and pending weather spells. Restoring it into another edition is refused. Missing weather fields and malformed cost or counter values are rejected. The desktop Science pause menu can save and continue a solo park in a separate OpenTPW slot. Loading validates the assets and builds the candidate before replacing the live park. The save preserves whether a ride was closed by the weather and which spell has already been delivered. A TPI save without a tour controller does not create one on load: the legacy repair consumed five extra draws in the Science smoke. `.tpilayout` still starts a new session, and no native save import or export is enabled.

The tests compare continued finance and weather against uninterrupted runs across month boundaries, including a Science economy built from retail data. The offscreen desktop smoke exercises the pause-menu save, cancel and continue actions, checks that a malformed save does not replace the live park, and compares the resumed guest and economy states after 180 fixed ticks with uninterrupted play. The same smoke returns to the Personnel File, verifies that **Load A Game** is disabled without a slot, rejects a corrupt slot without leaving the menu, then resumes the saved Science park with Polar Zone selected. A separate two-process smoke saves a played park, exits, then starts a new process at the Personnel File and verifies that loading restores the guest and economy digests. The pause menu freezes simulation without discarding the selected speed, and Science continuation does not repair missing queue links during registration. Theme Park World saves omit the new fields and retain the existing rules and year report.

Thirteen synthetic native replays verify the profile (type, generation, starting cash, exit counter, preferred excitement and boredom threshold), starting needs, the selected prank code and the generator state at both boundaries. Six further cases verify the thing-ID reseed and the ordinary and forced appearance paths. The isolated SAM loader verifies the guest-profile settings bindings. Native prank actions and the remaining arrival behavior are not enabled by this change.
