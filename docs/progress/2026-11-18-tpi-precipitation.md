## Theme Park Inc precipitation counts (November 18, 2026)

The Theme Park Inc weather state now advances its precipitation count on each park turn and preserves it on Science continuation. Rain and storms use the rain quality endpoints and snow uses its own. A dry spell clears the count immediately. Twenty-one isolated native cases cover the target calculation (`0x642A80`) and the per-turn update (`0x642230`), including the strict step boundary and the difference between the clamped renderer count and the raw audio request. These updates consume no random draws. The desktop slice now drives both original rain voices: the weather thing's flat ambient sentence in rain and storms (`0x642F40`), and the global level-driven sentence with parameter 8 (`0x657200`), which also follows the drop count in snow. A silent mixer test exercises the voice lifecycle, and the retail-data test verifies the event and its sample bank. The spell-start fixtures also verify modes 0 (rain and storm), 1 (snow) and 2 (off), and fall speeds 2.5, 4.5 and 0.8 for rain, storm and snow respectively. The simulation now takes the original world-generator draw at each spell start and stores its new wind angle in Science saves. Earlier saves without an angle start from 90 degrees. Seven additional native fixtures cover reflection at zero, the remainder at 360, the clamped force and the exact angle/force pair passed to the renderer at `0x642D45`, with identical results at all three tested x87 precisions. Twelve native cases also verify the normalized fall vector (`0x6EA9A0`) at both 24-bit and 53-bit x87 precision; several of the results differ. The desktop renderer now draws ordinary rain and snow using TPI's original textures, six-unit field, wind and spell speed. Three additional native cases verify the scroll and parallax handoff at `0x6ED800`. OpenTPW uses the 24-bit hardware reference for the fall vector; this does not identify the runtime precision of every original driver. The silent Science smoke checks rain and snow submissions and verifies that dry weather submits none. The spline snow branch at `0x6ECC50` and browser presentation remain separate. Separate storm helpers match nine native timer cases (`0x6422FE`) and eight strike-coordinate cases (`0x642550`, at both tested x87 precision settings). The timer keeps the existing thunder position when thunder is pending and then skips its re-arm draw. A strike uses four coordinate draws, one countdown reset draw, and a sixth draw only if thunder is armed. The geometry helper leaves the precision setting explicit; these tests do not establish which display mode selects which setting. The storm helpers are not connected to the live park or renderer. The TPI sky remains separate work.
