## Theme Park Inc storm lightning and thunder (November 18, 2026)

The weather thing's storm timers now run on the park turn with the precipitation step, drawing from the world generator in the native order. The four controls come from `levels/science/Standard.sam`: `WeatherEffects.LightningCountdownSpeed` 10, `LightningCountdownFrom` 500, `ThunderStartsThisFarAway` 10 and `ThunderRandomiser` 2, the same numbers the executable holds at `0x0A0252C`–`0x0A0253C`; `LightningTime` 2000 sets how long a bolt is drawn. A strike's geometry spans the map's cells (`0x642550`), the thunder keeps the bolt's top until its countdown reaches zero (`0x6423AB`), and both are saved with the weather as an optional `Storm` record that older saves simply lack.

The desktop slice draws the bolts through the existing weather renderer's `lightning.tga` ribbon and plays the ambient thunder event at the bolt's top. `--debug-tpi-storm` puts a Science park into a storm for observation and is deliberately not combined with the slice smoke, which asserts a dry park.

Verified: a storm test drives one turn through the wired path with a scripted draw sequence (four geometry words, the thunder countdown's and the re-arm's) and checks the bolt, the round trip and the presentation-only strikes; 401 tests across Theme Park Inc, weather and audio pass with the retail data (6 skipped). In a running retail Science park the storm produced five bolts with thunder at each of them, e.g. a bolt from (106, 355) to (82, 300, 341) and its thunder, from the park's own timers.

The thunder's event is the ambient 0x20 in Theme Park Inc too: `0x64241C` pushes 0x20 with the converted top point, so reusing the Theme Park World event is not a substitution. The strike coordinates use the same 24-bit hardware reference as the fall vector, which does not identify the active control word of every original driver. The weather draw picks a white tint on one branch (`0x6EC606`, `+0xA19C = 0xFFFFFFFF`, `+0xA1A0 = 4`) and otherwise a grey from `rand() & 0x3F` ORed into the colour (`0x6EC5D2`), then draws through the textured-quad routine at `0x6EC260`; the vertex record it builds carries state `0x9A1`. That is a tint choice for the drawn quad, not an established full-screen flash, so no flash is claimed for Theme Park Inc. A natural storm spell is now covered too: with storm-only seasons the roll produces the spell, its start makes the state a storm and the following turns strike and swing the thunder countdown to zero, as one test asserts. The visual storm was started through the debug restore.
