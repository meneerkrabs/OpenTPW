## Theme Park Inc: the "Game Over" letter loses the park (November 21, 2026)

Theme Park Inc's bank letter and its challenge firing letter are the game's two ways to lose, not just text. Both reach the tag system as a type-25 notification (ids 8 and 7), which builds a record with the subject `TAG_SYSTEM.str` 165, "Game Over - You Lose!", the sender row's name and title (173, 174), the player's name, the park date and the body 166 or 167 with a signature (`0x5CEF20`, `0x5CD0D0`, `0x5CD14D`, `0x5CEF60`, `0x5CD1C0`). It then opens the letter window on it: the layout table `0x9B4854` (builder `0x526CD0`) with To/From/Subject/Date fields, the sender's mugshot and the body frame. Closing the letter with any control but help (`0x526960`) calls `0x649E60` with reason 0 (the bank's category `0x5E`) or 1 (the challenge system's `0x5F`). That routine writes the lost world state 4 (`0x649E92`) and opens the loss window `0x50AFC0`, Theme Park World's loss scene with the caption "BANKRUPT!" or "Fired!" (UITEXT 921, 922). The earlier reading that a Theme Park Inc park is never lost had mistaken the letter window's message 5 for a level teardown's event.

OpenTPW now opens the letter window over the Theme Park Inc HUD in place of the advice-line caption, and closing it loses the park (`ParkEconomy.LoseThemeParkIncPark`, `ThemeParkIncLossReason`). The window's labels are dark blue and its values black, as the builder sets them (`0x84A6BB`); the body keeps the text class's own white (`0x5123A3`). The date is the park turn's date as day.month.year through UITEXT 956 (`0x6089C0`, `0x7D91E0`, `0x518C30`); the addressee is the Personnel File's "New Player" (`[EXT:no-player-profiles]`). A saved lost park now reloads lost, captioned as a bankruptcy because the reason is not saved (`0x64C2EC`, `0x50B1DE`). The loss scene follows in the Theme Park Inc HUD (`ThemeParkIncHud.Loss`): the gate's camera seat under the caption, centred on the canvas in font slot 0 and white (`0x53EE30`), with no animation. Escape, or a right click more than two seconds in with `RMBCancelOn` (`0x50ACE0`), leaves it for the hidden-HUD overlay, which keeps the caption. The overlay's Escape opens the lost park's four lines, Load Game, Resume Game, Select A New Player and Quit Game (UITEXT 768, 771, 774, 772; `0x50D3F3`), centred as a block. That menu pauses nothing, and the P key cannot pause a lost park, though it still resumes one that was paused when it was lost (`0x42A17C`, `0x42A27C`). A reloaded lost park goes straight to the overlay (`0x50B1C0`). The seat routine's extra writes and a few closed panels are not ported (ECON-030, whose text now describes the letter path and the scene).

The challenge firing line is corrected. A career starts at a fixed 5 (`0x74BDD1`, rank 1). Only a promotion raises it, to that rank's `FiringLineForRank[n].Limit` (`0x74C450`), so retail rank 0's 255 is never a starting value. Only a challenge's first loss takes one off (`mChallengeLost`, `0x5C4857`), and every loss with no line left posts the firing letter (`0x5C48B6`). The whole challenge system is now traced in `docs/reverse/TPI-CHALLENGE-SYSTEM.md`: ten parallel slots, a grace state, offers every `DaysBetweenOffers` with no randomness, no decline and no time warning. The level's `ChallengesInThisLevel` values are row numbers of `Challenges.sam`, not types. Its port is still to come.
