## Theme Park Inc: the mission window and the "Objective Complete!" letter (November 21, 2026)

Theme Park Inc's "Current Objective" window is ported from the executable (`ThemeParkIncMissionWindow`; builder `0x55A7F0`, procedure `0x55BA70`). Its layout table `0x9BE808` (34 controls) and the Challenges tab's own table `0x9B54CC` (30 controls) are now generated by `tpi_exe_layouts.py`. The window is letter-like: the title, the Objectives and Challenges tabs, the mission's `OPERATION.str` title and picture (`0x9BECCC`), Mr Maybury as the sender, a briefing page and a details page. Details are in [TPI-MISSIONS.md §7](../reverse/TPI-MISSIONS.md#7-the-mission-window).

- **Briefing.** Mission 0 shows `MISSION_DESC[0]` with its first statistic's target ("get 40 people"). A later mission shows `MISSION_DESC_GOTPROMO` when its required rank is already held, except mission 2, and `MISSION_DESC` otherwise. Mr Maybury's signature follows.
- **Details.** Every exit statistic gets its requirement and status line through the table `0x9BED18`. The page also lists the promotion line or its hint by counter and zone, the required challenges, the golden tickets, the research lines of an unresearched obstacle and the zone hint in another zone. It keeps the original's quirks: a removed obstacle leaves the last text with one more line feed, and a counter without a hint repeats the requirements heading.
- **Proceed.** It is enabled when the controller's MetRequirements holds. It posts message 0x24 for an obstacle mission or runs OnMissionComplete otherwise, then closes the window. Four seconds after the first opening every tick refreshes the window; only Close and Cancel stop that. The window pauses nothing.
- **Opening.** The key table's Objectives key, a plain O (`0x9A8A0C`), opens the window, and so does a text-HUD button that stands in for the native HUD's (`[EXT:tpi-text-hud]`). The first briefing's opening after the zone-arrival camera flight belongs to the flight's own package.
- **"Objective Complete!" letter.** The letter `0x5CEAE0` is composed with TAG_SYSTEM 168, `OBJECTIVE_COMPLETE[completed]`, the signature and the go-to target of `0x5CED9C`. The letter window's go-to button is enabled by a target and opens the mission window or the challenge panel. The original files this letter as a tag-tube row rather than opening it (`0x5CD060` accepts kinds 6 to 8 only), and the slice has no tag tube, so the controller's letters still wait.

Not ported: the body's scroll text, the interface sounds, the advisor panels the window opens and closes, the Proceed sparkle, the staff list's rows (the staff HQ's slots are not modelled), the native challenges window, a letter go-to's camera move and kind 0x12.

Verified: the Theme Park Inc Science smoke (`--tpi-smoke-test`) opens the window with the Objectives key, finds mission 0's Proceed disabled and closes it. The new tests cover the generated tables, the briefing rule, the details lines, the hints, the obstacle lines, Proceed per branch, the refresh timer, the letter and its go-to on synthetic strings. They also check the English retail texts and a headless draw over the real models with `OPENTPW_TPI_PATH`. `tpi_exe_layouts.py --check` matches, and the register check passes with 76 open IDs. The original game was not launched, and no binary, data or disassembly is included.
