## Theme Park Inc: entertainers and guards (November 22, 2026)

Every role of Theme Park Inc's staff pool can be hired and dropped now. The Entertainer and the Guard run Theme Park Inc's own state machines, and the guard's chase and first aid are not ported.

- **Entertainer.** States 0, 1 and 0x17 (idle, walk, performing) follow `0x5D1E20`: the idle voice and the idle end, the walking voice and walk step, and the performance with the work duration (`Entertainer.MinWorkDuration` 10 to `MaxWorkDuration` 75) and sound 0x87 at its end. The search (`0x5D1A30`) takes the gate, the tired test, the radius (always `MaxActiveRange` 5), a world draw divisible by 3 and a guest in the clipped square (the finder `0x5B1B20`, kind 1); the tail idles on turns divisible by 4 and patrols otherwise. His setter (`0x5D1D30`) draws once from the world whatever the state; for a performance the draw's remainder by the bank's performance count, the park's theme index (`arabian`, `science`, `water`, `wIntro`) and his look pick template 0x13 to 0x16, which are four new sprite programs of the table `0x9CDA94`.
- **Guard.** States 0 and 1 (idle voice 0xA7, patrol voice 0xA6, the decision `0x5D3C10`: the gate, then the patrol on three turns in four, else idle) and the training states; he learns ability 9. The setter `0x5D3E70` clears his speed bonus before the common setter. His start draws the world once for his sprite variant (`0x5D3253` → `0x6902F0(0xB)`, `(R >> 2)` modulo the banks), ignoring the candidate's byte; the entertainer keeps it.
- **Cell effects.** The constructors put the entertainer's row 0 and the guard's row 3 down on their cell, and the pre-updates (`0x5D1960`, `0x5D3B40`) move them when the cell changes, both times with the radius the member's experience gives (`MinActiveRange` 2 to 5, `MinDetectionRange` 5 to 10) through the region writer `0x5D7770`, which takes the radius as an argument. The guests' needs phase reads the grid, so the entertainer cheers the cells around him. A removal with a larger radius than its add leaves its difference in the grid; the guard's security row shows it, and the park save keeps it as the environment residue.
- **Sprites.** The entertainer is drawn from his theme's folder of `esprites.wad` (two banks each for `Arabian`, `Science`, `Water` and `wIntro`), the guard from `Generic\Guards`. The staff renderer now draws every role but the researcher (it skipped the gardener too).
- **Saves.** The Science continuation save keeps the performers' state, the performance start and the cell effect (cell word and radius). A Theme Park Inc save made before this is refused with a start-again message before its assets are checked (the standing refuse-and-document policy for older Science saves, not a fidelity necessity), and a save holding a guard in a chase state, a claim by either role or a patrol area smaller than the map is refused.
- **Texts.** The Staff panel's note and the slice's limits line are updated in all 11 languages; the role-refusal line is gone (every role is hired).

Not ported, and why: the guard's chase, carry, after-release walk and first aid (states 0x1A, 0x1C, 0x1D, 0x1E) are entered from the staff HQ's prank message and a first-aid loop and decide on the guests' own Theme Park Inc states (the catch test `0x614720` reads the guest's `+0x2C0`; the first aid's patient is a guest in state 0x1B), which a park's guests (Theme Park World's rules, with Theme Park World's state numbers) do not carry. The release points are known (`KidRelease` at `0xA02638`, the entrances at `0xA025D0`) but the states stay out until the guests' states are ported; `[EXT:tpi-capability-gates]` covers this. Also not ported: the guests' turn towards a performing entertainer (`0x614D41` in the guest's update, `[EXT:tpi-twp-guest-rules]`, amounts unread), the effect's removal by the destructors (staff cannot be fired yet), the visual spawn of `0x60B700` (presentation, no draw) and the patrol area `0x6256A0`. The two roles' shared helpers (`SearchPeriod`, `IdleTerm`, the working step) stay at 53 bits, as the gardener's; no new formula of theirs has a float. No `rand()` call is reached (the world draws include the guard's variant draw), and no label or approximation is added.

Verified with these checks:

- New tests for the theme table and template choice, the shipped settings and banks, the hire and the rows on the grid, the effect's move and the residue a larger radius leaves, the search's draws (one for the `% 3` test, one for the setter), a performance and its end, a tired entertainer, the guard's decision, the ported states, and a save round-trip with the residue, both refusals (an older save, a world save before its assets) and the invalid saves; the Theme Park Inc, save, staff and resume filters pass with the retail data.
- An x86 oracle (`tools/ppc-analysis/oracle_x86/tpi_performer_oracle.py`) runs the executable's pre-updates over the real region writer, the entertainer's search and setter and the guard's decision natively: no mismatch.
- The silent, offscreen Science smoke hires an Entertainer and a Guard, drops them in the park, sees them updated with a sprite frame showing, and finds their rows on the grid.

The original game was not launched, and no binary or data is included.
