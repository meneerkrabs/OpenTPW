## Theme Park Inc: the tag tube files the letters (November 22, 2026)

Theme Park Inc's letters now land in the tag tube, the game's mail list, and the player can read them in its "Message Centre" window.

- **Rows.** The tag system files every letter the slice posts as a row, as the row builder does (`0x635D80`): the zone arrival's first briefing (`0x5CD6E0(0x5C, 5, 1)`, now composed: "Welcome!") and each "Objective Complete!" letter (`0x5CEAE0`) are unread rows; the bank's red-month letter and the challenge system's firing letter are filed read, and their letter window still opens at once (`0x5CD060`). The objective letters no longer wait in a list nobody reads. Game inserts go to the head and can supersede an older row, rows expire by their own timers and the player's delete takes effect at the tag thing's next pass (`0x636880`, `0x636A40`, `0x635AB0`).
- **Unread counter.** The counter keeps a total and four categories and doubles its threshold (3, 6, 12, ...) each time the total reaches it (`0x54F1A0`, `0x54F320`).
- **The window.** Table `0x9BBC84` is generated by `tpi_exe_layouts.py`. The window lists the rows by date, newest first (the header buttons sort by subject or turn the direction), shows the selected row's sender, addressee, subject, picture and body, reads it after 2.5 seconds (or when another row is shown or the window closes after half a second), deletes it with Delete on the next tick and runs its go-to. A click on a row never opens the letter window: the original's only caller of that window is the row builder. It opens with the M key (`0x42EED0`) and a text HUD button that stands in for the native mail panel and shows the unread count (`[EXT:tpi-text-hud]`). Opening it closes the HUD's other window, and a letter or the mission window closes it.
- **Senders.** The play senders are the mission controller (`0x5EF991`), the arrival's end (`0x5F18DA`), the bank (`0x5C0969`) and the challenge system (`0x5C48DD`); the wage demands and training letters come with the staff work. The letter posts in `0x535xxx` and `0x538030` are buttons of the debug menu (`0x531BC0`, opened by HUD control 0x4F and F12): not ported, and the `rand()%200` at `0x5382A2` is that menu's test wage demand, not a play path.
- **Saves.** The Science continuation save keeps the tube's rows (each row's letter, numbers, read flag and date); a load appends them and recounts the unread ones silently, as `0x6355D0` does. A Theme Park Inc save from before the tube is refused with a message before its assets are checked.

Labels: `[EXT:tpi-capability-gates]` now also covers the advisor's unread-mail messages (0x198, 0x15D) and the go-to hint (0x211), whose descriptors are read but whose responses and speech choice are not, and the native mail panel's picture and chime. `[EXT:tpi-text-hud]` covers the window's HUD button. No new approximation or label is added, and no CRT `rand()` draw is reached.

- The silent, offscreen Science smoke completes a mission, sees the objective letter filed unread without a letter window, opens the tube with M, selects the letter, reads it at its deadline, deletes it and closes the window.
