## Theme Park Inc: the game thread's 24-bit x87 precision (November 22, 2026)

Theme Park Inc's economy, staff and arrival routines are now ported at the precision the original's game thread really runs at. `Game.exe` asks DirectDraw for `DDSCL_FPUSETUP` (`0x6BF33F`, `0x6BF5A8`), so the Direct3D device sets the x87 control word to `0x7F` (24-bit significand), as in Theme Park World. A static read of the executable finds nothing on the game thread that puts 53 bits back: `_fpreset` and `_control87` have no caller after the start-up, the renderer saves and restores the word around each frame, and its extended-precision restore needs a render-state bit that nothing sets. The earlier "53 bits" convention for these routines (`[BIN:TPI-EXE:0x5BCD60 precision]`) is withdrawn; the rounding is covered by the existing `[EXT:hardware-d3d-path]`.

- **Switched to single-precision steps** (`ThemeParkIncX87`, which rounds an exact integer-and-single result once): the staff wage review, daily experience gain, moving and working step, rest and meter add; the handyman's and gardener's search period and every role's idle wait; the shop cost of goods; the arrival group; and the scrap percentage of a plant. The guest base speed (#975), research, ride wear, plants and weather were already single-precision.
- **What a retail park sees.** With the shipped `Arrival` words a group is one guest larger without weather and in rain and snow (100 × 0.01f rounds to 1.0). A plant whose script condition is 0 sells for 101 percent (3 × 33.333332f rounds to 100.0). A staff member with 34000 of 100000 experience searches every 33rd turn instead of every 32nd. The staff meters move only in their last bits.
- **Smaller fixes on the way.** A staff meter whose sum is not a number is stored as 0, as the original's unordered compare does; the research lab's cash and cost products round from the exact integer; the arrival keeps the low 32 bits of a group of 2^31 or more, as `_ftol` does. The rounding helpers take allocation-free paths for the usual small operands.
- **Not switched.** Theme Park Inc's loan offers share Theme Park World's loan arithmetic; the shipped offers are the same at both precisions.

Verified with these checks:

- The x86 oracles now run the routines at `0x7F` with `0x27F` beside: `tpi_staff_formula_oracle.py` (now also the idle term and the search period; 2000 cases, no mismatch at either word), `tpi_goods_cost_oracle.py` (400 cases each), the new `tpi_scrap_percent_oracle.py` (416 counts) and the arrival target X7 (532 inputs; the oracle's Theme Park Inc tests pass). The price, candidate, weather-roll, performer, advisor-forecast and theme-engine oracles give the same answers at `0x7F`, and the price golden is unchanged.
- New tests pin the native answers at `0x7F`, among them the cases where 53 bits differ (`ThemeParkIncX87Tests`), the arrival and the plant's scrap percentage; the Theme Park Inc, save, Theme Park World staff, guest, save-world, original-park and simulation filters pass with the retail data, and the Science smoke runs.

Theme Park World's routines and pins are unchanged. The original game was not launched, and no binary or data is included.
