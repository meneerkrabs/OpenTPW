## Theme Park Inc: wage demands, the wage negotiation and strikes (November 23, 2026)

Theme Park Inc's staff now demand rises, the player negotiates them, and unhappy staff go on strike in the park.

- **Wage demands.** A member's demand on his demand day is filed in the tag tube as an unread "Urgent Wage Demand" from Miss Moody (`0x628CA0`, `0x5CEFB0`), replacing the HUD note; an older demand of the same member goes. The training letters (finished, suspended) are tube rows too.
- **The wage demand window.** The row's go-to opens the window (table `0x9C9F64`, generated by `tpi_exe_layouts.py`, whose parser now reads the varibox's layout command 19) with the member's statistics and an offer box that starts at the demand and steps by `WageStep` between `MinWage` and `MaxWage`. OK negotiates the offer, the close and cancel buttons an offer of nothing, and the fire button dismisses the member (`0x59A900`).
- **The negotiation.** The results dialog (table `0x9BE518`) answers with the decision `0x559E00` (the sums, then the single-precision ratio against `100 − PercentageForBadWageDecision`); closing it raises the salary by the offer, or, after a refusal, by the demand ("Accept the demand") or dismisses the member ("Let them leave").
- **The dismissal** (`0x629040`) pays one more salary, clears the strike when he was its only striker, deletes his tube rows and destroys his thing: the staff HQ, the research lab and the objects he claimed let go of him, and his claims and cell effect go.
- **Strikes.** The staff HQ's month consideration now runs in the park (before the paydays). A role on strike walks to the strike area (`0x62BBA0`, four world draws), stands there (states 4 and 5, all six roles, the strike template) and walks back to the entrance when the next month ends the strike or the park is closed and empty. The verified expected-wage expression matches the port's.
- **Saves.** The Science continuation save keeps the strikers, the strike records and the demand and training rows (`TpiWages`); a Science save from before is refused before its assets are checked.
