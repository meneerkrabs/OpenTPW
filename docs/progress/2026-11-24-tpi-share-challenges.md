## Theme Park Inc: special rides and the first share-prize challenges (November 24, 2026)

Theme Park Inc's special rides now await their activation, and the first share-prize challenge types are measured. These are the steps toward the last mission, miss17, which needs 51 % of the company's shares from thirteen share-prize challenges. Three of them, the Science rows 8, 14 and 22 (2900 shares), can now be offered and won in play.

- **Special-ride table.** A placed special ride (`Info.IsSpecial` 1) gets a record of the staff its `SkillSet[n].Num` keys ask for (`0x623DC0`). A member whose training ends takes a slot that asks for his staff type and new ability (`0x623FB0`), and leaves it when he goes to train again (`0x6241B0`). The prebuilt Science park's four records are imported as saved.
- **Activation.** Selecting a special ride that awaits its activation shows its staff slots, the golden tickets it needs and the note on undiscovered abilities, in the game's own words, with an Activate Ride button. The button is enabled once every slot is filled and the career holds the ride's `UsageInfo.GoldenTicketCost` (`0x6243C0`). It spends the tickets, marks the ride activated (`mBeenActivated`) and empties its record (`0x5E8230`, `0x624280`).
- **Trigger.** The share challenges' availability reads the target ride's activation, or the research lab's current project while the ride does not stand (`0x5C3828`, `0x61E6A0`: slot 1 counts only when slot 0 holds no project). Type 46's trigger reads the park analyser's breakdown count, which is never raised in play (`0x5C3927`).
- **Types.** 17 (riders on a special ride), 46 (the worst ride's repair, `0x5B7D30`), 52 (riders and the ride's repair) and 58 (riders as a percentage of the guests in the park) are measured. A guest boarding a Theme Park Inc ride counts for 17, 52, 58 and the first value of 57, as the native guest's admission does (`0x61A77E`). A win's share prize reaches the shares game.
- **Saves.** The table and the activated rides go into the Science continuation save; a Theme Park Inc save from before them is refused before its assets are checked, and a Theme Park World save that carries them is refused.

Labels: `[EXT:tpi-twp-guest-rules]` covers the admission events on the boarding of Theme Park World's guest, which the native guests' state 20 will take over. `[EXT:tpi-capability-gates]` now also covers the trigger's branch for a target that is not special, the activation's camera fly-over and track tool, the advisor's lines when a ride can be activated, and the share types still unmeasured: 48 and 56 (the mood rings), 50 and 55 (the guests' top three thoughts), 53 (the track-ride rating), 51 (the Arabian palace coaster, a special ride no OpenTPW world places) and 57 (the photo booth's users). `[EXT:tpi-text-hud]` covers the activation lines and button in place of the native activation window. No new approximation or label is added, and no CRT `rand()` draw is reached.
