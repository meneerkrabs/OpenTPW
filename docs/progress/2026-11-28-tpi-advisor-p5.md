## Theme Park Inc: the advisor's second event senders (November 28, 2026)

The fifth phase of Theme Park Inc's advisor port ([TPI-ADVISOR.md](../reverse/TPI-ADVISOR.md) §9.3). The advisor now hears the staff, the research lab, the bank, the strikes, the rides and their mechanics, the special rides and the lake's camera flight, and it answers the game messages it subscribes to as the original's handler does.

- **Staff.** A wage demand, a finished training and a cancelled one are said. When a mission's requirements are met, the mission status refresh says so (the second mission and later; the first waits for the first-hour clock), after the controller's status broadcasts, a finished training and the mission window's close.
- **Research.** A finished project says its category's advice, with the researched object; an upgrade project asks whether a mechanic can install it.
- **Bank and strikes.** The sixth month in the red is said before the bank's letter; every strike warning, strike, settlement and return to work names its staff role.
- **Rides.** A ride about to break down, one that broke down without a free mechanic, a condemned ride and a mechanic's claim are posted with the ride. Their Message Centre rows carry the ride's or the object's name, and a mechanic who starts the repair clears the ride's notices.
- **Special rides and the lake.** A special ride whose staff are complete says it can be activated (the Science water ride has its own line), and the lake's flight says its advice.
- **Review follow-ups of the previous phase.** A challenge without its sender's advice is refused when `Challenges.sam` or a save loads; a missing or damaged advisor schema is refused when the advisor is built; the advisor settings are their bound words; the producer inventory became test data and no longer ships; the co-op notes list the challenge accept's and the mission window's cancels; the oracle counts its cases.
- **Still gated** (`[EXT:tpi-capability-gates]`): everything that needs the first-hour clock (the guest messages, the tutorial tips and the first mission's line, P7); the research group levels; the annual report; the park door the slice does not have; the online and mode-3 games; the presenter window; and the world view's tools and windows.

No new label or approximation is added, and no CRT `rand()` is reached. The original game was not launched, and no binary or data is included.
