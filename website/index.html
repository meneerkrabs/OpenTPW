OpenTPW

A new engine for Theme Park World, the 1999 Bullfrog game. You bring your own copy; OpenTPW runs it on Windows, macOS and Linux, or in a browser tab.

Play in the browser Download

Free and open source. Needs the files of an original installation or CD. Why?

Now playableWhat you can do today

Build a park and fill it

Pick one of the four worlds in the lobby, lay paths and queues and place the original rides, shops and sideshows. Guests come in through the gate, queue, ride, eat and go home again. Money, loans, wages, research and staff run on the game's own settings files.

Build roller coasters

Place pylons from the station, raise them with the mouse wheel and close the circuit. Track shape, cost, rating and train motion follow the original code. The track itself is still drawn as a plain band, and go-karts and water rides are not done yet.

The advisor talks again

Original speech with lip sync, for the game events traced so far, such as a new park, Instant Action and bankruptcy. The park plays its own music and moves to a busier section as guests arrive.

The original interface, sharp

The lobby, HUD, buy window, finance, research, staff and map screens are built from the game's own models, fonts and strings, in English, Danish, Dutch, French, German and Swedish. Any resolution or aspect ratio works, and the interface scales without blurring.

Four worlds

  • Lost Kingdom
  • Wonder Land
  • Space Zone
  • Halloween World

Rendered by OpenTPW from the original game data, with the optional enhanced texture pack.

Honest listWhere it stands

OpenTPW is a development preview. The goal is the original game, not a remake: behaviour is traced from the original, and every place where OpenTPW still guesses is written down in a public register.

Works

  • The CD launcher, the intro movies and the 3D lobby
  • All four worlds and all 274 original rides, shops, sideshows and features, with their original scripts and animations
  • Guests, staff, money, loans, research, objectives, golden tickets and keys
  • Player-built roller coasters
  • The advisor's speech and the park music
  • Six languages, any resolution, Windows, macOS, Linux and the browser
  • Online: accounts, sharing and visiting parks, voting, postcards, chat

Not yet

  • Ambient, ride and most interface sounds
  • Go-karts and water rides; coaster track graphics
  • Original save games load only in part (no guests or research yet)
  • Shared online parks show the world's starting layout, not the builder's changes
  • Some rules still use a documented approximation instead of the original's exact formula

Details per file format and feature: README, feature matrix, progress notes.

The online world is back

The original ThemeParkWorld.com service closed long ago. OpenTPW has its own open server behind the same globe, and you can run one yourself.

  • Publish a park in a city on the globe and visit other players' parks
  • Vote for parks; the most-voted ones stand as islands in the city's lobby
  • Send postcards and chat with the original's chat commands
  • Delete your account, and everything of yours on the server, at any time

    Questions

    Do I need the original game?

    Yes. OpenTPW is an engine, not the game: it contains none of the original's graphics, sounds or other files and reads them from your own copy of Theme Park World, for example the PC CD or an installed copy.

    Does the browser version upload my game files?

    No. The page copies the files it needs from the folder you choose into your browser's memory. They are never sent to a server, and they are gone when you close the page.

    Is OpenTPW made by EA or Bullfrog?

    No. OpenTPW is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts or Bullfrog Productions.

    How faithful is it?

    The aim is to behave like the original. Behaviour is traced from the original game's code and data, and every place where OpenTPW still has to guess is listed in a public register, so it can be checked and improved.

    How can I help?

    Play it and report what differs from the original, help with research into the game's files and code, or contribute code on GitHub.

    Where OpenTPW comes from

    Alex Guthrie started OpenTPW in March 2022 at OpenTPW/OpenTPW: the first file readers, a renderer and the documentation of the game's file formats, with contributions from others, such as Beneggy's work on the save format. Since October 2026 this fork at meneerkrabs/OpenTPW continues the work at a faster pace, and it would gladly work together with the original project.

    The research behind it, from file formats to the ride script machine and the economy, is written up in the documentation.
