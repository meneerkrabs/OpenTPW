OpenTPW

A new engine for Theme Park World, the 1999 Bullfrog game. You bring your own copy; OpenTPW runs it on Windows, macOS and Linux, or in a browser tab.

Play in the browser Download

Free and open source. Needs the files of an original installation or CD. Why?

Now playableWhat you can do today

Build a park and fill it

Pick one of the four worlds in the lobby, lay paths and queues and place the original rides, shops and sideshows. Guests come in through the gate, queue, ride, eat and go home again. Money, loans, wages, research and staff run on the game's own settings files.

Build roller coasters

Place pylons from the station, raise them with the mouse wheel and close the circuit. Track shape, cost, rating, the drawn track and train motion follow the original code. Go-kart tracks and water rides are built with their own track tool, and a ride's upgrade list adds their jumps, tunnels and the Space Racers teleport.

The advisor talks again

Original speech with lip sync for 236 of the 248 messages the original game gives: the welcome, money trouble and bankruptcy, rides and breakdowns, litter, guest mood, staff, research, golden tickets and the help of the windows and tools. Restored advice adds 42 lines the original never played. The park plays its own music and moves to a busier section as guests arrive.

The original interface, sharp

The lobby, HUD, buy window, finance, research, staff and map screens are built from the game's own models, fonts and strings, in English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Korean, Polish, Spanish and Swedish. Any resolution or aspect ratio works, and the interface scales without blurring.

Make it yoursExtensions and mods

OpenTPW can be extended without touching the engine. Official extensions are OpenTPW's own features that you can switch off; mods are folders you add yourself. Both are managed on the Extensions and mods screen in the options; an official extension switched there applies to the next park you start or load, a mod at the next start. How it works

Pools, on by default

  • Paint pool water of any shape on your park, and build water slides and pool attractions: rope swing, jets, spa, wave machine and a lazy river.
  • Guests change in a changing room and really swim, with their own hi-res (2x) sprites and swim animations.
  • Everything comes with its own textures, objects and art, made for OpenTPW; nothing is taken from the original game.
  • Not for you? Switch it off on the Extensions and mods screen. It is not available in Theme Park Inc runs, and parks without water save exactly as before. Details

Mods you can write

  • Data mods replace game files, string tables and objects. They are off until you switch them on. Details
  • Script mods change guest decisions in TPW Script, a small language of our own. It is not Lua, and mods never run native code, so the simulation stays deterministic and saves resume exactly. Details
  • Optional texture packs are built on your own computer from your own copy of the game; OpenTPW distributes none. Details
  • Parks remember the mods they were saved with and refuse to load without them.

Not yet: script mods run on the desktop but not in the browser, and community mods cannot be added in the browser. Start with the modding guide.

Honest listWhere it stands

OpenTPW is a development preview. The goal is the original game, not a remake: behaviour is traced from the original, and every place where OpenTPW still guesses is written down in a public register.

You can

  • Start from the original CD launcher, watch the intro movies and walk into the 3D lobby. Details
  • Pick any of the four worlds (Lost Kingdom, Wonder Land, Space Zone, Halloween World) and build a park there, or open the jungle sandbox with --sandbox. Details
  • Place any of the 274 original rides, shops, sideshows and features; they run their original scripts and animations. Details
  • Run a park with guests, staff, money, loans, research, objectives, golden tickets and keys, and save it and resume it later (the app and the browser); a park in play is also autosaved every five minutes. Details
  • Build your own roller coaster: pylons, closed circuits, original cost, rating and train motion. Details
  • Build go-kart and water ride tracks and add their jumps, tunnels and the Space Racers teleport from the ride's upgrade list. Details
  • Hear the advisor, the park music and nearly every ride, weather, guest, staff and interface sound, and play the game in nine languages at any resolution. Details
  • Build swimming pools with changing rooms, slides, spas, a lazy river and swimming guests: the pools extension is on by default, with its own models, textures and guest animations (switch it off under Options, OpenTPW, Extensions and mods). Details
  • Change the game with mods: data mods, sandboxed TPW Script mods for guest decisions (desktop), the official extensions and local texture packs you build from your own copy. Details
  • Go online: publish and visit parks and walk around them with the other visitors, vote, send postcards and chat, on the official server or your own. Details

You cannot yet

  • Load an original save game completely: its staff, research, ride scripts and most guests load, but guests in queues, on rides, held by guards, walking to the arrival vehicle or standing off the paths are dropped, a roller coaster loads with its track and trains but without its riders, and the guests' animation scripts, the ride scripts' saved object animation and a few other blocks are not restored. Details
  • Hear every sound exactly: the stereo pan of a positioned sound, the order in which equally loud voices keep their place and the rounding of a fade still follow documented approximations (the room reverb is OpenTPW's own by design, because the original's lives in the sound card). Details
  • Build a park together with other players: a shared park is a snapshot, and its visitors walk it together but cannot change it. Details
  • Rely on the original's exact numbers everywhere: some rules still use a documented approximation. Details

Later: Full Theme Park Inc / Sim Coaster support is a later goal. The experimental building-and-guests slice can already start with your own files in the browser or through the desktop world command. Campaign, economy, staff, advisor and native park saves remain unavailable. The desktop edition chooser (--setup) is part of the releases since v0.8.0. Details

The online world is back

The original ThemeParkWorld.com service closed long ago. OpenTPW has its own open server behind the same globe, and you can run one yourself.

  • Publish a park in a city on the globe, visit other players' parks and meet their other visitors there
  • Vote for parks; the most-voted ones stand as islands in the city's lobby
  • Send postcards and chat with the original's chat commands
  • Delete your account, and everything of yours on the server, at any time

    Questions

    Do I need the original game?

    Yes. OpenTPW is an engine, not the game: it contains none of the original's graphics, sounds or other files and reads them from your own copy of Theme Park World, for example the PC CD or an installed copy.

    Does the browser version upload my game files?

    No. The page copies the files it needs from the folder you choose into your browser's memory. They are never sent to a server, and they are gone when you close the page.

    Is OpenTPW made by EA or Bullfrog?

    No. OpenTPW is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts or Bullfrog Productions.

    How faithful is it?

    The aim is to behave like the original. Behaviour is traced from the original game's code and data, and every place where OpenTPW still has to guess is listed in a public register, so it can be checked and improved.

    How can I help?

    Play it and report what differs from the original, help with research into the game's files and code, or contribute code on GitHub.

    Where OpenTPW comes from

    Alex Guthrie started OpenTPW in March 2022 at OpenTPW/OpenTPW: the first file readers, a renderer and the documentation of the game's file formats, with contributions from others, such as Beneggy's work on the save format. Since October 2026 this fork at meneerkrabs/OpenTPW continues the work at a faster pace, and it would gladly work together with the original project.

    The research behind it, from file formats to the ride script machine and the economy, is written up in the documentation.
