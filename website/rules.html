Community rules

For the official server at play.opentpw.io. Last updated 10 October 2026.

Theme Park World is a game for all ages, and so is its online world. Everything other players can see, from your name and park names to postcards and chat, should be fine for a child to read.

Be kind

  • No bullying, harassment, threats or hate against anyone.
  • No sexual, violent or otherwise adult content in names, parks, postcards or chat.
  • Do not pretend to be someone else, including the OpenTPW team.

Keep it safe

  • Do not share personal details such as addresses, phone numbers or passwords, yours or anyone else's.
  • No spam, advertising or links to other sites.
  • Do not attack, overload or try to break into the server or other players' accounts.

Moderation

Use Report in the game, or write to contact@opentpw.io. Moderators can mute players in chat, hide parks and ban accounts that break these rules. You can ask about a decision at the same address.

Under 16? Please ask a parent or guardian before you create an account.
